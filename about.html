About BetSorted

South Africa's independent guide to licensed sports betting sites. No spin. No paid placements. Just honest comparisons built for local punters.

Why BetSorted Exists

If you've ever tried to compare SA bookmakers online, you already know the problem: most "review" sites are just thinly disguised ads. The bookmaker who pays the most commission gets the five-star rating. The actual punter — trying to figure out whether Hollywoodbets or Betway is better for PSL betting — gets useless copy.

BetSorted was built to fix that. We're not paid to rank any bookmaker higher. We don't accept sponsored placements or "featured listing" fees. Our rankings are based on real data: odds comparisons, verified payout speeds, FICA processes, and what South African bettors actually report about each site.

We focus exclusively on South Africa — PSL, rugby, cricket, horse racing, EFT payments, Ozow, ZAR accounts. None of the global-site noise that doesn't apply to you.

What We Cover

  • Head-to-head comparisons — Betway vs Hollywoodbets, Sportingbet vs WSB, and more
  • Odds comparison tools — live best-odds finder across SA bookmakers + Polymarket reference prices
  • Betting calculators — free tools for singles, accumulators, each-way bets, and returns (zero data charges)
  • Payment guides — EFT, Instant EFT, Ozow, Capitec Pay, FNB, and card deposit guides for SA bettors
  • Sport guides — PSL team betting, Springbok rugby, SA20 cricket, horse racing

How We Make Money

BetSorted is free to use. We earn affiliate commissions when you sign up to a bookmaker through our links — at no cost to you. This is how independent comparison sites operate.

Important: our affiliate relationships do not affect our rankings or review scores. We've given lower scores to bookmakers we have affiliate deals with when the data warrants it. Our editorial policy (which you can read in full here) separates commercial decisions from editorial decisions.

Our Commitment to Responsible Gambling

We only list bookmakers that are licensed by South African provincial gambling boards. We prominently display responsible gambling resources and the National Responsible Gambling Programme helpline (0800 006 008) across the site.
