What is Sports Arbitrage?

Arbitrage (or "arbing") is placing bets on all possible outcomes of an event across different bookmakers, where the combined odds guarantee a profit regardless of result. This happens when bookmakers disagree on the probability of each outcome.

Example: Betway offers 2.10 on Team A to win. Hollywoodbets offers 2.10 on Team B to win. The arb percentage is (1/2.10 + 1/2.10) = 95.2% — meaning you lock in a 4.8% profit on any stake.

How to Find Arbs in SA

Use the Best Odds Finder to spot discrepancies between SA bookmakers. The best opportunities appear around match day, just after team news, or on less liquid markets (lower league rugby, domestic cricket).
