How Best Odds Finder Works

We fetch odds from multiple bookmakers twice per day (6 AM & 6 PM) and show you the top 5 best available odds for each match market.

SA Bookmakers vs Market Reference

SA Bookmakers: Licensed operators you can actually use in South Africa appear as clickable rows when they are present in the odds feed.

Market Reference: International bookmakers like Betfair, Matchbook, and Smarkets are shown as a benchmark. These are not local recommendations, but they help you see whether a South African price is competitive.

ℹ️ Manual Check Needed: Hollywoodbets, Sportingbet, Supabets don't have public APIs. We're working on adding them. For now, use this tool to see if odds are competitive, then check those bookmakers manually.
