Betting Bonuses South Africa: What Actually Matters

Promos look big. The terms decide whether they’re worth anything.

Ignore the headline number. The real value of a bonus is the rollover. If a book gives R2,000 but asks for 10x turnover on low odds, it’s not a gift — it’s a trap.

Rollover rules to watch

  • Turnover multiple: 1x–3x is fair. 6x+ is tough.
  • Minimum odds: If you must bet at 1.7+ odds, it limits safe plays.
  • Expiry: Some bonuses vanish in 7 days.
Tip: If the bonus feels confusing, skip it. Odds value over a season beats a bad promo.
