Sports Betting by City: South African Local Guides

Published 12 November 2026 · Sources checked 12 November 2026

Betting is regulated province by province in South Africa, and every city has its own teams, venues and big days. These local guides cover what's worth betting on near you, who licenses bookmakers in your province, and where licensed betting actually happens, based on regulator data rather than marketing claims.

Cape Town

Stormers and the Cape Town Sevens at DHL Stadium, MI Cape Town and the SA20 final at Newlands, Cape Town City's promotion race, and summer racing at Kenilworth. Licensed by the Western Cape Gambling and Racing Board.

Johannesburg

Chiefs, Pirates and the Soweto derby, the Lions at Ellis Park, Joburg Super Kings at the Wanderers, and the Summer Cup at Turffontein. Licensed by the Gauteng Gambling Board.

How we choose cities

We add a city guide when South Africans are actually searching for betting information about it, and when we can fill the page with verifiable local detail: fixtures, venues, racecourses and licence records. We'd rather have fewer, useful pages than one for every town with the name swapped in.

Before you bet anywhere

  • Check the bookmaker's licence on the National Gambling Board register. Our Western Cape Gambling Board guide shows how.
  • Read whether online betting is legal in South Africa.
  • Compare bookmakers in our best betting sites guide and bookmaker reviews.
