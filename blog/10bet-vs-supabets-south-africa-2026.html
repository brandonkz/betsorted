10Bet vs Supabets South Africa 2026: Which Bookmaker Is Right for You?

Published March 2, 2026 • 10 min read • Brandon Katz

Two of the most talked-about bookmakers in South Africa right now — and they could not be more different. 10Bet brings international pedigree and sharp football odds, while Supabets has cemented itself in townships and suburbs alike with Lucky Numbers and instant withdrawals. The question is not which one is "better" in some abstract sense. It is which one fits the way you actually bet.

This comparison breaks down every angle that matters for SA punters: welcome bonuses, odds quality, payment methods, mobile apps, market depth, PSL coverage, and live betting. Welcome Bonuses: R3,000 vs R50 — But Read the Fine Print

On paper, 10Bet wins this one by a country mile. Their welcome bonus goes up to R3,000, which is among the biggest in the SA market. Supabets offers a R50 free bet — modest, but it is a genuine no-strings signup reward that you can use immediately.

Here is the thing though: a R3,000 bonus means nothing if the wagering requirements are steep. You will typically need to roll the bonus amount over several times at minimum odds before you can withdraw any winnings. That is standard industry practice, but many punters sign up, see the bonus in their account, and assume it is free money. It is not.

Supabets' R50 free bet is honest. You get it, you use it, you move on. No complicated wagering gymnastics. For punters who bet small amounts — say R20 to R100 per match — the R50 free bet is actually more useful in practice than a theoretical R3,000 you may never unlock.

Odds Quality: Where Your Money Actually Goes

This is where the comparison gets interesting. 10Bet consistently offers sharper odds on major international football — Premier League, Champions League, La Liga. If you are betting on Liverpool vs Arsenal, 10Bet will usually give you a few extra Rand on the same stake compared to Supabets.

On PSL matches, the gap narrows. Both bookmakers price Sundowns, Chiefs, and Pirates fixtures competitively. For smaller PSL fixtures — think Stellenbosch vs Richards Bay — 10Bet sometimes widens their margins, while Supabets keeps pricing relatively consistent across the board.

Where Supabets has a unique edge is Lucky Numbers. This is not traditional sports betting, but it is hugely popular in SA. If Lucky Numbers is part of your routine, Supabets is the clear choice because 10Bet does not offer it at all.

Payment Methods: Capitec vs Card

This might be the single biggest differentiator for many SA punters, and it comes down to how you bank.

Supabets supports Capitec Pay, Ozow, and EFT — the holy trinity for South African online payments. If you bank with Capitec (and statistically, there is a good chance you do), Supabets makes depositing effortless. Money hits your betting account almost instantly.

10Bet leans more on Visa/Mastercard and EFT. That is perfectly fine if you have a credit or debit card, but some punters prefer not to link cards to betting accounts. And if your primary account is Capitec without a Visa card, 10Bet becomes slightly less convenient.

Withdrawal Speed

Supabets is known for fast payouts — often same-day, sometimes within hours. This is a massive deal. Nobody wants to wait three days for their winnings after a successful PSL weekend. If fast payouts matter to you, read our fastest paying bookmakers ranking.

10Bet withdrawals are reliable but can take 1–3 business days depending on your payment method and FICA status. Not bad, but not instant either.

Mobile Apps: Polish vs Simplicity

10Bet's app feels more polished. It is clearly built by a team with international experience — smooth animations, intuitive navigation, and a betslip that handles live betting without lag. If you value a premium app experience, 10Bet has the edge.

Supabets takes a different approach: keep it simple, keep it light. The app works well on older phones and slower connections, which matters if you are betting on mobile data in an area with patchy coverage. It does not look as flashy, but it gets the job done without chewing through your data.

Market Coverage: International vs Local

10Bet shines on international sports. Beyond football, you get solid coverage of tennis, basketball, cricket, and even niche markets like table tennis and esports. If you bet on the NBA, ATP Tour, or IPL alongside your PSL bets, 10Bet gives you everything in one place.

Supabets is more locally focused. The PSL coverage is solid, horse racing is available, and Lucky Numbers fills a gap that no other traditional bookmaker covers. But if you want to bet on a random Serie A midweek fixture or an ATP Challenger match, the market depth may disappoint.

PSL Betting Specifically

For PSL, both bookmakers cover all fixtures with standard markets: match result, over/under goals, double chance, both teams to score. 10Bet tends to offer slightly more prop markets on marquee fixtures (first goalscorer, correct score, half-time/full-time), while Supabets sticks to the essentials.

If you are building same game multis for PSL matches, check which bookmaker offers the specific combinations you want — availability varies.

Live Betting: Speed Matters

10Bet is the stronger live betting platform. Markets update quickly, the interface stays responsive during busy periods, and you can place in-play bets without excessive delays. For PSL derbies where momentum shifts fast, this responsiveness matters.

Supabets live betting is functional but more basic. You get the key markets — match result, next goal, over/under — but the speed and variety do not match 10Bet. If live betting is a big part of your strategy, 10Bet is the better choice.

Physical Presence: The Supabets Advantage

One thing 10Bet simply cannot compete with is physical branches. Supabets has outlets across South Africa, particularly in Gauteng, KZN, and the Eastern Cape. You can walk in, place a bet, collect a payout, and chat to staff who know the local scene.

This might sound old-fashioned, but for many SA punters, the ability to deposit and withdraw cash at a physical location is a dealbreaker. If you do not have a bank account or prefer cash transactions, Supabets is the only choice between these two.

Who Should Choose 10Bet?

You should lean towards 10Bet if you:

  • Bet heavily on international football (EPL, Champions League, La Liga)
  • Value a polished mobile app with fast live betting
  • Have a Visa or Mastercard for deposits
  • Want a bigger welcome bonus (and are willing to meet wagering requirements)
  • Prefer deeper market coverage across multiple sports

Who Should Choose Supabets?

Supabets is the better pick if you:

  • Bank with Capitec and want seamless deposits
  • Play Lucky Numbers regularly
  • Want instant or same-day withdrawals
  • Prefer a physical branch for cash transactions
  • Bet mostly on PSL and local markets
  • Want a low-barrier entry with a simple R50 free bet

FAQ

Is 10Bet or Supabets better for PSL betting?

Both cover all PSL fixtures. 10Bet offers slightly more prop markets and better odds on big matches, while Supabets is better for quick, convenient bets with fast payouts.

Can I use Capitec Pay on 10Bet?

Not directly. 10Bet supports Visa/Mastercard and EFT. If Capitec Pay is your preferred method, Supabets is the better option. See our Capitec Pay guide for details.

Which bookmaker pays out faster?

Supabets is generally faster, often processing withdrawals within hours. 10Bet typically takes 1–3 business days.

Do both bookmakers offer live betting?

Yes, but 10Bet's live betting platform is faster and offers more in-play markets. Supabets covers the basics reliably.

Should I have accounts with both?

Yes. Most experienced punters use multiple accounts to shop for the best odds. Use 10Bet for international sports and live betting, Supabets for Lucky Numbers and fast local payouts.
