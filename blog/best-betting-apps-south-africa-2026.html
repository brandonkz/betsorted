1. Betway — Best for Live Betting

Betway is the app you reach for when the whistle blows and you want to bet in-play. The betslip loads fast, odds refresh without lag, and the interface stays clean even when you are flipping between three PSL matches on a Saturday afternoon. It is the smoothest live betting experience in SA right now.

The app handles 4G connections well, which matters when you are betting from Loftus or the FNB Stadium and the signal is patchy. Deposits via Instant EFT or Ozow land quickly, and FICA verification is straightforward if you get it done early.

Pros: Fastest in-play engine, polished app design, competitive PSL odds on big fixtures.
Cons: Smaller market depth on lower-tier PSL matches. Pre-match props are limited compared to Hollywoodbets.

2. Hollywoodbets — Best for PSL Market Depth

If you want every possible market on a Chiefs vs Pirates derby — first goalscorer, correct score, half-time/full-time, corners, cards, player props — Hollywoodbets is where you go. They understand PSL betting better than any other bookmaker in South Africa, and the depth shows.

The app also has Lucky Numbers, which is popular with casual punters who want a quick flutter alongside their sports bets. Withdrawals are consistently fast, often landing within hours via EFT. The interface is not as slick as Betway, but it is functional and reliable.

Pros: Deepest PSL markets, fastest withdrawals, Lucky Numbers, strong local brand.
Cons: App design feels a generation behind Betway. Live betting is competent but not the standout.

3. Sportingbet — Best All-Rounder

Sportingbet does not dominate any single category, but it rarely lets you down. The app is stable, odds are fair, PSL coverage is solid, and payments work without surprises. If you want one app that handles everything from rugby to soccer to cricket without drama, Sportingbet is a safe choice.

The international sports coverage is broader than most SA-focused bookmakers, which makes it useful if you bet on Premier League, Champions League, or Springbok tests alongside your PSL picks. Banking options include EFT, Capitec Pay, and card deposits.

Pros: Reliable across all sports, decent odds, stable app, good international coverage.
Cons: Does not stand out in any one area. PSL depth is good but not Hollywoodbets-level.

4. Supabets — Best for Accumulators

Supabets has carved out a niche with accumulator bettors. The app makes it easy to build multi-leg bets, and they run regular acca promotions that boost your potential payout. If your betting style is "pick five PSL results and hope for the best," Supabets is built for that.

The trade-off is speed. The app can feel sluggish during peak times, and odds are not always the sharpest on individual markets. But for accumulators, the combined value often works out because of the promos. Use the accumulator calculator to check your combined odds before you commit.

Pros: Acca-friendly interface, regular promotions, decent PSL coverage.
Cons: App speed lags behind Betway and Hollywoodbets. Withdrawals can take longer.

5. Easybet — Best for Beginners

If you are new to sports betting and find other apps overwhelming, Easybet strips things back to the basics. The interface is clean, the bet placement flow is straightforward, and you will not get lost in menus trying to find a PSL fixture. It is the "just let me bet" app.

The downside is that experienced punters will outgrow it quickly. Market depth is limited, and odds are not always competitive on fixtures where bigger bookmakers sharpen their prices. But as a starting point, it works well.

Pros: Simple interface, beginner-friendly, quick to learn.
Cons: Limited markets, odds not always competitive, less useful for experienced punters.

6. World Sports Betting — Best for Horse Racing

WSB has been around longer than most of these apps and has a loyal following among horse racing punters. If the Durban July or Vodacom Gold Cup is your thing, WSB gives you the deepest racing markets and form guides that other bookmakers do not match.

For PSL and mainstream sports betting, WSB is fine but not exceptional. The app feels dated compared to newer competitors, and the mobile experience does not match Betway or Hollywoodbets for speed. It is a specialist tool rather than an all-rounder.

Pros: Best horse racing coverage in SA, established brand, reliable payouts.
Cons: Dated app design, PSL markets are basic, mobile experience needs work.

7. Playabets — Best for Small Stakes

Playabets caters to punters who want to bet with smaller amounts. Minimum stakes are low, and the interface does not pressure you into bigger bets. If you are on a tight bankroll and want to enjoy PSL weekends without risking much, it is a good fit.

The trade-off is everything else. The app is functional but basic, market depth is limited, and odds tend to be slightly worse than the top-tier bookmakers. Use it as a secondary app alongside a stronger option.

Pros: Low minimum stakes, accessible for casual bettors, no pressure to bet big.
Cons: Basic app, limited markets, slightly worse odds.

8. 10bet — Best for International Sports

10bet brings a wide international sportsbook to SA bettors. If you follow leagues beyond the PSL — La Liga, Serie A, NBA, UFC — 10bet often has more markets and better odds on international events than locally-focused bookmakers.

For PSL-specific betting, 10bet is decent but not a standout. The app is clean and fast, but it does not have the local flavour of Hollywoodbets or the live betting edge of Betway. Think of it as a complementary app for your international bets.

Pros: Broad international coverage, competitive international odds, clean app.
Cons: PSL depth is average, less known in SA market, not the best for local sport specialists.

The Multi-App Strategy

Most serious South African punters use two or three apps. The logic is simple: different bookmakers have different strengths, and odds vary enough between them that shopping around genuinely improves your returns over a season.

A solid combination for 2026:

Betway for live and in-play betting.
Hollywoodbets for PSL market depth and quick withdrawals.
Sportingbet or 10bet for international fixtures.

FAQ

What is the best betting app in South Africa in 2026?

It depends on what you value most. Betway leads for live betting speed, Hollywoodbets for PSL market depth and Lucky Numbers, and Sportingbet for all-round reliability. Most serious punters use two or three apps.

Are South African betting apps safe to use?

All bookmakers listed here are licensed under South African gambling regulations, which means they must follow FICA rules, keep funds segregated, and pay out in ZAR. Stick to licensed operators to protect your money.

Which betting app pays out the fastest in South Africa?

Hollywoodbets and Betway consistently rank among the fastest for withdrawals, especially when using Instant EFT or Ozow. Most payouts land within hours after FICA verification is complete.

Can I use multiple betting apps at once?

Yes, and most experienced bettors recommend it. Having accounts with two or three bookmakers lets you shop for the best odds on each fixture, which improves your returns over a full season.

Do I need to complete FICA on every betting app?

Yes. Every licensed South African bookmaker requires FICA verification before you can withdraw funds. Complete it early on each app to avoid delays when you want to cash out.
