Best Betway Alternatives for South African Bettors (June 2026)

Betway is one of South Africa's most popular betting sites, but it's not perfect. Whether you're frustrated with verification delays, looking for better odds on World Cup 2026 matches, or just want to explore what else is out there, there are several excellent Betway alternatives worth considering.

This guide breaks down the best Betway competitors in South Africa right now, what makes them stand out, and which one might be the right fit for you based on what you actually bet on.

Why Look for Betway Alternatives?

Don't get us wrong — Betway is a solid, licensed bookmaker. But here's why South African punters are exploring other options in 2026:

  • FICA verification takes forever: Some bettors report waiting days for account approval, which is frustrating when you want to bet on a World Cup match happening tomorrow.
  • Withdrawal speeds vary: While Betway has improved, competitors like 10bet and SupaBets often process payouts faster.
  • Odds aren't always the best: For PSL, rugby, and cricket markets, you can sometimes find better value elsewhere.
  • Market variety: Some bookmakers offer more niche markets (like URC handicaps or Springboks try-scorer bets) that Betway doesn't cover as well.
  • Bonuses and promos: Other sites run more aggressive welcome offers and ongoing promotions.

The good news? South Africa has a competitive betting market with plenty of licensed, legitimate alternatives. Let's look at the best ones.

What to Look for in a Betway Alternative

Before switching bookmakers, here's what actually matters:

1. Licensing (Non-Negotiable)

Only bet with bookmakers licensed by the Western Cape Gambling and Racing Board or other SA authorities. All the sites mentioned above are fully licensed. Avoid offshore sites that aren't legal in South Africa — if you win big, proving the source of funds to SARS becomes a nightmare.

2. Withdrawal Speed

This is where Betway alternatives often shine. If you hate waiting 3-5 days for payouts, 10bet and SupaBets are significantly faster. WSB offers instant cash withdrawals at branches.

3. Sports Coverage

Match the bookmaker to what you actually bet on:

  • PSL: Hollywoodbets or SupaBets
  • Rugby: WSB, 10bet, or SupaBets
  • Cricket: 10bet or Sportingbet
  • Horse racing: Hollywoodbets (no contest)
  • World Cup 2026: 10bet or Sportingbet

4. Mobile Experience

If you bet on your phone (and let's be honest, most of us do), SupaBets has the best app. 10bet and Sportingbet are close behind. Betway's app is decent but nothing special.

5. Deposit Methods

All major SA bookmakers accept:

  • Bank transfers (FNB, Capitec, Standard Bank, etc.)
  • Instant EFT (Ozow, SnapScan, Capitec Pay)
  • Debit/credit cards (Visa, Mastercard)

WSB also accepts cash deposits at branches, which is unique.

Can You Use Multiple Bookmakers?

Absolutely, and you should.

Smart punters in South Africa use 2-3 bookmakers to:

  • Shop for the best odds: Betway might offer 2.10 on Bafana to win, while 10bet has 2.25 for the same bet. That's free money.
  • Access different bonuses: Take a welcome offer from 10bet, then another from SupaBets.
  • Avoid limits: If one bookmaker limits your stakes after you win consistently, you have backup options.
  • Match specialties to sports: Use Hollywoodbets for PSL, 10bet for World Cup 2026, WSB for rugby.

There's no rule against having multiple accounts. Just make sure each one is FICA-verified properly to avoid withdrawal issues later.

Common Questions About Betway Alternatives

Is it legal to use bookmakers other than Betway in South Africa?

Yes, as long as the bookmaker is licensed in South Africa. All the sites in this guide (10bet, Hollywoodbets, SupaBets, WSB, Sportingbet) are fully licensed and legal for SA residents.

Which bookmaker pays out fastest?

10bet consistently has the fastest online payouts (same day). WSB offers instant cash withdrawals at physical branches.

Can I use the same bank account for multiple bookmakers?

Yes. You can deposit and withdraw from 10bet, SupaBets, Betway, etc., all using the same bank account. Just make sure the account name matches your FICA documents.

Do I need to verify FICA on every site?

Yes. Each bookmaker has its own verification process. It's annoying, but it's legally required. The good news: once you're verified, you won't need to do it again on that site.

Which alternative has the best odds on PSL matches?

Hollywoodbets and SupaBets consistently offer the best PSL odds, especially on Kaizer Chiefs, Orlando Pirates, and Mamelodi Sundowns matches.
