What Is Lucky Numbers?

If you're new to this, Lucky Numbers is not the same as buying a lottery ticket. Instead, you're placing a fixed-odds bet on the outcome of official lottery draws — both South African draws (SA Lotto, Powerball, Daily Lotto) and international ones (UK 49s, Ghana Lucky Tuesday, Irish Lotto, and dozens more).

Here's the key difference: when you buy a Lotto ticket, your payout depends on the prize pool and how many other winners there are. With Lucky Numbers, the bookmaker offers fixed odds before the draw. You know exactly what you'll win if your numbers come in.

How Lucky Numbers Works

  1. Choose a draw — SA Lotto, UK 49s Lunchtime, Ghana Lucky Tuesday, etc.
  2. Pick your numbers — You can bet on 1 number (a "single"), 2 numbers, 3 numbers, or more.
  3. Set your stake — From as little as R1 at most bookmakers.
  4. Wait for the draw — Results come from the official lottery operator. No manipulation possible.
  5. Get paid — If your numbers match, you win at the advertised odds.

The beauty of Lucky Numbers is flexibility. You don't have to pick all 6 numbers. Betting on a single number to appear anywhere in the draw is a perfectly valid (and popular) strategy with decent odds.

Most Popular Lucky Numbers Draws in South Africa

Not all draws are created equal. Here are the ones that SA punters love most:

UK 49s (Lunchtime & Teatime)

By far the most popular Lucky Numbers draw in South Africa. The UK 49s runs twice daily — Lunchtime (around 1:49 PM SA time) and Teatime (around 7:49 PM SA time). The draw uses 6 numbers plus a bonus ball from a pool of 1–49. Two chances per day to win makes it incredibly popular.

SA Lotto & SA Powerball

The local favourites. SA Lotto draws on Wednesday and Saturday, while Powerball draws on Tuesday and Friday. Many punters bet on these through Lucky Numbers rather than buying tickets, because the fixed-odds payouts on smaller matches (1–3 numbers) are often better than the lottery's own prize tiers.

Daily Lotto

South Africa's daily draw — 5 numbers from 1–36, every single day. Quick, simple, and a great option for punters who want action daily.

Ghana Lucky Tuesday & Other African Draws

These African lottery draws have gained a cult following among SA punters. They offer different number pools and draw times, adding variety to your Lucky Numbers schedule.

Irish Lotto

Another favourite — draws on Wednesday and Saturday. The 1–47 number pool and bonus ball make it a slightly different game to UK 49s.

Lucky Numbers Strategies: How to Play Smarter

Let's be real: Lucky Numbers is a game of chance. No strategy guarantees wins. But there are ways to play smarter and get more value from your bets. Use our betting calculator to work out potential returns before placing bets.

Singles vs Combos: Understanding Your Options

Single number bets are the simplest form of Lucky Numbers betting. You pick one number and bet that it will appear anywhere in the draw. Typical payout: around 5/1 (depending on the draw and bookmaker). These hit more often but pay less.

Combo bets (multiples) involve picking 2, 3, 4, or more numbers. All your chosen numbers must appear in the draw for you to win. The more numbers you add, the higher the payout — but the lower the probability of winning.

Bet Type Typical Payout Win Probability Risk Level
1 Number (Single) ~5/1 Higher Low
2 Numbers ~30/1 Medium Medium
3 Numbers ~200/1 Lower Higher
4 Numbers ~1,500/1 Very Low Very High
5+ Numbers ~10,000/1+ Extremely Low Extremely High

Our recommendation: Mix your approach. Use singles for consistent, smaller wins, and throw in the occasional 2- or 3-number combo for bigger payouts. Avoid going heavy on 4+ number combos — the maths is heavily stacked against you.

Understanding Payouts

Payouts vary between bookmakers and between draws. The same single-number bet on UK 49s might pay 5.5/1 at one bookmaker and 5/1 at another. Over time, that difference adds up.

Always compare payouts before placing a bet, especially on popular draws like UK 49s. Having accounts at two or three bookmakers lets you shop for the best price — just like you would with sports betting odds.

Bankroll Management for Lucky Numbers

This is where most punters go wrong. Lucky Numbers draws happen multiple times a day, making it easy to chase losses or bet more than you planned. Read our full bankroll management guide for detailed strategies, but here are the essentials:

  • Set a daily budget — Decide before you start how much you're willing to spend per day on Lucky Numbers. R20? R50? R100? Stick to it.
  • Don't chase losses — If your numbers don't come up in the lunchtime draw, don't double your stake for the teatime draw. That's a fast track to an empty wallet.
  • Track your bets — Keep a simple record of what you bet and what you won/lost. It's sobering — and it helps you spot patterns in your own behaviour.
  • Use the UK 49s wisely — Two draws per day means twice the temptation. Consider betting on only one of the two draws to manage your bankroll.
  • Avoid "systems" — No number tracking system, hot/cold number analysis, or pattern prediction actually works. Each draw is independent and random. Save your money on the bet itself, not on some scheme.

Choosing Your Draws Wisely

With 30+ draws available, it's tempting to scatter bets across everything. Don't. Focus on 2–3 draws that you know well. Learn the number pools, the draw times, and compare payouts specifically for those draws. Specialisation beats spray-and-pray.

Frequently Asked Questions

What is Lucky Numbers betting in South Africa?

Lucky Numbers is a form of fixed-odds betting where you wager on the outcome of official lottery draws — both South African (SA Lotto, Powerball) and international (UK 49s, Ghana Lucky Tuesday, etc.). Unlike buying a lottery ticket, you bet through a licensed bookmaker and get paid out at fixed odds based on how many numbers you match.

Which bookmaker has the most Lucky Numbers draws?

Hollywoodbets offers the widest selection of Lucky Numbers draws in South Africa, covering 30+ international and local lotteries including SA Lotto, UK 49s, Ghana Lucky Tuesday, Irish Lotto, New York Lunchtime, and many more. No other SA bookmaker matches this range.

What is the minimum bet for Lucky Numbers?

Minimum bets vary by bookmaker. Hollywoodbets allows bets from as little as R1, making it accessible for casual punters. Betway, Supabets, Easybet, and Playabets also offer low minimums starting around R1. Sportingbet and World Sports Betting typically start at R2.

Are Lucky Numbers payouts better than buying a lottery ticket?

It depends on the bet type. For single number bets, Lucky Numbers payouts can be more favourable than traditional lottery prizes for matching a few numbers. However, matching all 6 numbers on a lottery ticket offers jackpots that far exceed fixed-odds payouts. Lucky Numbers gives you more control over bet size and more frequent draws throughout the day.
