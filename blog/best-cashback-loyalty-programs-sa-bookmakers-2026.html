Best Cashback & Loyalty Programs at SA Bookmakers (2026)

Published: 21 March 2026 · Reading time: 11 min · By BetSorted

Welcome bonuses get all the attention — and fair enough, a R500 free bet is hard to ignore — but the loyalty and cashback programmes are where you actually extract long-term value from a bookmaker. A good rewards programme can effectively reduce your cost per bet over time, which matters whether you're putting R50 a week on the PSL or a few hundred rand on a big weekend of EPL and URC action.

South African bookmakers have improved their loyalty offerings significantly in recent years. Some are genuinely good. Some are confusing enough to be practically worthless. This guide breaks down every major programme honestly, with real rand amounts and the wagering requirements that determine whether the offer is actually valuable or just marketing fluff.

Betway Plus — The Most Structured Programme

Betway's loyalty programme, Betway Plus, works on a points system that rewards you for every qualifying bet placed. Points accumulate in your account and can be redeemed for free bets or bonus funds. It's the most transparent and structured of all SA bookmaker loyalty programmes.

How Betway Plus Works

You earn points for every rand wagered on qualifying bets. The rate varies by market type — sports bets earn more points than virtual sports, for example. Once you've accumulated enough points, you can exchange them for bonus credits in the Betway Plus shop.

Betway Plus Key Details

  • Minimum deposit: R25
  • Points earning: Typically 1 point per R10 staked on qualifying bets
  • Redemption: Exchange points for free bets (starting from around 100 points = R10 free bet)
  • Wagering requirement: 3x on redeemed free bets, minimum odds of 1.50 per selection
  • Point expiry: 90 days of inactivity
  • Real monthly value: A bettor staking R500/month can reasonably expect R30–R60 in free bet value, depending on bet types

The Betway Plus programme rewards consistent bettors who place multiple bets per week. If you're a weekend-only bettor putting R100 on PSL matches twice a month, you'll accumulate points slowly. If you're regularly betting on EPL, Champions League, URC, and cricket, the points add up noticeably.

💡 Betway Plus tip: Use accumulated free bets on accumulators rather than singles. The 3x wagering requirement on free bets means you need to turn them over before cashing out — accumulators let you meet that requirement faster with fewer bets. Hollywoodbets Top Up Bonus — Best for Casual Bettors

Hollywoodbets runs what's effectively a weekly cashback programme through their Top Up Bonus. The mechanics are straightforward: you get a percentage of your net losses back as bonus credits, up to a weekly maximum. This makes it particularly valuable for punters who have a losing week — you're getting some money back to try again.

How the Top Up Bonus Works

Hollywoodbets Top Up Details

  • Minimum deposit: R25
  • Cashback rate: Percentage of net weekly losses (typically 10–20% depending on activity tier)
  • Weekly cap: Usually R100–R200 maximum cashback per week
  • Wagering requirement: 3x, minimum odds 1.50 per selection
  • Credit timing: Top Up credits loaded by Monday morning
  • Best bet type: Works on football, rugby, cricket, MMA, and most major sports

For a casual bettor who puts R200–R300 into betting on weekends, Hollywoodbets' Top Up is genuinely useful. On a bad weekend where the PSL predictions went sideways and the Boks lost, you're not completely empty-handed come Monday morning. The 3x wagering requirement is reasonable — you're not expected to bet your cashback 10 times before withdrawing.

Hollywoodbets also runs event-specific promotions throughout the year — enhanced odds on Soweto Derbies, PSL finals, and big international fixtures. Worth following their promotions page around key fixtures in the SA sports calendar.

Sportingbet Cashback — Best for Losing Streaks

Sportingbet takes a different approach with their cashback programme. Rather than a points system, they offer periodic cashback promotions tied to specific events or time periods. The value is inconsistent week-to-week, but during active promotions, Sportingbet cashback is among the most generous in the market.

Sportingbet Cashback Details

  • Minimum deposit: R50
  • Cashback type: Event-specific and weekly loss rebates
  • Rate: Typically 10–15% of net losses during promo periods
  • Wagering requirement: Varies — some promotions offer 1x (cash) rather than bonus funds
  • Maximum cashback: Varies by promotion, typically R50–R200
  • Real value: Excellent during major tournaments (PSL, UCL, Rugby World Cup)
💡 Sportingbet tip: Check Sportingbet's promotions page before major SA sporting events. Their tournament-specific cashback offers during PSL finals and Springbok test series are frequently better value than their standard ongoing programme.

Supabets Reload Bonuses

Supabets has a loyal following among SA punters who bet heavily on PSL and European football. Their reload bonuses are designed to reward depositing members with bonus funds on top of deposits, effectively giving you more money to bet with every time you top up your account.

Supabets Reload Bonus Details

  • Minimum deposit: R25
  • Reload bonus: Up to 25% extra on qualifying deposits (varies by promotion)
  • Minimum deposit for bonus: Typically R50
  • Wagering requirement: 5x — higher than some competitors
  • Best for: Mid-week PSL and European football bettors who deposit regularly

The 5x wagering requirement is the catch with Supabets. If you receive R50 in bonus funds, you need to place R250 in qualifying bets before you can withdraw. For regular bettors this isn't an issue, but casual punters should be aware of the rollover before accepting the bonus.

World Sports Betting (WSB) Rewards

WSB is one of the older and more established SA bookmakers, and their rewards programme reflects a more traditional approach. WSB Rewards credits are straightforward — you earn rewards based on your betting activity and can redeem them for free bets.

WSB Rewards Details

  • Minimum deposit: R50
  • Rewards type: Activity-based points redeemable for free bets
  • Wagering requirement: 3x on free bets
  • Minimum odds: 1.50 per selection on qualifying bets
  • Best for: Bettors who value simplicity over complexity

Easybet, Sunbet, and Playabets

Easybet

Easybet has been growing its market share in SA with competitive odds and a user-friendly platform. Their cashback offers are targeted at lower-stakes bettors — a R200 minimum monthly stake typically qualifies for cashback, with 2x wagering on returned funds. Good for casual bettors who aren't chasing massive bonus value but want some protection on losing weeks.

Sunbet

Sunbet is operated by Sun International, which means their loyalty programme ties into the broader Sun MVG rewards ecosystem. For punters who also visit Sun City or GrandWest casinos, the combined loyalty value can be significant. Pure online bettors won't benefit as much, but Sunbet's regular weekly promotions are solid. Minimum deposit R25.

Playabets

Playabets has positioned itself as a mobile-first bookmaker with a strong focus on local SA markets. Their cashback and free bet promotions tend to be smaller in absolute value but are often easier to clear due to lower wagering requirements. Good entry point for punters new to online betting. Minimum deposit R25.

Which Programme Is Best For You?

🥇 Casual Weekend Bettor (R100–R300/month)

Go with Hollywoodbets Top Up. The weekly cashback structure means you're rewarded even on losing weeks, the R25 minimum deposit is accessible, and the 3x wagering requirement is reasonable. Perfect if you're betting on weekend PSL fixtures and the odd Boks test.

🥈 Regular Multi-Sport Bettor (R500–R2000/month)

Betway Plus is the move. The structured points system rewards consistent betting across multiple sports, and the free bet accumulation genuinely adds up over time. At R1000/month staked, you should be redeeming R60–R100 in free bets monthly. Combine with Sportingbet for their event cashback during big tournaments.

🥉 Heavy Accumulator Fan

Betway Plus + Supabets reload bonuses. Betway's points system rewards accumulator bettors well, and Supabets' reload bonuses give you extra stake to work with on mid-week accas. Just be disciplined about the 5x wagering on Supabets bonus funds.

🏟️ Live Betting Fans

For punters who love live in-play betting — whether that's the Soweto Derby, URC fixtures, or UFC fight nights — Betway's loyalty programme is the clear winner. Their live betting market is the deepest, and qualifying in-play bets contribute to Betway Plus points at the same rate as pre-match bets.

Understanding the Real Value of Bonus Offers

Here's the honest calculation that most bookmakers don't want you to do. A R100 bonus with a 5x wagering requirement at minimum odds of 2.00 requires R500 in qualifying bets. If you're an average bettor hitting about 45% of single bets at average odds of 2.00, you'll lose roughly R250 clearing that wagering requirement. So the R100 bonus actually cost you R150 in expected losses.

This doesn't mean all bonuses are traps — it means you need to read the conditions carefully.

⚠️ Bonus red flags to watch for:
  • Wagering requirements above 5x
  • Minimum odds requirements above 2.00
  • Bonuses that can only be used on specific markets (e.g., virtuals only)
  • Short expiry periods (less than 7 days to use a free bet)
  • Maximum win caps on free bets (e.g., "winnings capped at R200")

The best bonuses have wagering requirements of 1x–3x, minimum odds of 1.50 or lower, and no caps on winnings. Sportingbet's event-specific cashback, when structured as real cash (1x wagering), is the closest to genuinely "free money" you'll find in the SA market.

Frequently Asked Questions

Which SA bookmaker has the best loyalty programme?

For regular bettors who place multiple bets per week, Betway Plus offers the most structured long-term rewards through their points system. For casual punters who bet mainly on weekends, Hollywoodbets' Top Up bonus provides consistent weekly value without needing to track a points balance. Supabets' reload bonuses are worth looking at for punters who bet mid-week on PSL and European football.

What are wagering requirements on SA bookmaker bonuses?

Wagering requirements (also called rollover or playthrough) specify how many times you must bet your bonus amount before it can be withdrawn as cash. A R100 bonus with a 3x wagering requirement means you must place R300 in total bets before withdrawing. Most SA bookmakers apply wagering requirements of 3x–8x on free bets and cashback bonuses. Always read the terms before accepting a bonus.

Do SA bookmaker loyalty points expire?

Most SA bookmaker reward points have an expiry period of 30–90 days of inactivity. Betway Plus points typically expire after 90 days without qualifying activity. Hollywoodbets rewards are credited weekly and must be used within the promotional period. Always check the specific terms for each programme, as expiry policies can change.

Can I use cashback bonuses on any sport?

It depends on the bookmaker and the specific promotion. Most cashback offers have minimum odds requirements (typically 1.50–2.00 per selection) and may exclude certain bet types like system bets or cashed-out wagers. Some bookmakers restrict cashback to specific sports or tournaments. Betway and Hollywoodbets cashback offers generally apply across all sports including football, rugby, cricket, and MMA.
