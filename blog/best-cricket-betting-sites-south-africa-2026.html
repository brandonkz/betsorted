Popular Cricket Betting Markets Explained

Match Winner

The simplest bet: which team will win the match? Available for all formats (Test, ODI, T20).

Top Batsman

Bet on which player will score the most runs for their team. Popular for IPL and Proteas matches.

Top Bowler

Bet on which player will take the most wickets in the match. Great for Test cricket.

Total Runs

Over/under betting on total runs scored in an innings or match. Example: Over 280.5 runs in an ODI.

Man of the Match

Bet on which player will be named Man of the Match. Typically a top batsman or bowler.

Runs in First 6 Overs

Over/under on runs scored in the powerplay. Popular for T20 and IPL betting.

Total Boundaries

Bet on the total number of 4s and 6s in a match. High-scoring T20s often hit 25+ boundaries.

Cricket Betting Strategies

1. Line Shop for Best Odds

Don't stick to one bookmaker. Compare odds across Betway, Hollywoodbets, and SupaBets to find the best value. Even a 0.05 difference in odds matters over time.

2. Focus on One Format

Cricket has three formats (Test, ODI, T20). Focus on one and learn its patterns. T20 is faster and more unpredictable, while Tests reward patience and analysis.

3. Use Live Betting Wisely

Live betting is powerful but risky. Watch the match, track momentum shifts, and bet in-play when odds swing. Example: back the chasing team if they're 50/0 in the powerplay.

4. Bet on Player Props

Top batsman and top bowler bets offer better value than match winner. Research player form, venue stats, and head-to-head records.

5. Bankroll Management

Never bet more than 5% of your bankroll on a single bet. Cricket is unpredictable — even strong favorites lose. Use our betting calculator to manage stakes.
