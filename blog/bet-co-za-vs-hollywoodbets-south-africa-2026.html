Bet.co.za vs Hollywoodbets South Africa 2026: The OG vs The People's Champ

Published: 14 March 2026 · 10 min read · Brandon K

If you've ever placed a bet in South Africa, you've almost certainly come across these two names. Bet.co.za and Hollywoodbets are as much a part of the SA betting landscape as a Saturday afternoon braai is to our culture. But they come from very different backgrounds, and in 2026, they offer quite different experiences depending on what kind of punter you are.

Bet.co.za is one of the original gangsters of South African online betting. Established back in 2002 — when most of us were still figuring out dial-up internet — they've been a trusted name for over two decades. They were doing online betting before it was cool, and that kind of track record means something in an industry where trust is everything.

Hollywoodbets, on the other hand, started as a humble horse racing shop in KwaZulu-Natal. Fast forward to 2026, and they've become arguably the biggest name in SA betting. They sponsor football clubs in England (Brentford FC), they're plastered across PSL stadiums, and their distinctive purple branding is recognizable from Durban to Diepsloot. They're the people's champ — the bookmaker that your uncle, your colleague, and your barber all use.

So which one deserves your deposit? That's what we're here to break down. We'll compare everything from odds and Lucky Numbers to mobile apps and withdrawal speeds. Let's get into it.

Odds Comparison: Who Gives Better Value?

At the end of the day, odds are what separate the winners from the losers. You can have the flashiest app in the world, but if the odds are kak, what's the point? Let's look at how Bet.co.za and Hollywoodbets stack up across the sports that matter most to South African punters.

PSL Odds

For Premier Soccer League matches, both bookmakers are competitive. Neither consistently undercuts the other — you'll find Bet.co.za offering slightly better odds on some Kaizer Chiefs matches, and Hollywoodbets returning the favour on Orlando Pirates fixtures. The margins are typically thin, which is good news for punters.

That said, Hollywoodbets tends to have more market depth on PSL matches. You'll find more prop bets, player specials, and in-play markets on Hollywood compared to Bet.co.za. If you're the kind of punter who likes betting on first goalscorer, correct score, or corners, Hollywood usually gives you more to play with.

International Football

For the Premier League, Champions League, and major international tournaments, the odds are very similar across both platforms. Bet.co.za occasionally edges it on outright winners for European leagues, while Hollywoodbets tends to be stronger on Asian handicap markets. Use our betting calculator to see exactly how small differences in odds affect your returns — even a 0.05 difference adds up over hundreds of bets.

Rugby

South Africa loves rugby, and both bookmakers know it. For Springbok tests, URC matches, and the Currie Cup, Bet.co.za and Hollywoodbets offer comparable odds. Bet.co.za has been around long enough to have a solid rugby trader team, and it shows. Hollywoodbets matches them closely but their rugby offering feels slightly less deep in terms of prop markets.

Verdict on odds: It's essentially a tie, with Hollywoodbets winning on market variety and Bet.co.za holding its own on core match odds. If you're serious about getting the best price, you should have accounts with both — and check our odds comparison tool before placing big bets.

Lucky Numbers: Hollywoodbets Owns This Category

Lucky Numbers is massive in South Africa. For those unfamiliar, it's a form of betting where you predict numbers drawn in international and local lottery draws. It's not technically the lottery — you're betting on the outcome of lottery draws — but the experience feels similar, and the payouts can be life-changing.

Both Bet.co.za and Hollywoodbets offer Lucky Numbers, but this is one area where Hollywoodbets is the undisputed champion. Here's why:

  • More draws: Hollywoodbets covers lotteries from all over the world — UK 49s, New York Lunch, Greece Powerball, Russia Gosloto, and dozens more. Bet.co.za offers a solid selection but can't match Hollywood's breadth.
  • More bet types: Hollywood lets you bet on 1, 2, 3, or 4 numbers, with bonus ball options and various combination bets. Their Lucky Numbers interface is more polished and easier to navigate.
  • Better odds: Hollywood's Lucky Numbers payouts tend to be slightly more generous than Bet.co.za's, especially on singles and doubles.
  • Draw frequency: With Hollywoodbets, there's almost always a draw happening or about to happen. Their coverage of international draws means you can bet on Lucky Numbers virtually around the clock.

Bet.co.za offers Lucky Numbers too, and it's perfectly fine — but if Lucky Numbers is your main thing, Hollywoodbets is where you want to be. It's their bread and butter alongside horse racing, and they've invested heavily in making it the best experience in SA. For a deeper dive, check our complete Lucky Numbers betting guide.

Horse Racing: Where It All Started for Hollywoodbets

Hollywoodbets was born from horse racing. Before they were a betting giant, they were a horse racing bookmaker in KZN. That heritage runs deep, and it shows in every aspect of their racing product.

Hollywoodbets offers:

  • Comprehensive SA race coverage: Every meeting at Turffontein, Greyville, Kenilworth, Scottsville, and Fairview is covered in detail.
  • International racing: UK, Ireland, Australia, Hong Kong, and more — with competitive odds across the board.
  • Live streaming: Watch races live directly in the app or on the website. This is a massive advantage — you can follow your bets in real time without needing a separate DSTV subscription.
  • Tote and fixed odds: Both betting options available on most SA races.
  • Detailed form guides: Hollywoodbets provides proper form information to help you make informed selections.

Bet.co.za offers horse racing too, and they cover SA meetings well. But they don't have the same depth of international coverage, the live streaming feature, or the detailed form guides that Hollywood provides. If you're a racing fan, there's really only one choice here.

Verdict: Hollywoodbets wins horse racing by a country mile (pun intended). It's in their DNA.

Mobile App & User Experience

Most South Africans bet on their phones. Whether you're on the taxi, on lunch break, or lying on the couch on a Sunday afternoon — your betting experience is only as good as the app you're using.

Hollywoodbets Mobile

Hollywoodbets has apps for both iOS and Android, which immediately gives them an edge. The Android app can be downloaded from their website (not the Play Store, as Google restricts gambling apps in most regions), and the iOS app is available on the App Store.

The app itself is clean, fast, and purple. Navigation is intuitive — Lucky Numbers, sports, horse racing, and Spina Zonke are all easily accessible from the home screen. The bet slip works smoothly, and switching between pre-match and live betting is seamless. For a South African bookmaker, it's genuinely one of the best mobile experiences out there.

Bet.co.za Mobile

Bet.co.za has an Android app and a responsive mobile website that works well on iPhone. The absence of a dedicated iOS app is noticeable, though. Their mobile site is functional — it loads quickly and the bet placement process is straightforward — but it doesn't feel as polished as Hollywoodbets.

The app interface is clean and professional. Bet.co.za has been around long enough to know what works, and the layout reflects that experience. Markets are well-organized, and the Aviator game is prominently featured for those who enjoy it. But compared to Hollywood's slicker design, Bet.co.za's mobile experience feels a touch more utilitarian.

Verdict: Hollywoodbets takes this one. The dual-platform app availability and slightly more polished design give them the edge. That said, Bet.co.za's mobile experience is perfectly usable — it just doesn't feel as modern.

Withdrawal Speed & Payment Methods

You've won. Now you want your money. How quickly you can get it is arguably one of the most important factors when choosing a bookmaker. Nobody wants to wait days for a payout, eish.

Bet.co.za Withdrawals

  • EFT: 24–48 hours (standard bank transfer)
  • Ozow: Available for faster payouts
  • Minimum withdrawal: Varies, but typically low
  • Verification: FICA required (standard across all SA bookmakers)

Bet.co.za is reliable but not the fastest. If you withdraw on a Friday afternoon, you might only see the funds on Monday or Tuesday. It's not a dealbreaker, but it's worth knowing.

Hollywoodbets Withdrawals

  • EFT: Same day – 24 hours (often processed within a few hours)
  • Ozow: Near-instant in many cases
  • Branch withdrawal: Instant cash at any Hollywoodbets branch — a massive perk if you live near one
  • Voucher: 1Voucher and other options

Hollywoodbets has a real advantage here. Their withdrawal processing is genuinely fast — many punters report getting their money within hours of requesting it. And the branch withdrawal option is unique to Hollywoodbets. If you want cash in hand, today, you can walk into a branch and collect it. In a country where many people still prefer cash transactions, this is a huge deal.

Deposit Methods (Both)

Both bookmakers accept a wide range of deposit methods typical for South Africa:

  • EFT / bank transfer
  • Ozow (instant EFT)
  • Credit/debit card
  • Retail vouchers (1Voucher, OTT vouchers, Blu vouchers)
  • Airtime (select options)

The R10 minimum deposit on both platforms makes them accessible to everyone. Whether you're a casual punter having a R10 flutter on the weekend or a high roller staking thousands, the entry point is the same.

Verdict: Hollywoodbets wins on withdrawals. Faster processing, more options, and the branch cash collection is a feature nobody else can match.

PSL & South African Sports Coverage

Let's talk about what matters most to Mzansi: our local sports. The Premier Soccer League is the heartbeat of South African sport, and how a bookmaker covers it tells you a lot about whether they truly understand the local market.

Both Bet.co.za and Hollywoodbets are deeply invested in South African football. We're talking sponsorship deals, dedicated PSL markets, and regular promotions tied to local matches.

Hollywoodbets is a title sponsor of major SA football competitions and their brand is visible at stadiums across the country. Bet.co.za has a long history of PSL involvement too, with partnerships spanning over a decade.

In terms of actual betting markets, both offer:

  • Match result (1X2)
  • Correct score
  • Both teams to score
  • Over/under goals
  • First goalscorer
  • Halftime/fulltime
  • Multi-bet and accumulator options

Beyond the PSL, both cover Springbok rugby (test matches, Rugby Championship, URC), cricket (Proteas internationals, SA20, T20 World Cup), and other local sports like boxing and athletics.

Hollywoodbets does edge ahead in live betting for PSL matches — their in-play markets update faster and offer more options during the game. But for pre-match betting, there's very little separating them.

Verdict: A near-tie. Both understand the SA market intimately. Hollywoodbets has a slight edge in live betting depth, but Bet.co.za is no slouch.

Frequently Asked Questions

Is Bet.co.za or Hollywoodbets better for Lucky Numbers?
Hollywoodbets is the clear leader for Lucky Numbers. They offer more international lottery draws, more bet types (including bonus ball options), and their Lucky Numbers interface is more polished. Bet.co.za does offer Lucky Numbers too, but Hollywoodbets dominates this category.
Which bookmaker has faster withdrawals — Bet.co.za or Hollywoodbets?
Hollywoodbets generally processes withdrawals faster, often within a few hours and sometimes instantly via Ozow. Bet.co.za typically takes 24–48 hours for EFT withdrawals. Both support Ozow for quicker payouts, but Hollywoodbets has the edge on speed. Hollywood also offers cash withdrawals at physical branches, which is instant.
Do both Bet.co.za and Hollywoodbets cover PSL matches?
Yes, both bookmakers offer comprehensive PSL coverage including match-winner, correct score, over/under, and various prop markets. Both are deeply invested in South African football through sponsorships and partnerships. You'll get a great PSL betting experience on either platform.
Can I use Bet.co.za and Hollywoodbets on iPhone?
Hollywoodbets has a dedicated iOS app available on the App Store. Bet.co.za doesn't currently have an iOS app but their mobile website works well on iPhone through Safari. Both have Android apps available for download from their respective websites.
Which bookmaker is better for horse racing in South Africa?
Hollywoodbets is the undisputed king of horse racing in South Africa. They started as a horse racing bookmaker in KwaZulu-Natal and their racing coverage, odds, and features (including live streaming of races) are the best in the country. Bet.co.za offers horse racing too, but it's not their primary strength.
