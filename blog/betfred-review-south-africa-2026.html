Odds Quality: How Does Betfred Compare?

This is where Betfred's international experience shows. On Premier League, Champions League, and major international football, their odds are competitive with the best in SA. We've found them consistently in line with or slightly better than Betway on popular football markets.

For South African sport specifically — PSL, URC, cricket — the margins are wider than the top local bookmakers. Hollywoodbets and Betway tend to offer tighter odds on local fixtures, which makes sense given their deeper knowledge of the SA market.

Horse racing is a genuine strength. Betfred has a dedicated horse racing hub with coverage of South African meetings alongside UK, Australian, and US racing. If you're a racing punter who also bets internationally, this is a real selling point.

Sports Markets & Coverage

Betfred covers over 30 sports, which puts it near the top in terms of breadth. The full list includes:

  • Football: PSL, EPL, La Liga, Serie A, Bundesliga, Champions League — deep markets with accumulators, both teams to score, correct score, etc.
  • Rugby: URC, Rugby Championship, Six Nations, Currie Cup — match results, handicaps, try scorers, total points
  • Cricket: Proteas internationals, SA20, IPL, ICC events — full match and innings betting
  • Horse Racing: Comprehensive SA and international coverage with best odds guaranteed on selected races
  • Combat Sports: UFC/MMA, Boxing — fight result, method, round betting
  • Other: Tennis, golf, basketball, American football, esports, darts, snooker, and more

The live (in-play) section is solid, with thousands of markets available across football, cricket, rugby, and tennis. What they're missing is live streaming — which is a significant gap compared to Betway, who offer live video on many events.

Deposits & Withdrawals

Betfred accepts standard South African payment methods:

  • EFT (bank transfer): All major SA banks
  • Credit/Debit cards: Visa and Mastercard
  • Instant EFT: Available through select providers

What's missing: Ozow integration, voucher deposits (1Voucher, OTT), and Capitec Pay. For punters who prefer these more modern SA payment methods, that's a real limitation. Betway and Hollywoodbets have a clear edge here.

Withdrawal speed is average — 24–48 hours via EFT. It's fine, but it's nowhere near Betway's near-instant payouts. If fast withdrawals are your priority, check our fastest-paying bookmakers guide.

Mobile Experience

No native app yet — Betfred relies on a mobile-responsive website. It works well enough on both Android and iOS, and pages load quickly. But compared to the polished apps from Betway and Hollywoodbets, it feels a step behind.

If you're betting on the go (and most SA punters are), the mobile site is functional but not exceptional. Live betting works smoothly, markets load fast, and the bet slip is easy to use. We just wish there was a proper app for quicker access and push notifications.

How to Sign Up at Betfred

  1. Visit betfred.co.za
  2. Click "Join" in the top-right corner
  3. Enter your phone number to start registration
  4. Complete your personal details (name, ID number, address)
  5. Verify your identity via FICA (ID document + proof of address)
  6. Make your first deposit to claim the welcome bonus

The process takes about 5 minutes. FICA verification can take a few hours to a day, depending on how busy they are. Once verified, you're good to deposit and start betting.

Our Verdict

Betfred is a legitimate, well-established bookmaker that's brought genuine competition to the South African market. The R21,000 welcome bonus is the headline act, and the horse racing and international football coverage give established names a run for their money.

The gaps are real, though. No live streaming, no native app, limited SA payment methods, and slower withdrawals than the market leader (Betway). These are things a UK-based bookmaker can fix with time and investment in the local market — and given Betfred's track record of growth, we'd expect improvement.

For now, it's a solid choice as a second or third betting account, and it's worth signing up for the bonus alone. But it's not going to replace Betway or Hollywoodbets as your primary bookmaker just yet.

Frequently Asked Questions

Is Betfred legal in South Africa?
Yes. Betfred is licensed by the Western Cape Gambling and Racing Board. It operates legally at betfred.co.za and complies with South African gambling regulations, including FICA verification requirements.
What is the Betfred welcome bonus?
Betfred offers up to R21,000 in deposit bonuses plus 750 free spins across your first three deposits. The first deposit is matched at 100% (up to R7,000), the second at 50%, and the third at 75%.
How long do Betfred withdrawals take?
EFT withdrawals typically take 24–48 hours to process. This is slower than Betway (which processes in minutes) but in line with most other SA bookmakers.
Does Betfred have a mobile app?
Not yet. Betfred uses a mobile-responsive website that works on all devices. It's functional but lacks the polish of dedicated apps from Betway and Hollywoodbets.
Can I bet on PSL with Betfred?
Yes. Betfred covers the Betway Premiership (PSL) with match result, correct score, both teams to score, and accumulator markets. They also cover the Nedbank Cup, MTN 8, and Carling Knockout.
How does Betfred compare to Betway?
Betfred has a much bigger welcome bonus (R21,000 vs R1,000), better horse racing coverage, and comparable international football odds. Betway wins on withdrawal speed, live streaming, mobile app, and SA payment methods. Read our full comparison in the table above.
