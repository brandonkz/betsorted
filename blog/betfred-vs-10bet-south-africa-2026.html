Betfred vs 10bet South Africa 2026: International Bookmaker Showdown

Published 1 April 2026 · 8 min read

South Africa's betting market isn't just Hollywoodbets and Betway anymore. International operators have been making serious moves, and two of the most interesting newcomers are Betfred and 10bet — both UK-origin bookmakers that now hold SA licences.

Betfred is a heavyweight in the UK with 50+ years of history and nearly 1,400 betting shops. 10bet is newer but scrappier, competing on odds quality and aggressive sign-up offers. Both are licensed in SA, both accept rands, and both want your deposits. The question is: which one actually delivers for South African punters?

Odds Quality — Where Your Money Goes Further

This is where 10bet punches above its weight. For a mid-tier bookmaker, their football odds are surprisingly sharp — often matching or beating the SA market leaders like Betway. On a typical EPL match, 10bet's margin sits around 5-6%, which is genuinely competitive.

Betfred's margins are wider on football (7-8%) but tighter on horse racing and cricket. Their UK heritage shows — if you're betting on Cheltenham or the Grand National, Betfred's prices are hard to beat. For SA-specific sports like PSL and rugby, neither is the best in the market, but 10bet is generally the better of the two.

Sport Coverage

Betfred: The International Powerhouse

Betfred covers 30+ sports, with particular depth in horse racing, cricket, darts, and snooker — sports that get less love from SA-focused bookmakers. Their horse racing offering is world-class, covering UK, Irish, SA, and Australian meetings with early morning prices and best-price guarantees on selected races.

For South African sport, Betfred covers PSL, URC, Currie Cup, and Proteas cricket. The market depth is decent but not exceptional — you'll get match result, over/under, and major props, but for granular PSL markets (corner totals, booking points), you're better off at WSB or Hollywoodbets.

10bet: The Balanced Operator

10bet covers fewer sports (25+) but goes deeper on the ones SA punters actually bet on — football, rugby, cricket, and tennis. Their football offering spans leagues most SA bookmakers skip — Brasileirão, J-League, K League, and even lower European divisions. If you're a stats-driven bettor who finds value in obscure leagues, 10bet delivers.

UFC and MMA coverage at 10bet is also solid, with fight props and round betting available for most UFC cards. See our UFC betting guide for more.

Welcome Bonuses

Betfred: R500 Matched Bet

Betfred's welcome offer is the bigger headline number. Place your first bet up to R500, and if it loses, you get the stake back as a free bet. The catch: 3x turnover requirement on the free bet amount, and it must be used within 7 days. This is decent value if your first bet is genuinely R500 — if you bet R50, you only get R50 back.

10bet: R30 Free Bet (No Deposit Required)

10bet's offer is smaller but simpler. Register, verify FICA, get R30 in free bets. No deposit needed. The turnover requirement is 3x, and you have 14 days to use it. It's risk-free — you literally can't lose money on the sign-up.

⚠️ Our take: Betfred's offer has higher potential value (R500 vs R30) but requires a deposit and your first bet to lose. 10bet's is genuinely free money, no strings attached. For most SA punters signing up with a small bankroll, 10bet's no-deposit offer is the smarter start. See our free bet guide for extraction strategies.

Mobile Experience

Betfred

Betfred has an actual iOS app — rare among SA bookmakers. Their Android app is also available as an APK download. The interface is polished and fast, with quick navigation between sports. The bet slip is well-designed with clear accumulator building. Live betting updates are smooth.

The downside: the app is clearly designed for the UK market first. SA sports sometimes feel like an afterthought in the navigation — you might need to scroll past darts, greyhounds, and snooker to find PSL. Minor annoyance, not a dealbreaker.

10bet

10bet's mobile experience is web-first — no iOS app, Android APK only. The mobile site is responsive and functional, loading quickly even on slower connections. The UI is clean but not exciting. Sports navigation is straightforward, and the bet slip works without drama.

Neither bookmaker offers USSD or data-free betting. If that's important, look at our data-free betting guide.

Live Betting

This is where Betfred has a genuine advantage. Their live betting platform covers more events simultaneously, with deeper in-play markets. You'll find next goalscorer, corner timing, and booking markets during live PSL matches — stuff that 10bet doesn't always offer.

Betfred also offers partial cash-out, letting you lock in some profit while keeping part of your bet live. 10bet only offers full cash-out — it's all or nothing. If you're an active live bettor who manages positions, Betfred's partial cash-out is a meaningful advantage.

The Bottom Line

Both are legitimate international bookmakers with SA licences — you can trust either with your money. 10bet wins on value — better odds, faster payouts, and a risk-free sign-up bonus. Betfred wins on features — deeper horse racing, better live betting, and a more polished app. For most SA sports bettors, 10bet is the more practical choice. Horse racing purists should go Betfred.

Frequently Asked Questions

Is Betfred or 10bet better for South African bettors?

For most SA punters focused on football and rugby, 10bet offers better odds and faster payouts. Betfred is better for horse racing fans and those who want deep live betting markets with partial cash-out. Both are licensed and safe.

Are Betfred and 10bet licensed in South Africa?

Yes. Both hold valid South African gambling licences issued by provincial gambling boards. Your deposits are protected by SA gambling regulations.

Which has better odds — Betfred or 10bet?

10bet generally offers tighter margins on football (5-6%) compared to Betfred (7-8%). Betfred is more competitive on horse racing and cricket. The best approach is to compare prices before betting — use our odds comparison tool.

Can I do USSD betting with Betfred or 10bet?

No. Neither offers USSD or data-free betting. If you need that, look at Hollywoodbets or WSB, both of which offer data-free options.
