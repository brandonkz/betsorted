FICA, withdrawals and proof

FICA is not a side issue. It is the point where many betting accounts become frustrating. Use your real name, your own bank account and documents that match the information you gave during registration. If you deposit from one person and try to withdraw to another, delays are likely. If the bookmaker asks for documents, submit them through the official account area only.

Withdrawal timing should be judged from operator-published terms or your own small test, not from wishful thinking. If a public page does not state a payout time, BetSorted does not invent one. The cleanest approach is to test a small withdrawal before building a bigger betting balance. That test shows whether the account, payment route and verification process work in practice.

Keep screenshots or emails for bonus terms you accept. If a promotion has wagering, minimum odds, expiry rules or a maximum conversion cap, those details matter later. A bonus that blocks a clean withdrawal can be worse than no bonus at all.
