Is Betshezi Legal and Safe?

The site uses SSL encryption for all transactions. From a legitimacy standpoint, Betshezi ticks the same boxes as Betway, Hollywoodbets, and Sportingbet. It's not a fly-by-night operation.

Sportsbook: What Can You Bet On?

Betshezi covers 30+ sports, which is impressive for a newer SA bookmaker. Here's what matters for local punters:

Full PSL coverage with match winner, handicaps, totals, correct score, and same game multis. EPL, La Liga, Serie A, and Champions League are all well-covered. The depth of markets per match is solid — not quite Betway levels, but more than Supabets or Easybet.

Cricket

SA20, Proteas internationals, IPL, and Big Bash are all covered. In-play cricket markets update quickly, which is a plus if you're betting during matches.

Esports

This is where Betshezi actually stands out. Counter-Strike 2, League of Legends, Dota 2, and Valorant all have deep market coverage. If you're into esports betting, Betshezi might be the best option among SA-licensed bookmakers.

Niche Sports
