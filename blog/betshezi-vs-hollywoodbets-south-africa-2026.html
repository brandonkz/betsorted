Introduction: The Newcomer Takes On the Giant

This is a proper David vs Goliath matchup. Hollywoodbets is the biggest bookmaker in South Africa — full stop. Over 100 retail branches, millions of active punters, and a brand so recognisable that even your gogo probably knows the purple logo. They've been at this for over two decades and they've earned every bit of that dominance.

Then there's Betshezi. A newer entrant to the SA market that's been quietly making moves. They're not trying to outspend Hollywoodbets on billboards or stadium sponsorships — they're going after the digital-first punter with a clean mobile experience, sharp promotions, and a growing sports offering that's improving month by month.

Both are fully licensed and regulated in South Africa. Hollywoodbets holds its licence from the KwaZulu-Natal Gaming Board, while Betshezi is licensed by the Gauteng Gambling Board. Your money is protected with either. The question is which platform gives you the better betting experience in 2026.

We've used both — deposited real money, placed bets on PSL derbies and URC rugby, tested withdrawals through EFT and Capitec Pay, and spent hours on both mobile platforms. Here's the no-nonsense comparison.

Sports Coverage & Odds Hollywoodbets Wins

Hollywoodbets covers more than 30 sports with solid depth across the ones South Africans actually bet on. Football (PSL, Premier League, Champions League), rugby (URC, Rugby Championship, Currie Cup), cricket (SA20, Proteas internationals, IPL), horse racing — it's all there with competitive odds and decent market variety.

Betshezi covers around 25 sports and is growing. The core offerings — football, rugby, cricket, tennis — are present and the odds are competitive on main markets. Where Betshezi falls short is depth. On a standard PSL match, Hollywoodbets might give you 50-60 markets (correct score, both teams to score, half-time/full-time, first goalscorer) while Betshezi sticks closer to 20-30 markets with the basics: match result, over/under, double chance, and correct score.

Odds Quality

We compared odds on 15 PSL and Premier League matches across both platforms. The margins were tight on headline markets — a Kaizer Chiefs win might be 2.30 on Betshezi and 2.35 on Hollywoodbets, or vice versa. Neither consistently undercuts the other on main markets. However, Hollywoodbets tends to offer slightly better value on niche markets and specials, simply because they have more of them and competition drives those odds tighter.

For URC rugby, Hollywoodbets is noticeably stronger. More try scorer markets, handicap options, and total points lines. Betshezi covers the big rugby matches but the market depth is thinner — often just match result and handicap.

PSL & Local Sports Betting Hollywoodbets Wins

Hollywoodbets is a PSL sponsor. They get this. When you log in during a PSL weekend, the homepage is plastered with boosted odds on the Soweto Derby, enhanced multis across the weekend fixtures, and specials you won't find anywhere else. It feels like a bookmaker that actually watches South African football.

Betshezi covers every PSL match — you're not going to miss out on backing Sundowns or Pirates. But the experience is more utilitarian. The markets are there, the odds are fair, but there's less of that local flavour and fewer specials built around the SA football calendar.

Cricket

Both platforms cover SA20, Proteas internationals, and major tournaments like the IPL. Hollywoodbets has an edge with more betting markets per match — top batsman, method of dismissal, session runs — while Betshezi tends to stick to match winner, top batsman, and total runs. For the casual cricket punter, either works. For someone who wants to bet on Temba Bavuma's exact runs in the first innings, Hollywoodbets is the better choice.

Horse Racing

Hollywoodbets has deep roots in horse racing — it's literally where they started. The Durban July, Sun Met, and daily racecards from Turffontein and Kenilworth are covered extensively with win, place, forecast, and exotic bet types. Betshezi offers horse racing but with a more basic selection. If you're a racing punter, there's no contest here.

Lucky Numbers & Spina Zonke Hollywoodbets Wins

This is Hollywoodbets' home turf, and honestly, no bookmaker in South Africa comes close.

Lucky Numbers

Hollywoodbets practically invented the Lucky Numbers experience for SA punters. They cover dozens of international lottery draws — UK 49s, Irish Daily Millions, South Africa Lotto, German Lotto, and plenty of others that give you a draw to bet on almost every hour of the day. The interface is clean, the bet types are extensive (first ball, last ball, powerball, over/under), and the payouts are among the best in the market.

Betshezi has Lucky Numbers too, and it's a decent offering. But it covers fewer draws, has fewer bet types per draw, and the overall experience feels a step behind. If Lucky Numbers is your main thing, Hollywoodbets wins this easily.

Spina Zonke Slots

Spina Zonke is Hollywoodbets' online slots brand, and it's become a phenomenon. Hundreds of slot games, progressive jackpots, and you can start spinning from as little as R0.50. It's wildly popular — you'll see queues at Hollywood branches specifically for Spina Zonke terminals.

Betshezi has a casino section with some slots and games, but it's a fraction of what Hollywoodbets offers. If you enjoy spinning between bets on the football, Hollywoodbets gives you a genuinely massive selection. Betshezi is building its casino offering, but in 2026 it's still playing catch-up.

Mobile Experience Hollywoodbets Wins

Hollywoodbets has invested heavily in mobile and it shows. They've got a native Android app, an iOS app, a responsive mobile website, USSD betting for feature phones, and — this is the big one — data-free access on all major networks. Whether you're on Vodacom, MTN, Cell C, or Telkom, you can browse and bet without burning a single megabyte of data.

In a country where data costs genuinely affect what people can do online, this is a massive competitive advantage. A punter in Limpopo on a R29 data bundle can bet on Hollywoodbets all month without worrying about data running out. That matters.

Betshezi has an Android APK (download it from their website — it's not on the Play Store) and a functional mobile site. The mobile experience is clean enough and betting works fine. But there's no iOS app, limited data-free options, and the overall polish is a step behind Hollywoodbets. Navigation is straightforward for finding a match and placing a bet, but things like live betting and account management feel more basic.

If mobile is your primary way of betting — and for most SA punters, it is — Hollywoodbets gives you a more complete and accessible experience across all devices and networks.

Live Betting & Cash-Out Hollywoodbets Wins

Hollywoodbets' live betting has improved significantly over the past two years. During a PSL match, you'll find in-play markets for match result, next goal, total goals, corners, and cards — all updating smoothly. The cash-out feature lets you lock in profit or limit losses mid-match, and it works reliably on both the app and mobile site.

The experience isn't as deep as what you'd get from a Betway or an international bookmaker, but for the SA market, it's solid. We counted 30-40 live markets on a typical Sundowns Champions League match, which covers the needs of most punters.

Betshezi has live betting, and it functions. But the market range is more limited — often just match result, next goal, and over/under. The odds update a bit slower, and the cash-out feature is less consistently available. It feels like live betting is still being built out rather than being a mature product.

If you're the type who watches the game and wants to react to what's happening on the pitch, Hollywoodbets is the better platform right now. Betshezi will likely improve here, but in 2026, they're behind.

Promotions & Loyalty Draw

Both bookmakers run regular promotions beyond the welcome bonus, and both take different approaches that work in their own way.

Hollywoodbets Promotions

Hollywoodbets runs a steady rotation of promos — boosted odds on big PSL matches, multi boosts where your accumulator payout gets increased based on the number of legs, and regular Lucky Numbers specials. Their Top Bet feature lets you win up to 100x your stake on correct score predictions. It's not the flashiest promotion programme in SA, but it's consistent and the offers actually add value rather than being all sizzle and no steak.

Betshezi Promotions

Betshezi, as the newer bookmaker, tends to be more aggressive with promotions to win market share. They run multi-boost offers, free bet promotions for existing customers, and sport-specific bonuses around major tournaments. Some of these offers have been genuinely generous — during the 2025/26 PSL season, they ran a free bet promo that was better value than what most established bookmakers were offering.

The flip side is that Betshezi's promotional calendar is less predictable. Hollywoodbets runs the same types of promos on a regular cadence — you know what to expect. Betshezi's offers change more frequently, which can be exciting or frustrating depending on your perspective.

Retail Presence & Accessibility Hollywoodbets Wins

This one isn't even a competition. Hollywoodbets has over 100 retail branches across South Africa — from Sandton to Soweto to the small towns in KZN and the Eastern Cape. You can walk in, deposit cash, place a bet on a terminal, and collect your winnings over the counter. For punters who don't have bank accounts, don't trust online payments, or simply prefer face-to-face service, this is invaluable.

Betshezi is a purely online bookmaker. No branches, no retail terminals, no walk-in service. Everything happens on their website or mobile app. For the digital-native punter in Sandton who does everything on their phone, this isn't a problem. But for a significant chunk of the SA betting market — particularly in rural areas and townships — the lack of a physical presence is a real limitation.

Hollywoodbets' retail network is one of the main reasons they're the biggest bookmaker in the country. It's not just about convenience — it's about trust. People trust a brand they can physically see on their high street.
