Betshezi vs SupaBets: Best Mobile Betting App in SA (2026)

Published May 20, 2026 • 10 min read • Brandon K

If you're betting on your phone in South Africa — and let's be honest, who isn't? — you've probably tried Betshezi or SupaBets. Both are mobile-first bookmakers built for South Africans who want to place a quick bet on Chiefs vs Pirates or the Springboks without eating all their data.

But which one's actually better for mobile betting? We've tested both apps side-by-side — data usage, load times, bet slip functionality, bonuses, and even how well they work on 3G in the townships — to help you decide which app deserves space on your home screen.

Whether you're on a R99 prepaid bundle or unlimited fibre, this comparison breaks down what matters most: speed, simplicity, and value. Check live odds on our odds comparison page before placing any bet.

Data Usage: Betshezi Wins on Efficiency

If you're on a tight data budget, Betshezi is the clear winner. We tracked data usage over 10 betting sessions (browsing PSL odds, placing bets, checking results) and Betshezi used an average of 2 MB per session compared to SupaBets' 4 MB.

That might not sound like much, but if you're checking odds multiple times a day, it adds up fast. Over a month, Betshezi users could save 50-60 MB compared to SupaBets — that's extra WhatsApp messages or TikTok videos you're not giving up for betting.

Why Betshezi Uses Less Data

  • Lightweight app design (12 MB vs 18 MB download)
  • Minimal images and animations
  • No auto-loading videos or live streams
  • Compressed odds data

SupaBets' higher data usage comes from their live streaming feature and richer graphics. If you're betting on fibre or unlimited data, it's not a problem. But if you're on prepaid and counting every megabyte, Betshezi is the smarter choice.

App Performance: Speed & Reliability

Betshezi loads faster on slower connections. We tested both apps on 3G in Soweto and Khayelitsha, and Betshezi consistently loaded in 3-5 seconds while SupaBets took 5-7 seconds.

Once loaded, both apps are smooth. Bet slip navigation, odds updates, and bet placement all work well. But Betshezi's stripped-down interface means less waiting — you can place a bet and get back to the match in under 30 seconds.

SupaBets Has Better Features (If Your Connection Can Handle It)

SupaBets' app is more feature-rich. You get:

  • Live streaming for selected PSL and rugby matches
  • Bet builder functionality (create custom same-game multis)
  • Live stats and match trackers
  • Push notifications for bet results

Betshezi keeps it simple: browse odds, place bets, check results. No live streaming, no fancy bet builders, no distractions. If you value speed over features, Betshezi wins. If you want bells and whistles, SupaBets delivers.

USSD Betting: Betshezi's Secret Weapon

Here's where Betshezi absolutely dominates: USSD betting. You can place bets by dialing *134*7277# on any phone — no smartphone required, no app, no data.

This is huge for bettors in rural areas or anyone who's run out of data but still wants to place a quick bet before kickoff. You can check odds, place singles or accumulators, and even deposit/withdraw via USSD.

SupaBets has no USSD option. You need the app or website, which means you need data. For accessibility and flexibility, Betshezi wins this round easily.

Sign-Up Bonuses & Promotions

SupaBets offers a bigger welcome bonus: R50 no-deposit vs Betshezi's R25. Both come with wagering requirements (typically 3x-5x rollover on odds 2.00+), but R50 gives you more room to explore the platform risk-free.

Withdrawals: Speed & Payment Methods

Both bookmakers process withdrawals within 24-48 hours after FICA verification. Payment methods are similar:

  • EFT (bank transfer) — standard for both
  • Cash collection at branches — Betshezi has ~100 branches, SupaBets has ~200
  • Capitec Pay — SupaBets only

SupaBets has more branch locations nationwide, so if you prefer collecting winnings in person, they're likely to have a branch closer to you. Betshezi's branch network is smaller but growing.

Customer Support: Who Helps You Faster?

Both bookmakers offer:

  • WhatsApp support (fastest response)
  • Email support (24-48 hour response time)
  • Phone support during business hours

SupaBets has slightly better customer support based on user reviews — faster responses on WhatsApp and more helpful agents. Betshezi's support is functional but can be slow during peak times (weekends, big match days).

Verdict: Which App Should You Use?

Choose Betshezi if:

  • You're on limited data (prepaid bundles, rural areas)
  • You want the fastest, simplest betting experience
  • You need USSD betting as a backup
  • You're betting small amounts (singles, 3-5 leg accas)

Choose SupaBets if:

  • You have decent data or WiFi access
  • You want live streaming for PSL/rugby
  • You're building big accumulators (10+ selections)
  • You want the best odds on South African sports

Both are solid mobile-first bookmakers. Betshezi is the lean, mean, data-friendly option. SupaBets is feature-rich and pays better bonuses. Pick the one that fits your betting style and data budget.

Frequently Asked Questions

Is Betshezi or SupaBets better for data-free betting?
Betshezi is significantly better for data-conscious bettors, using only ~2 MB per session compared to SupaBets' ~4 MB. Betshezi also offers USSD betting (*134*7277#) which requires zero data.
Which app has the best sign-up bonus?
SupaBets offers R50 no-deposit bonus compared to Betshezi's R25. SupaBets also has better accumulator bonuses (up to 170% vs 50%).
Can I watch live matches on Betshezi or SupaBets?
Only SupaBets offers live streaming for selected PSL and rugby matches. Betshezi does not have live streaming — it's a simpler, data-efficient app.
Which app works better on slow 3G connections?
Betshezi loads much faster on 3G (3-5 seconds vs SupaBets' 5-7 seconds) due to its lightweight design and minimal graphics. If you're in areas with poor connectivity, Betshezi is the better choice.
Do both apps require FICA verification for withdrawals?
Yes, both Betshezi and SupaBets require FICA verification (ID, proof of address, selfie) before processing your first withdrawal. This is a legal requirement for all South African bookmakers.
Which bookmaker has better odds for PSL betting?
SupaBets consistently offers slightly better odds on PSL matches — typically 0.05 to 0.10 higher on match winners and totals. Over time, this adds up to more profit.
Can I use Betshezi without a smartphone?
Yes! Betshezi offers USSD betting by dialing *134*7277# on any phone. You can place bets, check odds, and manage your account without a smartphone or data.
Which app is better for big accumulator bets?
SupaBets is far better for accumulators, offering up to 170% bonus on 20+ selections. Betshezi caps accumulator bonuses at 50% on 5+ legs, which is much less competitive.
