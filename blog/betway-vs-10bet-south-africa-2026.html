Odds Comparison: Who Pays Better?

This is the section that actually matters for your wallet. Let's break it down by sport.

PSL (DStv Premiership)

Here's where 10bet makes its strongest case. On PSL matches — your Kaizer Chiefs vs Mamelodi Sundowns, Orlando Pirates vs Stellenbosch — 10bet consistently offers competitive odds that match or slightly beat Betway's prices. They've clearly targeted the local football market, and it shows. On a typical PSL weekend, you'll find 10bet paying 2–4% better on selected match outcomes.

That doesn't sound like much, but if you're betting R500 per weekend on PSL matches, those extra cents per rand add up to real money over a season. Smart punters know this — which is why they compare odds before every bet.

Premier League & Champions League

International football is where Betway flexes. Their margins on Premier League and Champions League matches are consistently tighter than 10bet's. On a big match like Manchester City vs Liverpool, Betway's combined margin might sit around 4–5%, while 10bet's is closer to 6–7%. That difference means you're getting better value with Betway on the global stuff.

10bet is still competitive — they're not offering terrible prices — but if you bet heavily on European football, Betway is the smarter choice more often than not.

Rugby (URC, Springboks, Rugby Championship)

Betway has the edge on rugby. Their coverage of URC matches, Springbok Tests, and the Rugby Championship is broader, with more betting markets per game. 10bet covers the major rugby events, but the depth isn't quite there — fewer player props, fewer handicap options, and slightly wider margins.

If rugby is your main sport, Betway is the clear pick. If you only bet on the odd Springbok Test, either bookmaker will serve you fine.

Withdrawal Speed: Betway's Biggest Weapon

If you've ever waited three days to get your winnings from a bookmaker, you know the frustration. Betway has essentially eliminated this problem in South Africa.

Once you've completed your FICA verification (ID, proof of address — standard stuff), Betway processes EFT withdrawals almost instantly. We're talking minutes. Request a payout at 10 PM on a Friday night, and the money's in your bank account before you've finished your beer. It's genuinely impressive and one of the main reasons punters stick with Betway.

10bet's withdrawal speed is decent by industry standards — 24 to 48 hours for EFT withdrawals. That's not bad. Many SA bookmakers take similar timeframes. But when your mate is cashing out from Betway in 3 minutes while you're still waiting for 10bet to process, it does feel a bit slow.

10bet does support multiple withdrawal methods including EFT and e-wallets, and they don't charge withdrawal fees, which is a plus. But on raw speed alone, Betway is in a league of its own.

Winner: Betway — instant payouts are a game-changer.

Welcome Bonuses: R1,000 vs R300

Betway offers up to R1,000 in free bets when you sign up and make your first deposit. It's a matched bonus — deposit R1,000, get R1,000 in free bet credits. The wagering requirements are standard: you'll need to turn over the bonus amount before withdrawing winnings. Nothing unusual there, but do read the T&Cs.

10bet's welcome offer is a R300 free bet. It's smaller, but the wagering requirements are straightforward and the minimum qualifying deposit is reasonable. For punters who aren't looking to throw R1,000 at a new bookmaker right away, 10bet's lower-entry bonus might actually feel more accessible.

Here's the real talk: welcome bonuses are a one-time thing. They get you in the door, but they shouldn't be the deciding factor. What matters is the ongoing value — odds quality, promotions, and payout reliability. Both bookmakers run regular promotions on football matches, with Betway's "Betway Boost" enhanced odds being particularly popular.

Winner: Betway on size. 10bet is more accessible for smaller bankrolls.

Mobile App: Betway's Polish vs 10bet's Functionality

Let's be honest — most of us are betting from our phones. The app experience matters.

Betway's mobile app is one of the best in South Africa. Available on both iOS and Android, it's fast, clean, and mirrors the full desktop experience. Live betting works smoothly, multi-bets are easy to build, and biometric login means you're in and placing bets within seconds. The UI is intuitive — even if you've never used Betway before, you'll find your way around quickly.

10bet offers an Android app and a mobile-optimised website that works well on iOS. The app is functional and covers all the essential features — pre-match betting, live betting, account management, deposits, and withdrawals. It does the job. But it doesn't feel as polished as Betway's offering. Navigation is slightly more cluttered, and the live betting section doesn't update quite as snappily.

That said, 10bet's app has improved significantly since they entered the SA market. If you're not obsessed with UI design and just want to place bets without friction, 10bet's app will serve you perfectly well.

Winner: Betway — the app is genuinely excellent. But 10bet is perfectly serviceable.

Live Betting: Depth vs Simplicity

Live betting is where the differences between these two bookmakers become most obvious.

Betway's live betting platform is deep. During a PSL match, you might have 80+ markets available — next goal, corners, cards, Asian handicaps, exact score, half-time/full-time combos, and more. The odds update in real time, bet placement is fast, and there's minimal lag between what's happening on the pitch and what's available on your screen. They also offer live streaming on selected events, so you can watch and bet in the same app.

10bet's live betting is solid but simpler. You'll get the main markets — match result, over/under, both teams to score, next goal — and the odds are competitive. But the range of micro-markets that makes live betting genuinely exciting is narrower compared to Betway. If you want to bet on the number of corners in the second half of Pirates vs Sundowns, Betway has it. 10bet probably doesn't.

For casual live bettors who stick to the main markets, 10bet is absolutely fine. For punters who live on the in-play markets and want maximum choice, Betway is the obvious pick.

Winner: Betway — more markets, faster updates, live streaming.

FAQ

Is Betway or 10bet better for PSL betting in South Africa?

10bet often edges Betway on PSL match odds, offering slightly better prices on local football markets. However, Betway's live betting depth during PSL matches is superior, with more in-play markets available. If you want pre-match value on the PSL, 10bet is worth checking. For live PSL betting, Betway wins.

Which bookmaker pays out faster — Betway or 10bet?

Betway is significantly faster. Once FICA-verified, Betway processes EFT withdrawals in minutes — often under 5 minutes. 10bet typically takes 24–48 hours for EFT withdrawals. If fast payouts are your priority, Betway is the clear winner.

Does 10bet have a mobile app for South Africa?

Yes, 10bet offers a mobile app for Android and a mobile-optimised website for iOS users. The app covers all sports markets and live betting. It's functional and reliable, though Betway's app is generally considered more polished.

Can I use both Betway and 10bet at the same time?

Absolutely. Many experienced SA punters hold accounts with multiple bookmakers to shop for the best odds on every bet. There's no rule against it, and it's one of the smartest things you can do to maximise long-term value.

Are Betway and 10bet both licensed in South Africa?

Yes. Both Betway and 10bet hold valid licences from the relevant South African provincial gambling boards. Both are legal and regulated, and both require FICA verification before you can withdraw funds.
