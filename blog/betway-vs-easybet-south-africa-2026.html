Betway and Easybet sit at opposite ends of the South African betting spectrum — and that's exactly what makes this comparison interesting. Easybet is the scrappy underdog that's carved out a loyal following with rock-bottom deposits, Lucky Numbers, and a social media presence that actually feels human. Both are licensed, both cover the PSL, and both want your money. The question is which one deserves it.

We've tested both platforms side by side — deposits, withdrawals, odds, live betting, the lot — so you don't have to open two accounts and figure it out the hard way. Odds Comparison: Who Actually Pays More?

Odds are the thing that matters most to your bottom line, so let's get into it.

PSL (Betway Premiership)

This is where things get interesting. On a typical Kaizer Chiefs vs Orlando Pirates derby, Easybet's odds are often surprisingly competitive — sometimes matching Betway, occasionally beating them by a small margin on selected outcomes. They've clearly identified the local football market as their battleground, and they're pricing aggressively to win punters over.

For example, on a Mamelodi Sundowns match where they're heavy favourites, you might find Easybet offering 1.85 on a Sundowns win where Betway has 1.80. That's a small edge, but across a full PSL season of regular betting, those extra cents per rand compound into something meaningful. Our odds comparison tool lets you check this in real time before every bet.

Premier League & International Football

International football is where Betway pulls ahead. Their margins on EPL matches are consistently tighter. On a big match — Arsenal vs Chelsea, City vs Liverpool — Betway's overround might sit at 4–5%, while Easybet's is closer to 7–8%. That gap is noticeable if you're a regular EPL punter.

Easybet's international coverage is decent but not as deep. You'll get the main markets on major leagues, but Betway offers more depth — Asian handicaps, player props, half-time markets, and specials that Easybet doesn't always match.

Rugby & Other SA Sports

Betway has the edge on rugby — broader URC and Rugby Championship coverage with more markets per match. For Springbok Tests, Betway offers deeper props (first try scorer, handicaps, total points) compared to Easybet's more basic match result and handicap offerings. If you're the type who bets on whether Cheslin Kolbe will score the first try against the All Blacks, Betway is your spot.

Cricket coverage is similar — Betway offers more depth on Proteas matches and T20 leagues, while Easybet covers the big events without going too deep.

Welcome Bonuses: R1,000 vs R50

On paper, this isn't close. Betway offers up to R1,000 in free bets — deposit R1,000, get R1,000 in free bet credits. It's a generous welcome offer by SA standards, though the standard wagering requirements apply. You'll need to play through the bonus before withdrawing, so read the T&Cs.

Easybet's welcome bonus is a R50 free bet. That's significantly smaller, but here's the thing — it matches the kind of punter Easybet is targeting. If you're the type who deposits R20 to bet on the Chiefs game this weekend, a R50 free bet is actually quite useful. Not everyone has R1,000 to throw at a new bookmaker on day one.

The honest take: welcome bonuses are a one-time thing. They get you through the door, but they're not why you should pick a bookmaker. Ongoing value — odds quality, regular promotions, and Betway's popular "Betway Boost" enhanced odds — matters far more in the long run. Easybet runs regular social media promotions and giveaways that keep their community engaged, even if they can't match Betway's bonus size.

Winner: Betway on raw numbers. But Easybet's lower barrier makes it more realistic for budget punters.

Withdrawal Speed: Betway's Biggest Advantage

If you've ever won a nice bet and then sat refreshing your banking app for two days waiting for the payout, you'll understand why this section matters so much.

Betway's withdrawal speed in South Africa is genuinely industry-leading. Once you've completed your FICA verification — that's your ID document and proof of address, standard for all legal SA bookmakers — EFT withdrawals process almost instantly. We're talking minutes. Win a bet at 9 PM, request a withdrawal, and the money is in your FNB or Capitec account before you've finished your Castle. It's one of the main reasons punters stay loyal to Betway.

Easybet's withdrawals take 24 to 48 hours via EFT. By industry standards, that's perfectly normal — most SA bookmakers operate in that timeframe. They don't charge withdrawal fees, which is a plus. But compared to Betway's near-instant payouts, it feels slow. If you're someone who values getting your money out quickly (and who doesn't?), Betway wins this one convincingly.

Both bookmakers require FICA verification before your first withdrawal. Get this sorted as soon as you open your account — don't wait until you've won and want your cash. Upload your documents early and save yourself the frustration.

Winner: Betway — instant payouts are a genuine game-changer.

Mobile App: Polish vs Practicality

Most South Africans are betting on their phones, so the app experience is non-negotiable.

Betway's mobile app is one of the best in the country. Available on iOS and Android, it's fast, clean, and mirrors the full desktop experience. Live betting is smooth, building multi-bets is intuitive, and biometric login means you're in and placing bets within seconds. The UI is well thought out — markets are easy to find, the betslip is always accessible, and nothing feels cluttered.

Easybet's app is available on Android with a mobile-optimised website that works on iOS. It gets the job done — you can browse markets, place bets, manage your account, deposit, and withdraw without any drama. It's not as slick as Betway's app. Navigation feels a little more basic, and the live betting section doesn't have the same real-time polish. But here's the thing many punters appreciate: Easybet's app is lighter on data. If you're betting on mobile data rather than WiFi (which is most of SA, let's be real), that's a practical advantage.

Winner: Betway on design and features. Easybet gets points for being data-friendly.

PSL & South African Sports Coverage

Both bookmakers cover the PSL — that's table stakes for any SA-focused betting site. But the depth differs.

Betway's PSL coverage is comprehensive. Every Betway Premiership match gets full pre-match and live markets — match result, correct score, over/under, both teams to score, Asian handicaps, first goalscorer, and a range of specials. During a big match like Chiefs vs Sundowns, you might see 80+ markets. They also cover the Nedbank Cup, MTN8, and lower divisions to varying degrees.

Easybet covers all PSL matches with the main betting markets — match result, over/under, both teams to score, double chance. The range is narrower than Betway's, but the odds on those core markets are solid. Where Easybet falls short is in the depth of live betting during PSL matches — fewer in-play markets and slightly slower updates compared to Betway's real-time platform.

Beyond football, Betway offers more breadth across SA sports — rugby (URC, Currie Cup, Springboks), cricket (Proteas, SA20), and even some coverage of local horse racing. Easybet's focus is more squarely on football and Lucky Numbers, with less depth on rugby and cricket.

Lucky Numbers: Easybet's Secret Weapon

Here's where Easybet has something Betway simply doesn't — Lucky Numbers.

If you're not familiar, Lucky Numbers lets you bet on the outcomes of lottery draws from around the world. It's massively popular in South Africa — arguably more popular than traditional sports betting for a large chunk of the market. You pick numbers, choose your draw (UK 49s, SA Lotto, international draws), and if your numbers come up, you win. Simple, fast, and addictive in the best way.

Easybet offers a full Lucky Numbers product with multiple daily draws, different bet types, and competitive payouts. For many SA punters, this is the primary reason they have an Easybet account. It's a product that fits the South African betting culture perfectly.

Betway does not offer Lucky Numbers. Full stop. If Lucky Numbers is part of your regular betting rotation — and for millions of South Africans, it absolutely is — then you need an Easybet account (or a bookmaker that offers it). This is Betway's most significant gap in the SA market.

Winner: Easybet — you can't win a category you don't compete in.

The Verdict: Betway vs Easybet South Africa

Betway is the more complete bookmaker overall. Instant payouts, a world-class app, deep live betting, competitive international odds, and the Betway Boost feature make it the default recommendation for serious punters. If you had to pick just one bookmaker, Betway is the safer choice for most people.

But Easybet fills gaps that Betway leaves wide open. The R5 minimum deposit is the lowest in South Africa and genuinely matters for punters who don't want to commit R100+ to get started. Lucky Numbers is a massive product that Betway ignores entirely. And Easybet's competitive PSL odds mean you're not sacrificing value on local football just because you're betting with a smaller operator.

The smart move? Have both. Use Betway for international sport, live betting, and instant payouts. Use Easybet for Lucky Numbers, PSL matches where they're offering better prices, and those weekends when you only have R20 to play with. Having accounts with both costs you nothing and makes you a sharper punter.

FAQ

Is Betway or Easybet better for betting in South Africa?

It depends on what you value most. Betway is the stronger all-round platform with instant payouts, deeper live betting markets, and a polished app. Easybet is better for budget-conscious punters with its R5 minimum deposit, Lucky Numbers, and competitive odds on PSL football. Many SA punters use both.

Does Easybet have Lucky Numbers?

Yes, Easybet offers Lucky Numbers — a hugely popular product in South Africa that lets you bet on lottery draws from around the world. Betway does not offer Lucky Numbers, which is a significant gap for punters who enjoy this type of betting.

Which pays out faster — Betway or Easybet?

Betway is significantly faster. Once FICA-verified, Betway processes EFT withdrawals almost instantly — often within minutes. Easybet takes 24–48 hours for EFT withdrawals. If fast access to your winnings matters, Betway wins this category by a wide margin.

What is the minimum deposit for Easybet South Africa?

Easybet has the lowest minimum deposit of any major SA bookmaker at just R5. Betway's minimum deposit is R10. Both are accessible, but Easybet is the clear winner for punters on a tight budget who want to get started with very little.

Are both Betway and Easybet licensed in South Africa?

Yes. Both Betway and Easybet hold valid licences from South African provincial gambling boards. Both are legal and regulated, and both require FICA verification (ID and proof of address) before you can withdraw funds.
