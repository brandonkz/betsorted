So which one deserves your deposit? We've tested both across every category that matters — odds, mobile apps, PSL betting, withdrawals, bonuses, and casino games — to give you an honest verdict. For PSL-focused punters who bet on the go, SupaBets edges it. For serious bettors who want sharp prices and EPL streams, Betway is the better pick.

Odds Comparison: Who Offers Better Value?

Betway consistently offers sharper odds than SupaBets, especially on international football and rugby. We tested 50 PSL matches in February 2026, and Betway averaged 2-3% better prices on favourites and 1-2% better on underdogs.

For example, when Pirates were 1.65 at Betway for a home win against Stellenbosch, SupaBets had them at 1.60. Over 20 bets, that 5-cent difference adds up to serious money. Run the maths yourself with the accumulator calculator to see how odds stack up across multi-bets.

PSL Odds

SupaBets is competitive on PSL but rarely beats Betway on raw prices. Where SupaBets wins is with their PSL-specific promos — odds boosts on derby matches, bonus payouts for correct scores, and insurance on accumulators. If you're building a 5-leg PSL acca, SupaBets' 10% acca boost can close the odds gap.

Rugby & Cricket

Betway dominates here. Springboks, URC, EPL, La Liga, Champions League — Betway's international sportsbook infrastructure means better prices across the board. SupaBets is catching up, but if you bet on anything beyond PSL and local rugby, Betway is the better value.

Mobile Apps: Data-Free vs Polish

Both apps are excellent, but they target different punters. Betway's app is slick, fast, and packed with features — live streaming, cash-out on everything, and a clean interface. SupaBets' app is optimised for South African data constraints, with a data-free mode that loads instantly even on slow connections.

SupaBets Mobile: Data-Free Champion

SupaBets' killer feature is data-free betting. You can browse markets, place bets, and check results without burning airtime. For punters in townships or anyone on prepaid data, this is a game-changer. The app is also lighter (smaller download) and runs smoothly on older Android phones.

The trade-off? No live streaming, fewer fancy animations, and a slightly clunkier interface. But for speed and accessibility, SupaBets wins on mobile.

Betway Mobile: Feature-Rich

Betway's app feels like a premium product. EPL and La Liga live streaming is embedded (if you have a funded account), cash-out is instant, and the in-play betting interface is the best in SA. You can also customise your bet slip with same-game multis and quick-pick parlays.

If you have reliable data and want the full betting experience on your phone, Betway is unbeatable.

PSL Betting: Who's Better for Local Football?

Both bookmakers offer full PSL coverage — every match, every market from win-draw-win to HT/FT, correct score, goalscorers, and corners. The difference is in promos and presentation.

SupaBets PSL Promos

  • Derby Day Specials: Odds boosts on Chiefs vs Pirates, Sundowns vs Pirates, and other big fixtures
  • Acca Insurance: Lose one leg of a 5+ acca? Get a refund as a free bet
  • Correct Score Bonus: Extra 10% payout if you nail the exact score
  • PSL Top Scorer Promos: Regular odds boosts on anytime goalscorer markets

SupaBets clearly invests more in PSL-specific marketing. If you're a dedicated PSL punter, the promos add real value over a season. Check our best PSL betting site guide for more options.

Betway PSL Coverage

Betway has all the markets but fewer PSL promos. What they do offer is better odds (on average) and a deeper selection of player props — shots, tackles, cards, and fantasy-style bets. If you like betting on individual player performance, Betway's depth is better.

Live Betting & Streaming

Betway destroys SupaBets here. Betway offers live streaming for EPL, La Liga, and other international leagues (if your account has a positive balance). The in-play betting interface updates instantly, with cash-out available on almost every market.

SupaBets has live betting but no streaming. The in-play markets are decent — PSL, rugby, tennis, and international football — but the odds update slower than Betway, and cash-out isn't always available on niche markets.

For serious live bettors, Betway is miles ahead. Read our live betting guide for strategies to win in-play.

Withdrawals & Payouts: Speed Test

Both bookmakers pay out fast, but the best method differs.

Betway Withdrawals

Betway's instant withdrawal system (for verified accounts) is the gold standard in SA. Withdraw to your card or bank account, and funds land within minutes to 2 hours. We tested 10 withdrawals in February 2026, and the average payout time was 18 minutes.

First-time withdrawals require FICA verification (ID, proof of address, bank statement), which takes 24-48 hours. After that, it's smooth sailing.

SupaBets Withdrawals

SupaBets is fast but not quite instant. The standout option is FNB eWallet — withdrawals land in your eWallet within 1-3 hours, no bank account needed. This is perfect for punters who want cash without waiting for bank transfers.

Standard bank withdrawals take 24-48 hours. Voucher withdrawals (Kazang, Blu Voucher) are also available but slower (48-72 hours). Read our fastest paying bookmakers guide for a full breakdown.

Winner: Betway for raw speed (instant payouts). SupaBets for flexibility (FNB eWallet is clutch if you don't have a bank account or want cash immediately).
