FAQ

How long do Betway withdrawals take?

It depends on FICA, bank processing and account checks. Verified accounts are usually smoother than accounts trying to withdraw before documents are accepted.

Does FICA affect Betway withdrawals?

Yes. South African bookmakers need identity checks before withdrawals can be processed properly.

Should I accept a bonus before withdrawing?

Only if you understand the wagering terms. Bonus restrictions can delay or block withdrawals until the terms are met.
