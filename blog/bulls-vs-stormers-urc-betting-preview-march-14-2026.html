Bulls vs Stormers URC Betting Preview: March 14, 2026

The Vodacom Bulls host the DHL Stormers at Loftus Versfeld on Saturday, March 14 in what's shaping up to be one of the most important SA derbies of the URC season. With both teams fighting for playoff spots and just six points separating them on the table, the loser's top-eight hopes take a serious hit.

URC Standings Context

Going into this fixture, the Stormers sit 5th on the URC log with 36 points, while the Bulls are in 8th with 30 points. That three-place gap doesn't tell the full story though — the top eight is incredibly tight this season, and a bonus-point win for either side could shuffle the entire picture.

The Stormers need to stay in the top eight to keep their playoff hopes alive, while the Bulls are looking to build momentum after putting together back-to-back wins against fellow South African opposition. A loss at Loftus for the Bulls, though, and they could find themselves in serious trouble with the business end of the season approaching.

Recent Form: Who's Coming in Hotter?

Vodacom Bulls — Building Momentum

The Bulls have found something in the last couple of weeks. After a patchy start to 2026, they've put together consecutive wins against SA teams, and Loftus is starting to feel like a fortress again. Their forward pack has been dominant, the set piece is functioning, and they've been clinical when opportunities arise.

Key talking points:

  • Back-to-back wins building confidence at home
  • Scrum has been a weapon — they'll target the Stormers here
  • Lineout drive functioning well, which is crucial at altitude
  • Loftus Versfeld factor — the altitude and the crowd make it a tough place to visit

DHL Stormers — Searching for Answers

The Stormers are in a bit of a rut. Consecutive losses to the Sharks and Lions have exposed issues in attack and defence that coach John Dobson needs to sort out quickly. Their scrum has been under pressure, and the backline hasn't been able to create the kind of free-flowing rugby they're known for.

Key concerns:

  • Back-to-back defeats have dented confidence
  • Scrum under scrutiny — the Bulls pack will test them relentlessly
  • Attack has misfired, struggling to convert territory into points
  • Defence has been shaky, especially under the high ball

⚠️ Form warning: The Stormers haven't won at Loftus in their last three URC visits. The combination of altitude, a hostile crowd, and a Bulls pack firing on all cylinders makes this a difficult trip.

Head-to-Head: What History Tells Us

The Bulls vs Stormers rivalry is one of the great fixtures in South African rugby. In URC history, the home team has generally had the advantage — and that trend has been particularly strong at Loftus Versfeld, where the altitude gives the Bulls a genuine edge.

In their last meeting at Cape Town earlier this season, the Stormers edged a tight contest. But Loftus is a different beast entirely. The thin Highveld air saps visiting packs, and the Bulls' forward-oriented game plan is perfectly suited to home conditions.

The reverse fixture this season was tight — expect more of the same here.

Betting Markets & Analysis

Match Result

The Bulls are narrow favourites at home, and the market reflects the tight nature of this contest. You'll typically find the Bulls somewhere around 1.65–1.80 and the Stormers at 2.10–2.30, depending on your bookmaker. The draw is priced around 18.00–22.00.

Given the Bulls' home form and the Stormers' recent struggles, the home side looks fair value here. But don't sleep on the Stormers — they're a better squad than their recent results suggest, and they tend to respond well under pressure in big games.

Total Points (Over/Under)

SA derbies in the URC have been trending towards tighter, lower-scoring affairs this season. The over/under line will likely sit around 42.5–45.5 points. Given both teams' forward-heavy approaches and the Stormers' recent attacking struggles, the under looks tempting.

That said, the Bulls at Loftus have been capable of putting 30+ on teams when their maul gets going. If the Stormers can't contain the driving maul, the over could come into play purely off Bulls dominance.

Try Scorer Markets

Watch for Bulls forwards in the try scorer markets. When the lineout drive is working at Loftus, the hooker and flankers often benefit. If you can get decent odds on a Bulls forward scoring the first try, that's worth considering.

Handicap Betting

The spread will likely be set around Bulls -3.5 to -5.5. This is a derby, so margins are typically tight. The Stormers won't go down without a fight, but the Bulls' home advantage and momentum tilt the scales.
