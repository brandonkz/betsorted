Easybet vs 10bet South Africa 2026: The Budget Bookmaker Showdown

Published March 7, 2026 • 9 min read • Brandon Katz

Easybet and 10bet occupy a similar space in SA betting — they're the mid-tier bookmakers that compete on accessibility, bonuses, and mobile experience rather than trying to out-muscle Betway or Hollywoodbets on brand recognition. But they're different bookmakers built on different strengths. Easybet leans into affordability and data-free betting. 10bet brings international sportsbook depth to a local audience. Which one's right for you depends entirely on how you bet. Let's break it down.

Odds Comparison: Are Either Any Good?

Neither Easybet nor 10bet will consistently beat Betway or Sportingbet on odds. Let's be honest about that upfront. But within the mid-tier bookmaker bracket, both offer reasonable value — and sometimes surprise you.

10bet tends to have slightly tighter margins, especially on international football. On a typical Premier League match, 10bet's margin sits around 5-6%, compared to Easybet's 6-7%. That's a meaningful difference if you're placing multiple bets per week. Over a season of EPL betting, the punter using 10bet keeps more money in their pocket.

For PSL matches, the gap is smaller. Both bookmakers price Betway Premiership fixtures competitively, and on marquee games — the Soweto Derby, Sundowns vs Pirates, any Nedbank Cup semi-final — prices are comparable. Easybet occasionally offers enhanced odds on SA-specific events as promotional boosts, which can tip the value their way on individual bets.

On rugby and cricket, 10bet has the edge. Their international sportsbook DNA shows — Springbok tests, URC matches, and Proteas internationals are all priced more aggressively than Easybet's equivalents.

Winner: 10bet — marginally better across the board, especially on international sport.

Mobile Experience & Data-Free Betting

This is where Easybet has a genuinely unique selling point that no other SA bookmaker matches.

Easybet's data-free betting lets you place bets, check results, and manage your account without using your mobile data. In South Africa — where data costs remain among the highest in the world relative to income — this isn't a gimmick. It's a genuine cost saver for punters who bet frequently from their phones. If you're spending R50-R100 per month on data just to place bets, data-free betting pays for itself immediately.

Both bookmakers offer Android and iOS apps, which puts them ahead of some SA bookmakers that still don't have iOS apps (looking at you, several major brands). Easybet's app is functional and straightforward — not the prettiest interface, but it gets the job done quickly. 10bet's app is more polished, with a cleaner design, better navigation between sports, and a more intuitive bet slip.

For live betting on mobile, 10bet wins. Their app handles live markets smoothly, with quick odds updates and the ability to build multi-bets from live events without lag. Easybet's live betting works but can feel slower on lower-end devices.

Winner: Easybet on accessibility (data-free betting is huge). 10bet on app quality and live betting experience.

Welcome Bonuses & Promotions

Both bookmakers offer R1,000 welcome bonuses, but the structure differs:

Easybet gives you up to R1,000 in free bets. Deposit, place qualifying bets at minimum odds, and you receive free bet credits. The wagering requirements are on the lower side for SA bookmakers, and free bets can be used across sports and Lucky Numbers. Easybet also runs regular "Bet and Get" promotions — bet a certain amount on a weekend's fixtures and receive free bets for the following week.

10bet offers up to R1,000 as a matched deposit bonus. Deposit R1,000, get R1,000 in bonus funds. Wagering requirements are moderate — you'll need to bet the bonus amount several times at minimum odds of 1.50 or higher before withdrawing. 10bet's ongoing promotions are competitive, including accumulator boosts (extra percentage added to multi-bet payouts), enhanced odds on selected events, and regular casino reload bonuses.

The ongoing promotions are more important than the welcome offer — you claim the bonus once, but promotions come every week. 10bet's accumulator boost is particularly valuable if you regularly build multi-bets, which is SA punters' favourite pastime.

Winner: Draw — similar welcome offers, different ongoing strengths. Easybet for free bet frequency, 10bet for accumulator boosts.

Lucky Numbers

South African punters love Lucky Numbers, and both bookmakers know it.

Easybet has built a strong Lucky Numbers offering. You can bet on South African lotteries (Lotto, PowerBall, Daily Lotto), international draws (UK 49s, Spain La Primitiva, Ghana, Nigeria), and special draws. The interface makes it easy to pick numbers, place combos, and check results. Minimum stakes start from R1 on some Lucky Numbers markets.

10bet also offers Lucky Numbers but it's less central to their platform. The coverage is there — major SA and international draws — but the interface and promotion around Lucky Numbers feels secondary to their sportsbook focus.

If Lucky Numbers are a significant part of your betting, Easybet treats it as a first-class product. 10bet treats it as an add-on.

Winner: Easybet — better Lucky Numbers experience and more prominence on the platform.

Sports Coverage & Market Depth

10bet's international heritage shows in their sportsbook depth. They cover:

  • Football: PSL, NFD, EPL, La Liga, Serie A, Bundesliga, Champions League, Europa League, plus South American, Asian, and smaller European leagues
  • Rugby: URC, Super Rugby, Six Nations, Rugby Championship, Springbok tests, Currie Cup
  • Cricket: Proteas internationals, IPL, BBL, The Hundred, CSA T20 Challenge
  • Tennis: Grand Slams, Masters 1000, ATP 500/250, WTA
  • Other: UFC/MMA, boxing, horse racing, darts, eSports, volleyball

Easybet covers the same core sports but with fewer niche markets per event. On a PSL match, Easybet might offer 20-30 markets while 10bet offers 40-50. On a Champions League match, the gap widens further. If you bet primarily on match result, over/under, and BTTS, you won't notice the difference. If you like building multi-bets from player props and corner markets, 10bet gives you more to work with.

Winner: 10bet — wider and deeper sportsbook.

FAQ

Is Easybet or 10bet better for PSL betting?

10bet offers more PSL markets per match and slightly better odds on Betway Premiership fixtures. Easybet covers all the basics (match result, over/under, BTTS) and sometimes runs enhanced odds on big derbies. For casual PSL betting, either works. For building multi-bets from PSL player props, 10bet.

Which has a better app — Easybet or 10bet?

Both offer Android and iOS apps. 10bet's app is more polished with a cleaner interface and better live betting. Easybet's advantage is data-free betting — you can place bets without using mobile data. If data costs matter, Easybet wins. If UX matters more, 10bet.

Does Easybet offer data-free betting?

Yes. Easybet's data-free feature lets you browse, place bets, and manage your account without using mobile data. This works through their app and is a significant cost saver for SA punters. 10bet does not offer this feature.

Can I bet with R1 on Easybet or 10bet?

Easybet allows minimum bets as low as R1 on selected markets including Lucky Numbers. 10bet's minimum bet is R2. Both bookmakers are among the most accessible in South Africa for small-stakes punters.

Are Easybet and 10bet legal in South Africa?

Yes. Both hold licences from the Mpumalanga gambling authority and operate legally under the National Gambling Act. Both are FICA-compliant and require identity verification for withdrawals, same as all regulated SA bookmakers.
