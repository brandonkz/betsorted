Why Do Withdrawals Take So Long?

If you're wondering why most bookmakers take 24–72 hours to process withdrawals when banks can do it instantly, here's the reality:

  1. Manual verification: Bookmakers manually review withdrawal requests to prevent fraud and ensure compliance with anti-money laundering regulations.
  2. FICA compliance: South African law requires bookmakers to verify your identity before processing withdrawals. If your documents aren't uploaded or approved, your withdrawal gets delayed.
  3. Batch processing: Most bookmakers process withdrawals in batches (once or twice per day) to reduce banking costs. Betway and Sunbet process in real-time, which is why they're faster.
  4. Bank processing time: Even after the bookmaker approves your withdrawal, your bank might take 24 hours to credit your account (especially on weekends).

Pro tip: Complete your FICA verification before you place your first bet. Upload your ID and proof of address as soon as you create your account. This way, your first withdrawal won't be delayed by document verification.

How to Speed Up Your Withdrawals

Want to get paid faster? Here's how to minimize withdrawal delays:

1. Complete FICA Verification Immediately

Don't wait until you win to upload your documents. Do it when you sign up. Most bookmakers verify FICA within 24–48 hours, and you only need to do it once.

Required documents:

  • Copy of your South African ID or passport
  • Proof of address (utility bill, bank statement, lease agreement — must be under 3 months old)
  • Proof of payment method (bank statement showing your name and account number)

2. Use the Same Payment Method for Deposits and Withdrawals

Bookmakers require you to withdraw to the same payment method you used to deposit. If you deposited via EFT, you must withdraw via EFT to the same bank account. This is an anti-money laundering rule, not a choice.

If you deposit using multiple methods (e.g., card + EFT), your withdrawal might be split across both methods, which can slow things down.

3. Avoid Weekends and Public Holidays

Most South African banks don't process EFT transfers on weekends or public holidays. If you request a withdrawal on Friday afternoon, you probably won't see your money until Monday or Tuesday.

Best time to request a withdrawal: Monday to Thursday morning. This ensures your withdrawal is processed on the same business day.

4. Request Withdrawals During Business Hours

Some bookmakers only process withdrawals during business hours (9am–5pm). If you request a withdrawal at midnight, it won't be processed until the next morning.

5. Stick to EFT (Avoid Third-Party Payment Processors)

EFT (Electronic Funds Transfer) is the fastest and most reliable withdrawal method in South Africa. Avoid third-party processors like e-wallets or voucher systems — they add extra steps and processing time.

Most South African bookmakers have minimum and maximum withdrawal limits:

If you win big (congrats!), you might need to split your withdrawal across multiple transactions. Betway allows R1 million per withdrawal, which is the highest in SA.

What If My Withdrawal is Delayed?

If your withdrawal is taking longer than expected, here's what to check:

  1. Is your FICA approved? Check your account settings. If your documents are still pending, your withdrawal will be on hold.
  2. Did you meet the wagering requirements? If you claimed a bonus, you need to meet the rollover requirements before you can withdraw. Check the bonus terms.
  3. Is it a weekend or public holiday? Banks don't process EFT on weekends. Your withdrawal will go through on the next business day.
  4. Did you request a large withdrawal? Withdrawals over R50,000 sometimes trigger additional security checks. Contact customer support.

If everything checks out and your withdrawal is still delayed, contact the bookmaker's customer support. Most issues are resolved within 24 hours.
