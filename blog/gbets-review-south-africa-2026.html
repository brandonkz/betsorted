Is Gbets Legal in South Africa?

Yes. Gbets is part of the Goldrush Group, one of South Africa's largest and oldest gambling operators. They've been running physical betting shops and casinos across SA for years before launching the online platform. The licence is held through the Mpumalanga Economic Regulator.

This isn't some offshore operation — Goldrush is a properly established South African company with physical presence. That matters because if something goes wrong with your account, there's actual recourse through SA gambling regulators.

Sportsbook: What Can You Bet On?

Gbets covers the sports that matter to SA punters, plus a few surprises. Here's the breakdown:

Football (Soccer)

Full PSL coverage is the baseline, and Gbets delivers. Match winner, correct score, both teams to score, over/under, first goalscorer, and same game multis. The depth per match isn't quite Betway levels, but it covers everything most punters want.

European football coverage is solid — EPL, La Liga, Serie A, Bundesliga, Champions League, and even some second-division leagues. If you follow the PSL and EPL (as most SA punters do), you won't feel limited.

Rugby

URC matches get good coverage with match winner, handicaps, total points, try scorer, and halftime/fulltime. Currie Cup coverage is decent during the season. For international rugby — Springboks Tests, Nations Championship, British & Irish Lions tours — Gbets typically has competitive markets.

Where Gbets could improve: deeper rugby prop markets. Betway sometimes offers more niche rugby bets (penalty count, first scoring method, exact tries) that Gbets misses.

Cricket

SA20, Proteas internationals, IPL, Big Bash, and international Tests. In-play cricket markets are available with live odds updates. Match winner, top batsman, top bowler, total runs, and method of dismissal are all covered.

Esports — Gbets' Hidden Strength

This is where Gbets genuinely surprises. Their esports coverage rivals specialised betting sites:

  • Counter-Strike 2: Deep market coverage including map winner, total rounds, pistol round winner
  • League of Legends: Match winner, first blood, total kills, map handicaps
  • Dota 2: Full tournament coverage with live betting
  • Valorant, PUBG, FIFA: Regular markets for major tournaments

If you're a gamer who also bets, Gbets is arguably the best SA-licensed option for esports. Betway has improved in this area, but Gbets was early to the party and it shows.

Lucky Numbers

Lucky Numbers (lotto betting) is massive in South Africa, and Gbets has one of the better Lucky Numbers platforms. You can bet on local draws (SA Lotto, PowerBall) and international draws (UK 49s, Irish Lotto, French Lotto). Payouts on Lucky Numbers are competitive — Gbets typically offers slightly better odds on some numbers compared to Hollywoodbets.

For a deep dive on which bookmaker is best for Lucky Numbers, check our Lucky Numbers comparison guide.

Other Sports

Horse racing (SA meetings and international), tennis, basketball (NBA), boxing, MMA, motor sport, golf, table tennis, and darts. The coverage is comprehensive without being bloated with obscure markets nobody uses.

Live Betting

Gbets offers live betting across football, rugby, cricket, tennis, basketball, and esports. The interface is clean — odds update at a reasonable pace and you can see the match timeline and key stats (goals, cards, corners for football).

Cash-out is available on selected live bets, including partial cash-out. The cash-out values are generally fair — we didn't notice significant margin increases compared to pre-match pricing.

No live streaming. This is the same gap as most SA bookmakers outside Betway and Hollywoodbets. You'll need to watch on a second screen. For a bookmaker pushing esports so heavily, adding streaming for CS2 and LoL tournaments would be a natural fit — hopefully that's on the roadmap.

The mobile experience is honestly one of Gbets' strengths. The site loads quickly even on slower data connections, navigation is intuitive, and all features (live betting, cash-out, deposits, Lucky Numbers) work seamlessly on mobile.
