Odds Comparison — Who Gives Better Prices?

We checked odds on the same events across both bookmakers. Here's what we found:

PSL Football

For a typical PSL match, the odds difference between Gbets and Easybet is small — usually within 0.05 of each other. Gbets tends to be slightly more competitive on the match result market, especially for the bigger derbies. Easybet's odds on over/under goals are comparable.

Edge: Gbets — marginally better odds, and more markets per match (correct score, BTTS, half-time/full-time). Easybet covers the basics but doesn't go as deep.

Rugby (URC, Test Matches)

This is where Gbets pulls ahead more clearly. They offer handicap markets, try scorer, and more exotic props for URC and Springbok matches. Easybet's rugby coverage is basic — match result, handicap, and over/under points.

Edge: Gbets — wider rugby markets and generally better prices on handicaps. If you're a rugby bettor, Gbets is the obvious choice between these two.

Lucky Numbers

Here's where Easybet fights back. They offer a wider range of international Lucky Numbers draws — more lotteries from around the world, and slightly higher payouts on some outcomes. If Lucky Numbers is your thing, Easybet has the edge.

Edge: Easybet — more draws, better Lucky Numbers payouts. Gbets has Lucky Numbers but it's not their focus.

Mobile Experience — Apps and USSD

Gbets Mobile

Gbets has an Android APK (no Play Store listing — you download from their website). The app is functional and reasonably quick. The mobile site works on any browser. Navigation is decent but could be cleaner — finding specific markets sometimes takes a few taps too many.

Easybet Mobile

Easybet also offers an Android APK, plus a mobile-optimised website. The interface is simpler, which is actually an advantage for casual bettors — fewer menus, faster to find what you need. The big differentiator: Easybet supports USSD betting. If you're in an area with limited data or using a feature phone, you can dial in and place bets without any internet. Gbets doesn't offer this.

Edge: Easybet for accessibility (USSD is a game-changer for many SA punters). Gbets for features and depth.
