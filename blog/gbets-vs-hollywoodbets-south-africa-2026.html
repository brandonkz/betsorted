South African Bookmaker Showdown

Gbets ⚡ Hollywoodbets

Two homegrown favourites, one honest verdict

This is one of the most-requested comparisons we've had. Gbets and Hollywoodbets are both proudly South African bookmakers — no international parent company, no offshore headquarters. They're built for local punters, they understand the SA market, and they compete fiercely for your business.

But they're very different products. Hollywoodbets has the brand recognition, the retail shops, and the massive marketing budget. Gbets has quietly become one of the sharpest bookmakers in the country, with competitive odds and a polished platform that punches well above its weight.

We've tested both extensively — depositing real money, placing bets across PSL, rugby, cricket, and Lucky Numbers, and withdrawing funds. Here's the unfiltered comparison.

Welcome Bonus DRAW

Both bookmakers offer R50 to new customers, which makes this category a dead heat. The structure differs slightly though.

Gbets gives you a R50 free bet just for signing up and verifying your account — no deposit required. You need to wager the free bet amount once on odds of 5/10 (1.50) or higher before you can withdraw winnings. Simple, clean, no catch.

Hollywoodbets also offers a R50 sign-up bonus, and they occasionally run top-up bonuses for first deposits. The wagering requirements are similar — single turnover at minimum odds.

Neither bookmaker tries to hook you with an inflated "deposit R1,000 get R1,000" offer with impossible rollover requirements. Both are honest about what you're getting. We respect that.

BetSorted take: R50 free bets are modest but realistic. Neither bookmaker is trying to trick you with unachievable wagering. Both are fair — call it a draw.

Odds Quality GBETS WINS

This is where Gbets consistently surprises people. For a smaller bookmaker, their odds are genuinely competitive — often matching or beating the bigger names on popular markets.

We've compared odds across dozens of PSL, URC, and IPL matches over the past few months. The pattern is clear:

  • PSL soccer: Gbets offers marginally better odds on match winners in about 60% of games we checked. The margins are small — maybe 1.83 vs 1.80 on a home win — but over hundreds of bets, it adds up.
  • Rugby (URC/Springboks): Both are competitive, but Gbets' handicap lines tend to offer slightly better value.
  • Cricket (IPL/Proteas): Gbets has noticeably strong cricket odds. If you're betting the IPL 2026 season, check Gbets first for match winner markets.
  • Lucky Numbers: Hollywoodbets has better Lucky Numbers payouts on most draws. If Lucky Numbers is your thing, Hollywood is the pick.

Hollywoodbets' odds aren't bad — they're middle-of-the-pack for SA bookmakers. But Gbets consistently offers slightly better prices on mainstream sports markets. If you're a volume bettor, that 2-3% edge on odds matters enormously over a season.

Lucky Numbers HOLLYWOODBETS WINS

Lucky Numbers is a massive market in South Africa, and Hollywoodbets basically owns this category. They offer draws from around the world — South Africa, UK, Ireland, Hong Kong, Greece, Italy, and more — with good payouts and a slick interface for picking numbers.

Gbets has Lucky Numbers too, but the range of international draws is smaller, and Hollywoodbets' payouts tend to be slightly better on most draws. If you play Lucky Numbers regularly, Hollywoodbets is the obvious choice.

Mobile App & Usability HOLLYWOODBETS WINS

Hollywoodbets wins this category for one specific reason: their data-free app.

In a country where mobile data is expensive, being able to browse markets and place bets without using data is a genuine competitive advantage. Hollywoodbets' data-free app (available on Android) lets you do exactly that. They also support USSD betting (*120*069#) for feature phones — brilliant for reaching punters across all income levels.

The Hollywoodbets app itself is clean, fast, and well-designed. Navigation is intuitive, bet placement is quick, and live betting works smoothly.

Gbets' mobile experience is also good — their mobile site is responsive and fast, and they have an Android app that works well. But no data-free option. If you're on a tight data budget or in an area with inconsistent signal, Hollywoodbets' accessibility is unmatched.

For desktop users, both platforms work well. Gbets actually has a slightly cleaner desktop interface — less cluttered, easier to find specific markets.

Deposits & Withdrawals GBETS WINS

Both bookmakers accept the standard SA payment methods — bank transfers, Ozow, Capitec Pay, 1VOUCHER, OTT vouchers, and credit/debit cards. No issues on the deposit side for either.

Where Gbets pulls ahead is withdrawal speed. In our testing:

  • Gbets: Withdrawals to FNB processed in under 2 hours. Standard Bank took about 3–4 hours. Consistently fast.
  • Hollywoodbets: More variable. Some withdrawals landed in 2 hours, others took closer to 24 hours. The average was about 6–8 hours in our experience.

Neither bookmaker gave us problems — no declined withdrawals, no verification delays beyond the initial account setup. But Gbets' consistency on speed gives them the edge.

Live Betting DRAW

Both bookmakers offer live (in-play) betting with decent market coverage. Neither is at the level of Betway for live betting depth, but both handle the basics well.

  • Gbets live: Smooth interface, reasonable market updates, cash-out available on popular events. Cricket live markets are particularly good.
  • Hollywoodbets live: Clean design, decent selection, cash-out available. Their live PSL coverage is solid with next goal, corners, and cards markets.

If live betting is your main focus, we'd actually recommend reading our live betting comparison — Betway leads this category. But between just Gbets and Hollywoodbets, it's a fair draw.

Casino & Slots HOLLYWOODBETS WINS

Hollywoodbets' Spina Zonke is arguably the most popular online slots product in South Africa. The name is catchy ("Spin Everything" in Zulu), the game selection is massive, and they've integrated it beautifully into the main platform. Add live dealer games and virtual sports, and Hollywood has a complete casino package.

Gbets has casino games too — slots, table games, and live dealer — but the selection is smaller and the brand doesn't have the same "slots destination" reputation that Hollywood has built.

If you're primarily a sports bettor who plays the occasional slot, both are fine. If casino is a big part of your betting, Hollywoodbets is the better product.

Customer Support DRAW

Both bookmakers offer multiple support channels:

  • Gbets: Live chat (responsive, usually under 5 minutes), email, phone, WhatsApp
  • Hollywoodbets: Live chat, email, phone, social media (very active on Twitter/X), plus in-store support at retail branches

Hollywoodbets' advantage is physical presence — if you have a branch nearby, walking in and speaking to someone is always an option. Gbets' live chat is consistently quick and helpful though. Neither left us hanging.

Responsible Gambling

Both bookmakers are licensed by South African gambling boards and offer deposit limits, self-exclusion, and cooling-off periods. Hollywoodbets has invested more publicly in responsible gambling initiatives, which we think matters.

Frequently Asked Questions

Is Gbets or Hollywoodbets safer?

Both are licensed by South African gambling boards, so both are fully regulated and legal. Your money is protected under SA gambling law with either bookmaker.

Which has better PSL odds?

Gbets generally offers marginally better PSL odds on match winner and over/under markets. The difference is small per bet but adds up over a season.

Can I use both at the same time?

Absolutely — and we recommend it. Having accounts at multiple bookmakers lets you compare odds and always take the best price. This is called "line shopping" and it's standard practice among profitable bettors.

Which pays out faster?

Gbets, in our experience. Bank transfer withdrawals from Gbets typically land within 1–4 hours. Hollywoodbets can be faster on good days but averages 6–8 hours.

Which is better for beginners?

Hollywoodbets is slightly more beginner-friendly thanks to the data-free app, retail shops for in-person help, and a cleaner mobile interface. But Gbets isn't complicated either — both are accessible for new punters. See our best betting sites for beginners guide.
