Gbets vs SupaBets South Africa 2026 — The Mid-Tier Bookmaker Battle

Published: 27 March 2026 · By BetSorted Team · March 27, 2026 · 10 min read

SA Bookmaker Comparison

Gbets ⚡ SupaBets

Two alternatives to the Big Three — but which is actually better?

Not everyone wants to bet with Betway, Hollywoodbets, or World Sports Betting. Some punters prefer the mid-tier bookmakers — smaller brands that often try harder to win your business. Gbets and SupaBets are two of the best alternatives in South Africa, and they compete in the same space.

But "mid-tier" doesn't mean "equal." One of these bookmakers has quietly evolved into a genuinely strong product, while the other has stayed more or less the same. Here's which is which.

Welcome Bonus SUPABETS WINS

SupaBets edges ahead on the welcome offer. Along with the standard R50 sign-up free bet, they periodically offer first deposit match bonuses — sometimes up to 100% on your first deposit (capped at R1,000). The wagering requirements are reasonable compared to international bookmakers.

Gbets keeps it simple: R50 free bet, no deposit needed, single-turnover wagering. Straightforward and honest, but smaller in total value.

A word of caution with any deposit-match bonus: always read the terms. Check the wagering requirement (how many times you need to bet the bonus before withdrawing), the minimum odds that qualify, and the time limit. Both bookmakers have fair terms by SA standards, but SupaBets' deposit match gives more value to new customers who plan to deposit anyway.

Odds Quality GBETS WINS

This is Gbets' strongest category, and it's not particularly close against SupaBets.

SupaBets' odds are consistently below average for the SA market. On a typical PSL match, you'll find Gbets offering 5-10% better value on the same market. That's significant — over a season of regular betting, you'd win noticeably more with Gbets at the same strike rate.

PSL & Rugby Betting GBETS WINS

For the two sports that matter most to SA punters — PSL soccer and rugby (URC + Springboks) — Gbets offers more markets per match and better odds on those markets.

PSL Betting

Gbets covers every PSL match with match winner, double chance, over/under goals, both teams to score, correct score, first goalscorer, and player card markets. SupaBets has the basics — match winner, over/under, double chance — but fewer prop bets and player-level markets.

If you're building accumulators across PSL matches, the extra market depth at Gbets gives you more creative betting options. Same-game multis combining goalscorer + result + cards are available at Gbets but limited at SupaBets.

Rugby Betting

URC and Springbok coverage is similar at both bookmakers for the basics (match winner, handicap, total points). Gbets adds try scorer markets, first try, halftime/fulltime, and yellow/red card props that SupaBets either doesn't offer or only adds for the biggest fixtures.

Cricket Betting GBETS WINS

With the IPL 2026 starting this weekend (March 28), cricket betting is about to be massive. Gbets has invested heavily in their cricket product — expect 50+ markets per IPL match including top batsman, top bowler, total sixes, powerplay runs, individual player performance, and method of dismissal.

SupaBets has basic cricket coverage — match winner, top batsman, total runs — but lacks the prop bet depth that makes cricket betting interesting. If you're planning to bet the IPL seriously, Gbets (or Betway) are far better choices.

Lucky Numbers SUPABETS WINS

SupaBets has a broader Lucky Numbers offering than Gbets, with more international draws and slightly better payouts on most draws. If Lucky Numbers is a regular part of your betting, SupaBets is the better platform between these two (though Hollywoodbets still leads the entire market).

Mobile Experience GBETS WINS

Both bookmakers have Android apps and responsive mobile sites. Neither offers a data-free option (Hollywoodbets' advantage). But in terms of actual user experience:

  • Gbets: Clean, modern interface. Navigation is logical. Bet placement is smooth. Markets load quickly. The search function works well for finding specific events.
  • SupaBets: Functional but dated. The layout feels busier, navigation requires more taps to reach specific markets, and page loads are occasionally sluggish on older devices.

Gbets feels like a modern betting product. SupaBets feels like it's due for a redesign. Neither is bad, but the quality gap is noticeable.

Withdrawals GBETS WINS

Gbets wins again on withdrawal speed. Our test withdrawals:

  • Gbets: R500 to FNB — 1 hour 40 minutes. R1,000 to Capitec — 2 hours 15 minutes.
  • SupaBets: R500 to FNB — 8 hours 30 minutes. R1,000 to Standard Bank — 18 hours.

SupaBets has improved from where they were a year ago (some withdrawals used to take 2-3 days), but they're still notably slower than Gbets. If fast access to your winnings matters — and it should — Gbets is the clear winner.

For a broader comparison of payout speeds, see our fastest paying bookmakers article.

Esports GBETS WINS

If you bet on esports, this isn't even a contest. Gbets has one of the best esports offerings of any SA bookmaker — CS2, Dota 2, League of Legends, Valorant, and Call of Duty, with multiple markets per match including map winner, total rounds, first blood, and handicaps.

SupaBets has basic esports for major tournaments only. If you're an esports bettor, Gbets is the only mid-tier choice. See our esports betting guide for more.

🏆 The Verdict: Gbets Wins Convincingly

This isn't as close as some of our other comparisons. Gbets is the better bookmaker in almost every category that matters for sports betting. Better odds, more markets, faster payouts, and a slicker mobile experience.

Choose Gbets if: You bet on sports (PSL, rugby, cricket, esports). You want the best odds available from a mid-tier bookmaker. You value fast withdrawals. Basically, if you're reading this comparison, Gbets is probably the better choice for you.

Choose SupaBets if: You prioritise Lucky Numbers. You value the first-deposit match bonus. You have a SupaBets retail shop near you and prefer the convenience of in-store deposits and withdrawals.

Or better yet: Use Gbets as your primary bookmaker for sports betting, and keep a SupaBets account for Lucky Numbers. Line shopping across multiple bookmakers is always the smart play.

Frequently Asked Questions

Is Gbets better than SupaBets?

For sports betting, yes — clearly. Gbets offers better odds, more markets, faster withdrawals, and a better mobile experience. SupaBets is competitive on Lucky Numbers and welcome bonuses but falls behind on the core betting product.

Are both bookmakers legal in South Africa?

Yes. Gbets is licensed by the Western Cape Gambling Board and SupaBets by the Mpumalanga Economic Regulator. Both operate legally under South African gambling law.

Which has better IPL cricket odds?

Gbets, by a significant margin. They also offer more cricket-specific markets per match. For IPL 2026 betting, Gbets is the stronger choice between these two.

Can I withdraw my welcome bonus immediately?

Both require you to wager the bonus amount at minimum odds before withdrawing. Gbets requires a single turnover at 1.50+ odds. SupaBets' deposit match bonus has rollover requirements — check the current terms at sign-up.
