Hollywoodbets vs 10bet: Which SA Bookmaker is Better in 2026?

Published February 23, 2026 • 8 min read • Brandon K

Hollywoodbets and 10bet represent two very different approaches to the South African betting market. Hollywoodbets is the local champion — proudly SA, built on horse racing, with branches across the country. 10bet is the international operator — slick, odds-focused, and quietly building a serious presence in South Africa.

Both are licensed, both are legitimate, and both want your business. So which one actually delivers? We've compared them across every category that matters to SA bettors.

Odds Quality: Where Your Money Actually Goes

Let's start with what matters most — the odds. Because the bookmaker with the best odds is the one paying you the most when you win.

10bet's international heritage shows in their odds quality. They operate in dozens of countries, which means their trading team is sophisticated and their margins are tight. For Premier League football, Champions League, tennis Grand Slams, and other international events, 10bet's prices are consistently 2-4% better than Hollywoodbets. Over time, that margin is the difference between a profitable year and a break-even one.

For PSL football, the picture changes. Hollywoodbets has invested heavily in South African sport markets, and their PSL odds are sharp. On a Kaizer Chiefs vs Mamelodi Sundowns match, Hollywoodbets will often match or beat 10bet's price. Hollywoodbets knows the local market better, and it shows in their pricing.

Rugby follows a similar pattern. Springbok matches and the Rugby Championship — 10bet edges it. URC matches with the Bulls, Sharks, and Stormers — Hollywoodbets is competitive or better.

Our take: If you bet across international and local sport, having accounts with both makes sense. Check the odds comparison page to see who's paying more on any given match.

Bonuses: R25 Free vs Up to R3,000

On paper, 10bet's welcome offer looks far more generous — up to R3,000 versus Hollywoodbets' R25 no-deposit bonus. But as always with bonuses, the details matter.

10bet's R3,000 is a matched deposit bonus. You need to deposit money, and then the bonus is credited in stages with wagering requirements attached. You'll need to turn the bonus over multiple times on qualifying bets before you can withdraw anything. It's not R3,000 in your pocket — it's R3,000 in potential value if you bet enough at the right odds.

Hollywoodbets' R25 is simple: sign up, get R25, bet with it. There are wagering requirements, but it's genuinely risk-free. No deposit needed. For someone new to betting who just wants to test the waters, that's genuinely useful.

Beyond the welcome bonus, both bookmakers run regular promotions. 10bet has weekly enhanced odds specials and cashback offers. Hollywoodbets runs promotions tied to PSL matches and horse racing carnivals.

Winner: 10bet on raw value for serious bettors. Hollywoodbets for beginners who want zero risk.

Sports Coverage: Local vs Global

This is where the two bookmakers diverge most clearly.

10bet covers an enormous range of international sports. Beyond the obvious football and rugby, you'll find deep markets for tennis, basketball (NBA and EuroLeague), cricket, MMA/UFC, esports, and even niche sports like handball, volleyball, and table tennis. If it's played professionally somewhere in the world, 10bet probably has a market for it.

Their market depth on major events is impressive. A Champions League match might have 150+ betting options on 10bet — corners, cards, goalscorer markets, half-time results, Asian handicaps, the lot.

Hollywoodbets covers all the major sports but shines brightest on South African events. Their PSL coverage is excellent, with pre-match and in-play markets that reflect genuine knowledge of the local game. Horse racing is where they're untouchable — Turffontein, Greyville, Kenilworth, international tracks, exotic bets, tote, fixed odds. No other SA bookmaker comes close.

Hollywoodbets also offers Lucky Numbers, Spina Zonke (slots), and a casino section that 10bet doesn't match in the South African market.

Winner: 10bet for international sport variety and market depth. Hollywoodbets for SA sport and horse racing.

Withdrawal Speed & Payment Methods

Neither bookmaker can match Betway's instant payouts (see our fastest paying bookmakers guide), but both are reasonable.

Hollywoodbets processes EFT withdrawals in 24–72 hours. The timeline depends on when you submit the request and verification status. Their big advantage is branch withdrawals — if you live near a Hollywoodbets branch (and there are hundreds across SA, especially in KwaZulu-Natal and Gauteng), you can walk in and collect cash. That's a genuine convenience no online-only bookmaker can offer.

10bet processes EFT withdrawals in 24–48 hours. Their payment processing is efficient, though being online-only means no cash option. They support all major SA payment methods: EFT, Visa, Mastercard, and Ozow.

For deposits, both are fast. Hollywoodbets accepts everything from bank transfers to vouchers to branch deposits. 10bet covers EFT, card payments, and popular e-wallets. Both have a R10 minimum deposit (Hollywoodbets) and R50 minimum (10bet).

Winner: Hollywoodbets for flexibility (branches + USSD + vouchers). 10bet for slightly faster online processing.

Mobile Experience

Both bookmakers know that most SA bettors use their phones, and both have invested accordingly.

10bet's mobile app is polished and fast. It's available on both iOS and Android, the interface is clean, and live betting works smoothly. The app mirrors the full desktop experience without feeling cramped. If you're used to international betting apps, 10bet feels familiar and professional.

Hollywoodbets has a solid Android app and a mobile-optimised website. The interface isn't as slick as 10bet's, but it gets the job done. Their killer feature is USSD betting — you can place bets by dialling a code, no app or data needed. For bettors in areas with limited connectivity or those on older phones, this is a massive advantage that 10bet simply can't match.

Winner: 10bet for app quality. Hollywoodbets for accessibility.

Live Betting

10bet has the edge here. Their live betting platform covers a wide range of events with markets updating in real time. You'll find in-play options for football, tennis, basketball, cricket, and more, with decent depth on each match.

Hollywoodbets offers live betting too, and it's improved significantly over the past year, but the range of in-play markets doesn't quite match 10bet's breadth. For PSL live betting, Hollywoodbets is perfectly fine. For a Saturday night with Champions League, NBA, and a cricket Test all happening simultaneously, 10bet gives you more to work with.

Winner: 10bet, particularly for multi-sport live betting sessions.

The Verdict

This comparison really comes down to what kind of bettor you are.

If you're a proudly South African punter who bets on the PSL, loves horse racing, and values the convenience of branch access, Hollywoodbets is hard to beat. It was built for SA bettors, and it shows in every part of their product.

If you're an odds-focused bettor who bets on international sport and wants the tightest margins available, 10bet delivers. Their odds quality is excellent, their market range is massive, and their welcome bonus is one of the best in the SA market.

The smart move? Use both. Hollywoodbets for horse racing and PSL. 10bet for Premier League, Champions League, and international cricket. Run the numbers on our betting calculator and compare prices on the odds comparison page before every bet.

FAQ

Is 10bet safe to use in South Africa?

Yes. 10bet holds a valid South African gambling licence issued by the Western Cape Gambling and Racing Board. They are legally permitted to operate in SA and are subject to the same regulations as Hollywoodbets.

Which has better odds, Hollywoodbets or 10bet?

For international sport (Premier League, Champions League, tennis), 10bet typically offers slightly better odds due to its international operation. For PSL and South African sport, Hollywoodbets is very competitive and often matches or beats 10bet's prices.

Can I use both Hollywoodbets and 10bet?

Absolutely. Many experienced South African bettors maintain accounts with multiple bookmakers to take advantage of the best odds on each market. There's no restriction on having accounts with both.
