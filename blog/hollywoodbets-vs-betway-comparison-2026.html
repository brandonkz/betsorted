Hollywoodbets vs Betway: Which Bookmaker Is Better for SA Bettors?

📅 March 10, 2026 • ⏱️ 10 min read

If you're betting in South Africa, you've heard of Hollywoodbets and Betway. They're the two biggest names in the game, and for good reason — both are licensed, reliable, and offer solid odds across a wide range of sports.

But which one is actually better?

The answer depends on what you're looking for. Hollywoodbets is the local favorite, especially for horse racing and sports betting traditionalists. Betway is the international powerhouse with slick tech, better soccer odds, and a more modern feel.

Here's how they stack up across the things that actually matter.

Odds Comparison: Who Offers Better Value?

This is the most important question. Better odds = more profit over time.

Hollywoodbets

Best for: Horse racing, rugby (URC, Currie Cup), local soccer (PSL)

Hollywoodbets dominates in horse racing. If you're betting on local tracks like Turffontein, Kenilworth, or Greyville, their odds are consistently among the best in South Africa. They also offer tote betting and exotic bets (Pick 6, Jackpot, etc.) that other bookies don't.

For rugby, Hollywoodbets is competitive. Their odds on URC and Springbok matches are solid, though Betway occasionally edges them out on major internationals.

For soccer, they're fine — but not exceptional. PSL odds are decent, but if you're betting on the EPL or Champions League, Betway usually wins.

Betway

Best for: EPL, Champions League, international soccer, live betting

Betway is the go-to for soccer fans. They consistently offer higher odds on top-tier leagues — EPL, La Liga, Serie A, Bundesliga. Their Champions League markets are also deeper, with more props and live betting options.

They're also strong in NBA, NFL, and tennis — sports where Hollywoodbets' coverage is thinner.

Where Betway falls short: horse racing. If that's your thing, stick with Hollywoodbets.

Verdict: Hollywoodbets wins for horses and local rugby. Betway wins for international soccer and live betting.

Welcome Bonuses: Which One Is Better?

Hollywoodbets

You get R25 free bet just for signing up — no deposit required. It's the easiest way to test the platform risk-free.

The catch? You need to use it on bets with minimum odds of 1.50, and you can't withdraw the R25 itself — only your winnings from it.

Betway

Betway also offers R25 free bet on signup, but they sweeten the deal with a deposit match bonus if you fund your account. Deposit R50, get an extra R50 in bonus funds (up to a certain limit).

The bonus funds come with wagering requirements (usually 1.5x turnover on odds of 1.75+), so you can't just cash out immediately. But if you're planning to bet anyway, it's free money.

Verdict: Betway's bonus is bigger if you're depositing. Hollywoodbets is better if you want zero-risk trial.

Mobile App Experience

Hollywoodbets

The Hollywoodbets app works, but it feels... old-school. The interface is functional, betting is straightforward, but it lacks the polish of newer apps.

If you're used to it, it's fine. But if you're coming from Betway or international betting apps, it'll feel clunky.

Betway

Betway's app is one of the best in South Africa. It's fast, clean, and intuitive. Live betting is smooth, odds update in real time, and the cashout feature actually works reliably.

For live betting, Betway is miles ahead. You can place bets mid-match without the app freezing or odds disappearing — a common issue on older platforms.

Verdict: Betway wins decisively. If you bet on your phone, this matters.

Sports Coverage: Who Has More Markets?

Hollywoodbets

Hollywoodbets focuses on sports South Africans actually care about: soccer (local and international), rugby, cricket, horse racing. They also cover tennis, golf, and a few niche sports.

Their local coverage is excellent — PSL, Currie Cup, domestic cricket. If you're betting on SA teams, they're as good as anyone.

Betway

Betway's range is broader. Beyond the usual (soccer, rugby, cricket), they offer deep markets in NBA, NFL, UFC, F1, esports, and even politics.

If you want to bet on obscure leagues or niche events, Betway is more likely to have it.

Verdict: Betway has more variety. Hollywoodbets has stronger local depth.

Live Betting: Who Does It Better?

Live betting (in-play betting) is where you place bets during a match as the action unfolds.

Both platforms offer live betting, but Betway's is significantly better. More markets, faster odds updates, and better mobile experience.

Hollywoodbets' live betting works, but it's more limited. Fewer prop bets, slower updates, and occasional glitches during high-traffic matches.

Verdict: Betway dominates live betting.

Withdrawals: Who Pays Faster?

Hollywoodbets

Withdrawals typically take 24-48 hours to hit your bank account. Instant EFT is available for some banks.

There's no withdrawal fee, and the minimum withdrawal is R50.

Betway

Betway takes 24-72 hours depending on your bank and verification status. First withdrawals can take longer due to FICA checks.

No fees, minimum withdrawal is also R50.

Verdict: Slight edge to Hollywoodbets for speed, but both are reasonable.

Customer Support: Who Helps You Faster?

Hollywoodbets

Hollywoodbets has local call centers, which is a big plus. You can call them and speak to a South African agent in English, Afrikaans, or Zulu.

Response times are generally good, though phone lines can get busy during major sporting events.

Betway

Betway relies more on live chat and email. Their chat is fast (usually under 5 minutes), but you might not always get a local agent.

Email support can take 12-24 hours.

Verdict: Hollywoodbets wins for people who prefer phone support. Betway is fine if you're okay with chat.

Which One Should You Choose?

Choose Hollywoodbets if:

  • You bet on horse racing regularly
  • You prefer local sports (PSL, URC, SA cricket)
  • You like old-school betting with strong customer support
  • You want a no-deposit free bet to start

Choose Betway if:

  • You bet on international soccer (EPL, Champions League)
  • You want the best live betting experience
  • You prefer a modern, fast mobile app
  • You bet on niche sports (NBA, UFC, esports)

Or Use Both

Here's the real answer: you don't have to pick one.

Most serious bettors have accounts at both bookies and shop for the best odds on each bet. Hollywoodbets for horses, Betway for soccer — that's a common strategy.

Final Verdict

Hollywoodbets: Best for horse racing, local sports, traditional bettors who value strong customer service.

Betway: Best for international soccer, live betting, and anyone who wants a modern, tech-forward betting experience.

Both are licensed, safe, and reliable. You can't go wrong with either — it just depends on what you're betting on.
