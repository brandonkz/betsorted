So is it worth sticking with Hollywood, or does Easybet offer enough to make you switch? We've tested both side by side — odds, apps, PSL betting, withdrawals, casino games, and USSD access — to give you the full picture. Mobile Experience: Hollywoodbets vs Easybet Apps

Hollywoodbets Mobile

Hollywood's app does a lot — sports betting, Lucky Numbers, Spina Zonke slots, live betting, account management — and it shows. The app can feel cluttered, especially if you're just trying to place a quick PSL bet. Load times are reasonable but not blazing fast, and the navigation takes some getting used to.

The big advantage? USSD betting via *120*065#. No app, no data, no smartphone needed. Dial in, pick your market, place your bet. For punters on feature phones or in areas with poor signal, this is massive. Hollywood built their empire on USSD accessibility, and it remains their trump card.

Easybet Mobile

Easybet's app is cleaner, faster, and more modern. The interface is intuitive — find a match, tap the odds, confirm your bet. It's built mobile-first rather than being a desktop site crammed into an app. The bet slip is smooth, markets load quickly, and the whole experience feels designed for punters who live on their phones.

No USSD channel though, which locks out feature phone users entirely. If you have a smartphone and data, Easybet's app is the better experience. If you need to bet without data or a smartphone, Hollywood is your only option here.

Lucky Numbers & Spina Zonke: Hollywood's Secret Weapons

Let's be honest — a huge chunk of Hollywoodbets' popularity comes from Lucky Numbers and Spina Zonke, not traditional sports betting.

Lucky Numbers

Lucky Numbers lets you bet on international lottery draws (UK 49s, Irish Lotto, etc.) with payouts up to 1:300 for a single number hit. It's essentially lotto betting but with more draws per day and smaller minimum stakes. Township punters love it because you can bet R1 and potentially win R300. It's addictive, accessible, and Hollywoodbets owns this market completely.

Easybet doesn't offer Lucky Numbers. If this is your thing, there's no alternative.

Spina Zonke Slots

Spina Zonke ("spin all" in isiZulu) is Hollywoodbets' online casino platform with 500+ slot games, progressive jackpots, and minimum stakes from R0.50. It's become a cultural phenomenon — you'll hear people talking about Spina Zonke in taxis, at braais, and on social media. Popular titles include Sweet Bonanza, Gates of Olympus, and Big Bass Bonanza.

Easybet has a casino section with 100+ games, but it's a fraction of Hollywood's offering. The games are decent (standard slots and table games from known providers), but there's no Spina Zonke brand energy or community around it.

If casino gaming is part of your betting routine, Hollywoodbets is the clear winner.

Live Betting

Both bookmakers offer in-play betting across football, rugby, tennis, cricket, and other sports. Neither offers live streaming, so you'll need to watch the game elsewhere while betting.

Easybet's live odds tend to update slightly faster, and their in-play interface is cleaner on mobile. Hollywoodbets has wider market depth on bigger matches (more player props, more micro-markets), but the experience can feel slower on the app.

Neither bookmaker is best-in-class for live betting in SA — Betway holds that crown. But for casual in-play punting on PSL matches, both get the job done.

Easybet fully supports FNB eWallet for both deposits and withdrawals — it's one of their strongest features. Hollywoodbets supports various payment methods but their eWallet integration isn't as seamless as Easybet's. For eWallet users, Easybet is the better choice.
