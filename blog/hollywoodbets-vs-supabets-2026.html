Hollywoodbets vs SupaBets: Best for PSL & Soccer Betting in SA (2026)

Published February 18, 2026 • 9 min read • Brandon K

Hollywoodbets and SupaBets are two of the biggest homegrown bookmakers in South Africa, and if you're betting on the PSL or local soccer, you've probably wondered which one's actually better. Compare the latest prices on our odds comparison page before you place a bet.

Both are proudly South African. Both have branches across the country. Both sponsor major local teams. But when it comes to odds, bonuses, PSL markets, and payouts, there are some real differences that can affect your bankroll. Run the numbers with the betting calculator to estimate payouts.

We've compared them across the categories that matter most for soccer punters — PSL odds, accumulator bonuses, mobile apps, withdrawal speed, and live betting — so you can pick the right bookmaker for your next Chiefs vs Pirates bet.

Sign-Up Bonuses: Who Gives You More to Start?

SupaBets wins the bonus war straight out the gate. They give you a R50 sign-up bonus with no deposit required — double what Hollywoodbets offers (R25).

Both bonuses come with wagering requirements, so you can't just withdraw the cash immediately. You'll need to roll it over on odds of 2.00 or higher (usually 3x to 5x turnover, depending on the promo terms). But R50 is R50, and if you're new to betting or just want to test the platform without risking your own money, SupaBets gives you more runway.

Hollywoodbets Bonus Breakdown

  • R25 no-deposit bonus on sign-up
  • R25 birthday bonus (if opted in)
  • Accumulator bonuses up to 100% on 6+ legs
  • Weekly free bets and reload bonuses (inconsistent)

SupaBets Bonus Breakdown

  • R50 no-deposit bonus on sign-up
  • Up to 170% accumulator bonus on 20+ selections
  • Cashback offers on losing bets (selected promotions)
  • PSL-specific bonuses during big matches

If bonuses matter to you — especially accumulator bonuses — SupaBets has the edge. Their 170% acca bonus on 20-leg multis is one of the highest in SA. Hollywoodbets caps out at 100%, which is still solid but not as generous.

PSL Odds: Who Pays Better on Local Soccer?

We tracked odds across 15 recent PSL matches (Chiefs, Pirates, Sundowns, SuperSport United, Cape Town City) and found that SupaBets consistently offered slightly better odds on match winners and over/under markets.

The difference isn't massive — we're talking 1.90 vs 1.85 on a typical PSL match winner — but over time, better odds mean more profit. If you're betting R1,000 per match over a season, those extra few cents per rand add up.

Live Betting & Streaming: SupaBets Pulls Ahead

If you're betting in-play during PSL matches, SupaBets has the better setup. They offer live streaming for selected PSL and international soccer matches directly in the app. Hollywoodbets has limited streaming (mostly horse racing), so you'll be relying on TV or radio for live match updates.

Both bookmakers offer solid in-play markets — match winner, next goal, corners, cards, etc. — but SupaBets' interface updates faster and has more granular markets (like "next throw-in" or "goal in next 10 minutes").

Hollywoodbets' live betting is functional but feels a step behind. The odds update more slowly, and the market variety isn't as deep. If you're a casual live bettor, it'll do the job. If you're serious about in-play soccer betting, SupaBets is the better tool.

Withdrawals: Who Pays Faster?

SupaBets edges out Hollywoodbets on withdrawal speed — 24 to 48 hours vs Hollywoodbets' 24 to 72 hours. Both require FICA verification before your first withdrawal, which can add a few days if you haven't submitted your documents.

Payment methods are similar:

  • EFT (bank transfer) — standard for both
  • Branch cash collection — available at both (more branches at Hollywoodbets)
  • Capitec Pay — supported by SupaBets

If you're in a rush to cash out winnings, SupaBets is slightly faster. If you prefer the security of collecting cash in person, Hollywoodbets has 5x more branches across SA, so there's probably one closer to you.

Customer Support: Branch Network vs Digital

Hollywoodbets has over 1,000 physical branches across South Africa — KZN, Gauteng, Eastern Cape, Western Cape, everywhere. If you have a problem or need help with FICA, you can walk into a branch and sort it out face-to-face. That's a huge advantage for many SA punters who prefer in-person support.

SupaBets has about 200 branches (growing), so you might not have one nearby. But their digital support is faster — live chat, WhatsApp, and email support are all responsive. Hollywoodbets' online support can be hit-or-miss; sometimes you'll wait hours for an email reply.

If you value in-person service and you live near a branch, Hollywoodbets wins. If you're comfortable with digital support and want faster online responses, SupaBets is better.

Accumulators: SupaBets Dominates

Let's be real — most PSL punters love a good accumulator. Six-leg multis, ten-leg weekend specials, the classic "rent money on 15 matches" accumulator. Both bookmakers offer accumulator bonuses, but SupaBets blows Hollywoodbets out the water.

SupaBets offers up to 170% bonus on accumulators with 20+ selections. Hollywoodbets caps out at 100% on 6+ legs. If you're building big accumulators (and the odds are already against you, let's be honest), you want every extra percent you can get.

Who Should Use Hollywoodbets?

Hollywoodbets is the better choice if you:

  • Bet on horse racing regularly (unmatched coverage in SA)
  • Live in a rural area or need USSD betting (data-free option)
  • Prefer in-person support and have a branch nearby
  • Want the reliability of SA's biggest and most established bookmaker
  • Value brand trust and local sponsorship (they sponsor PSL clubs)

Who Should Use SupaBets?

SupaBets is the better choice if you:

  • Focus on PSL and soccer accumulators (better odds + bigger bonuses)
  • Want live streaming for in-play betting
  • Prefer a modern, fast mobile app
  • Value faster withdrawals (24–48 hours)
  • Build big accumulators and want maximum bonus (170% on 20+ legs)

The Verdict: Which Bookmaker Wins?

If you're betting on PSL soccer and building accumulators, SupaBets is the better bookmaker. Better odds, bigger bonuses, faster withdrawals, live streaming, and a superior mobile app. It's built for soccer punters.

If you're betting on horse racing, live in a rural area, or value in-person support, Hollywoodbets is the safer bet. Their branch network, USSD betting, and horse racing coverage are unmatched in South Africa.

The smartest move? Use both. Many experienced punters have accounts with multiple bookmakers to shop for the best odds on every bet. SupaBets for PSL accumulators, Hollywoodbets for the Durban July. There's no rule that says you have to choose one.
