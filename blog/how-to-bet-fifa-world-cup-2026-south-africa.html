World Cup 2026 Betting Markets Explained

The World Cup offers more betting markets than any other single tournament. If you've only ever bet on PSL match results, you're about to discover a whole new world. Here's what's available:

🏆 Outright Winner

The big one. Who lifts the trophy in July? This is the most popular World Cup bet and also where you'll find some of the best early value. Current favourites:

  • Argentina — Defending champions and still loaded with talent. Messi retired from international duty after 2024, but Álvarez, Garnacho, and the next generation are ready. Around 6/1 with most SA bookmakers.
  • France — Mbappé is in his prime, and France always turn up at World Cups. Around 5/1.
  • Brazil — Five-time champions with a new golden generation. Home continent advantage (close to USA). Around 7/1.
  • England — Perennial "nearly men" but genuinely talented squad. Around 8/1.
  • Germany — Don't write them off. Ever. Around 10/1.
  • Spain — Euro 2024 champions with the best young midfield in world football. Around 8/1.

The outright market is best for long-term punters. You place your bet now, and it settles in July. The earlier you bet, the better the odds — prices will shorten as the tournament approaches and favourites start winning warm-up matches.

📊 Group Stage Betting

With 48 teams split into 12 groups of four, group betting is massive at this World Cup. Markets include:

  • Group winner — Which team tops the group?
  • To qualify from group — Top two advance (plus some third-place teams). Lower odds but safer bets.
  • Group stage points — Over/under on total points a team earns in the group.
  • Group stage total goals — How many goals across all group matches?

This is where Bafana betting gets really exciting. South Africa's group has been drawn, and the odds on Bafana topping their group or qualifying for the round of 16 are juicy. More on that below.

⚽ Match Betting

Every single World Cup match — all 104 of them — will have full betting markets. For each game, you can bet on:

  • Match result (1X2) — Win, draw, or lose
  • Both teams to score (BTTS)
  • Over/under goals (0.5, 1.5, 2.5, 3.5)
  • Correct score
  • Half-time/full-time result
  • First goalscorer — Bet on who opens the scoring
  • Anytime goalscorer — Player to score at any point
  • Handicap betting — Give weaker teams a virtual head start
  • Corners, cards, and player specials

On a big knockout match, Betway will offer 200-300 markets. Even Hollywoodbets and Sportingbet will have 50-80 markets per game. Use our betting calculator to work out potential returns before placing.

👟 Top Goalscorer

Who finishes the tournament as top scorer? This is a brilliant long-term market with big odds. With 48 teams and 104 matches, goals are going to fly in. Keep an eye on:

  • Kylian Mbappé (France) — The obvious pick. Around 8/1.
  • Erling Haaland (Norway) — If Norway qualify, he'll be in the conversation. Around 12/1.
  • Julián Álvarez (Argentina) — The new main man for the defending champions.
  • Harry Kane (England) — Tournament specialist. Always scores when it matters.
  • Vinícius Jr. (Brazil) — Ballon d'Or winner and Brazil's talisman.

🎯 Specials & Prop Bets

This is where World Cup betting gets properly fun. Specials markets include:

  • Best African team — Can Bafana be the top-performing African nation? 🇿🇦
  • Biggest shock result — Will there be a massive upset in the group stage?
  • Tournament total goals — Over/under for the entire World Cup
  • Will there be a red card in the final?
  • Fastest goal of the tournament
  • Country of winning team's confederation — Europe, South America, or an outsider?

Hollywoodbets and Betway both load up on specials as the tournament gets closer. Check back in May and June for the full range.

World Cup Betting Strategies for SA Punters

The World Cup is a marathon, not a sprint. 104 matches over a month means there's no shortage of betting opportunities — but it also means you need a strategy to avoid blowing your bankroll in the group stage. Here's what works:

1. Grab Early Value on Outrights — Now

Outright odds are at their longest right now, three months before kickoff. Once the warm-up matches start in May and June, prices will shorten. If you fancy Argentina, Brazil, or France, bet now and lock in the best price.

The same goes for Bafana markets. The "to qualify from group" price is better now than it will be the week before the tournament when every SA punter piles in. Be early. Be smart.

2. Build Group Stage Accumulators

The group stage is accumulator heaven. With 12 groups playing simultaneously, you can build accus across multiple matches on the same day. A four-fold accu with each leg at 1.50 pays around 5/1 — much better than a single bet.

Focus on "both teams to score" and "over 1.5 goals" in group stage matches. Teams play more openly in the groups (needing points, not just survival), and early World Cup matches historically produce more goals than knockouts.

Use our accumulator calculator to see exactly what your multi-bet pays out before placing.

3. Save Your Biggest Bets for Knockouts

It's tempting to go big on the group stage because there are so many matches. Resist the urge. The knockout rounds are where the real value lives — upsets happen more frequently, odds are more generous, and you've had two weeks of the tournament to assess which teams are actually performing well versus which were just hyped pre-tournament.

4. Use Live Betting Tactically

World Cup matches are emotional. Teams concede early goals. Red cards change everything. A favourite going 1-0 down in a group match where they need a win creates massive in-play value.

Betway's live betting platform is the best in SA for this — smooth odds updates, cash-out available on most markets, and 80+ live markets per match. If you're watching the game, have the app open.

5. Don't Bet With Your Heart on Bafana

I know, I know. We all want Bafana to win every match. But blind patriotism and betting don't mix. Back Bafana where the odds offer genuine value. If they're playing a group favourite and the match result odds are 8/1, a small punt is fine. But don't stake your entire World Cup bankroll on Bafana beating Brazil in the quarter-finals because you watched a motivational YouTube video.

Be smart. Be selective. Use the calculators to manage your stakes properly.

Bafana Value Bets

  • Bafana to reach the quarter-finals at 15/1 — A stretch, but the 2010 spirit is alive. Hugo Broos has the tactical nous to grind out knockout wins. Worth a small stake.
  • Bafana top African nation at 5/1 — With Morocco, Nigeria, Cameroon, and others in the mix, this is competitive but Bafana's form and hunger give them a shot.

Understanding World Cup Odds Formats

South African bookmakers display odds in decimal format (e.g., 2.50 means you get R2.50 back for every R1 staked, including your stake). Some international sites use fractional odds (e.g., 3/2) or American odds (+150).

Live Betting During World Cup Matches

The World Cup runs from June to July with multiple matches per day. That's live betting paradise — especially in those opening group stage rounds where four matches play simultaneously.

Here's why live betting is particularly good at the World Cup:

  • Time zone advantage — Matches in USA/Mexico/Canada will kick off in the late afternoon and evening SA time. Perfect for watching and betting after work.
  • Early goals change everything — A tournament favourite going 1-0 down to an underdog in the first 15 minutes? The in-play odds swing massively. That's your opportunity.
  • Cash-out is your friend — Placed an accu with three legs won and one to go? Cash out for guaranteed profit rather than risk it all on the last leg.
  • More data, better bets — By the third group game, you've watched teams play twice. You know who's fit, who's struggling, who's tactically vulnerable. Use that knowledge in-play.
