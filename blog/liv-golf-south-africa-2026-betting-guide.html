LIV Golf Johannesburg — What You Need to Know

Let's set the scene. LIV Golf Johannesburg runs from March 19–22, 2026 at The Club at Steyn City, a private estate course in the northern suburbs of Joburg. It's a 54-hole individual competition played alongside a team event, with a total purse of $25 million (roughly R460 million at current rates). The individual winner takes home $4 million.

The format is different from traditional golf. Every round uses a shotgun start — meaning all players tee off simultaneously from different holes. There are no cuts. Every player competes all three days (with an additional final round for this event). It's faster, louder, and frankly more fun to watch than your average tour event.

The altitude in Joburg (about 1,750m above sea level) is a genuine factor here. The ball flies roughly 10% further at altitude, which changes club selection and course management. Players who've competed at altitude before — particularly South Africans — have a subtle edge.

LIV Golf Betting Markets Explained

If you're used to betting on football or rugby, golf markets might look a bit different. Here's what's available at most SA bookmakers for LIV Golf events:

Outright Winner

The simplest market — pick who you think will win the tournament. Golf fields are large (48 players at LIV events), so odds tend to be generous even for favourites. That's the appeal of golf betting — even the favourite is usually priced at 5.00 or higher.

Top 5 / Top 10 / Top 20 Finish

These are essentially place bets. You're backing a player to finish in the top 5, 10, or 20. It's a great way to bet on a player you fancy without needing them to actually win. For a 48-player field, a top 10 bet gives you roughly a 20% baseline chance even before considering skill differences.

Head-to-Head Matchups

The bookmaker pairs two players and you pick which one finishes higher in the tournament. This is probably the best market for punters who know their golf — you're not trying to predict the winner from 48 players, you're just comparing two guys. Look for matchups where you think the odds don't reflect the true difference in form.

Team Winner

Unique to LIV Golf. The 13 teams of four players compete alongside the individual event. The best three scores from each team count each round. This adds a fun extra dimension — you can back Crushers GC (Bryson's team), Stinger GC (Oosthuizen's team), or any of the 13 squads.

Round Leader / First Round Leader

Who'll be leading after round one? This market usually offers big odds and is popular with punters who follow player form closely. Some players are notorious fast starters.

Steyn City Course Breakdown — What Matters for Betting

The Club at Steyn City is a relatively modern course designed by Nicklaus Design. It's a parkland-style layout with wide fairways, large greens, and water features on several holes. Here's what matters for your bets:

Altitude advantage: At 1,750m, the ball flies significantly further. Big hitters like DeChambeau, Rahm, and Koepka will bomb it past 350m regularly. But length alone doesn't win golf tournaments — accuracy and approach play matter more.

Greens: Kikuyu rough is thick and grabby. If you miss greens into the rough, chipping becomes tricky. Players with strong short games and experience on Kikuyu will have an edge. This quietly favours the South African contingent.

Weather: March in Joburg means warm days (around 25°C) with the possibility of afternoon thunderstorms. Rain can soften the course and make it play longer, which generally benefits lower-ranked players as it levels the field.

Betting Strategy for LIV Golf South Africa

Golf betting is different from football or rugby. Here are some strategies that actually work:

1. Spread Your Stakes

With 48 players in the field, even the favourite has roughly an 18-20% chance of winning. Rather than lumping R500 on Rahm, consider spreading R100 across five players at different price ranges. A R100 bet on Oosthuizen at 15.00 returns R1,500 if he wins.

2. Use Head-to-Head Markets

If you know your golf but can't pick a winner from 48, head-to-head matchups are your friend. You only need to be right about who finishes higher between two players. Look for value where a player's current form doesn't match their price.

3. Consider Top 10 / Top 20 Bets

These hit far more often than outright winners and still pay well. A top 10 bet on a mid-priced player at 3.00–4.00 can be a solid return for a reasonably likely outcome.

4. Watch the Weather

If afternoon storms are forecast, players with early tee times (or early shotgun positions) might get the better conditions. Check the forecast before finalising your bets.

The Team Event — An Extra Betting Angle

Don't overlook the team competition. LIV Golf's team format adds a completely separate betting market that many punters ignore. Here are the teams to watch at Steyn City:

Legion XIII (Jon Rahm's team): The favourite for the team event. Rahm carries the squad, but they've got solid support players. If Rahm fires, Legion XIII will be hard to beat.

Crushers GC (Bryson DeChambeau): DeChambeau's squad features Charles Howell III and Anirban Lahiri. They're inconsistent but capable of big days.

Stinger GC (Louis Oosthuizen): The sentimental favourite for the home crowd. The all-South African team includes Charl Schwartzel (a Masters champion) and Branden Grace. Playing in front of a home crowd, this team could be dangerous. Worth a punt at the odds available.

Ripper GC (Cameron Smith): Smith's team has been solid all season. With Marc Leishman and Matt Jones, it's an Australian-heavy squad that plays consistent golf.
