LottoStar Review South Africa (2026): Lucky Numbers, Virtual Games & Sports Betting

Published: 29 March 2026 · Updated: 29 March 2026 · 8 min read

LottoStar occupies a weird niche in the South African betting landscape. It's not really a traditional sportsbook. It's not a casino. It's somewhere in between — a platform built primarily around Lucky Numbers and virtual games, with a sports betting offering bolted on the side.

For a certain type of punter, that's exactly what they want. For others, it's a deal-breaker. Let's break it all down.

What Is LottoStar?

LottoStar is a licensed South African betting operator (Western Cape Gambling and Racing Board) that launched with a focus on fixed-odds betting on international lottery outcomes — what the industry calls "Lucky Numbers." They've since expanded into virtual sports, a handful of casino-style games, and basic sports betting.

The brand is 100% South African, accepting ZAR only, and primarily targets players who enjoy lottery-style bets with frequent draws throughout the day.

Lucky Numbers: The Core Product

This is LottoStar's bread and butter, and honestly, where they're most competitive.

How It Works

You bet on the outcome of international lottery draws — UK 49s, Ireland, Russia, Sweden, Greece, and dozens more. You're not buying lottery tickets. You're placing fixed-odds bets on which numbers will be drawn. The distinction matters because:

  • Higher flexibility: Bet on 1 number, 2, 3, 4, or all 6. Mix and match
  • Fixed payouts: You know exactly what you'll win before the draw
  • Frequent draws: Multiple international draws happen every hour throughout the day
  • Low stakes: Start from R1 per bet

Lucky Numbers Odds Comparison

Here's where it gets interesting. LottoStar's Lucky Numbers odds are competitive with Hollywoodbets and often slightly better on certain markets:

  • 1 number correct (UK 49s): LottoStar typically pays around 5/1, Hollywoodbets around 4.8/1
  • 2 numbers correct: LottoStar around 50/1, Hollywoodbets around 48/1
  • 3 numbers correct: Both around 600-650/1

The margins are small, but they add up if you're a regular Lucky Numbers player. Use our betting calculator to check potential returns before placing.

Virtual Games

LottoStar's virtual games section is decent. They offer:

  • Virtual football: Simulated matches with results every 3-5 minutes
  • Virtual horse racing: Automated races with form guides and jockey stats
  • Virtual dogs: Greyhound racing simulations
  • Spin & Win games: Wheel-of-fortune style games
  • Numbers games: Keno-style draws running constantly

If you compare the virtual football to what Betway or Hollywoodbets offer, LottoStar holds its own. The graphics are decent, results come quickly, and minimum bets are low.

Sports Betting: The Weak Spot

Here's where we have to be honest. LottoStar's sports betting is… basic.

What's Available

  • Football (PSL, EPL, Champions League, Serie A)
  • Rugby (URC, test matches)
  • Cricket (international, IPL)
  • Tennis, basketball, boxing

What's Missing

  • Limited markets per match: You might get 15-20 markets for a PSL game. Betway offers 100+
  • No bet builder / same-game multi: A major gap in 2026
  • Basic live betting: Available but with few in-play markets and slow updates
  • Odds quality: Generally 5-10% worse than Betway on mainstream football

If you're a serious sports bettor — someone who follows PSL closely, wants deep markets, and shops for the best odds — LottoStar is not your primary bookie. Full stop.

But if you mainly play Lucky Numbers and want to throw the occasional accumulator on weekend PSL matches, it's fine for that.

Deposits and Withdrawals

Deposit Methods

  • Instant EFT: Via Ozow, iPay — works with all major SA banks
  • Vouchers: 1ForYou, OTT vouchers, Blu Voucher
  • Card payments: Visa and Mastercard
  • Capitec Pay: Direct from the Capitec app

Minimum deposit is R10, which is lower than most competitors. Good for casual players who don't want to commit big amounts.

Withdrawal Speed

This is where LottoStar does well. Withdrawals are processed within 24 hours for verified accounts, and most EFT withdrawals land within 1-2 business days. That's in line with the fastest-paying SA bookmakers.

FICA verification is required (as with all licensed SA bookies) — have your ID, proof of address, and bank confirmation ready.

Mobile Experience

LottoStar has a mobile-optimised website rather than a native app on the Play Store or App Store. For South African users accustomed to data-free betting apps, this is a slight negative.

The mobile site loads quickly, Lucky Numbers are easy to navigate on a phone, and the virtual games work smoothly. Sports betting navigation is clunky on mobile — finding specific markets requires too many taps.

No data-free option. If you're watching data usage, Hollywoodbets and Betway are better choices.

Bonuses and Promotions

LottoStar runs occasional promotions, but they're not as aggressive as Betway or Hollywoodbets. You'll typically find:

  • First deposit bonus: Usually a 100% match up to a modest amount (R200-R500 range)
  • Lucky Numbers boosted odds: Enhanced payouts on selected draws
  • Multi-bet bonuses: Percentage boosts on accumulators with 3+ legs

Read the terms carefully. Wagering requirements on deposit bonuses tend to be 5-8x, which is fair compared to international operators but still meaningful for small deposits. Check our bonuses comparison for the latest offers across all SA bookmakers.

BetSorted Verdict

LottoStar is a specialist, not a generalist. If Lucky Numbers and virtual games are your thing, it's one of the best platforms in South Africa for that specific niche. The odds are competitive, draws run constantly, and the minimum stakes are the lowest around.

But if you're here for football, rugby, or cricket betting? Look elsewhere. Betway and Hollywoodbets are better all-round options by a significant margin.

Use LottoStar for: Lucky Numbers, virtual games, casual low-stake betting
