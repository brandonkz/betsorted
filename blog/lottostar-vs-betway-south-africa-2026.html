By BetSorted • April 5, 2026 • 10 min read

Betway is the safest all-rounder in South Africa: fast withdrawals, deep live betting, and consistently sharp odds on PSL and rugby. LottoStar (Lottostar) is built around Lucky Numbers and lotto-style markets, with a lighter sportsbook. If you want big match depth and reliable payouts, Betway wins. If you’re mainly a Lucky Numbers player who occasionally bets sport, LottoStar is still a good option.

FAQ

Is LottoStar a sports betting site or a Lucky Numbers site?
It’s primarily a Lucky Numbers and lotto-style bookmaker, but it does offer sports betting. The sportsbook is lighter than Betway, with fewer markets and less in-play depth.
Which bookmaker pays faster in South Africa?
Betway is faster overall (instant to 24 hours). LottoStar typically processes withdrawals in 24–72 hours. Faster payouts often depend on Instant EFT and completed FICA.
Do both offer betting calculators?
Yes, but BetSorted’s free calculators are easier to use. Try the betting calculator or accumulator calculator before placing a multi.
Which has better PSL odds?
Betway is usually sharper on PSL match markets and live odds. LottoStar can still be competitive on selected fixtures, but Betway is more consistent week to week.
