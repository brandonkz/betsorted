Lucky Numbers — The Main Event

This is the section that matters most for this comparison. If you're choosing between Lottostar and Hollywoodbets, there's a good chance Lucky Numbers is your thing. So let's go deep.

Range of Draws

Lottostar offers 60+ international lottery draws — from the obvious ones (UK 49s, SA Daily Lotto, US Powerball) to draws from Spain, France, Germany, Greece, Russia, and smaller markets. It's the widest selection of any SA-licensed platform. Our Lucky Numbers bookmaker ranking puts Lottostar at or near the top for draw range.

Hollywoodbets covers 30+ draws, which is still solid — you get UK 49s, Ireland, Greece, and the popular draws that most SA punters actually bet on. But if you want the more obscure international draws, Lottostar has them and Hollywoodbets doesn't.

Edge: Lottostar Winner — significantly more draws. If variety matters, Lottostar is the clear pick.

Payouts

This is where it gets interesting. Lottostar tends to offer slightly higher payouts on certain Lucky Numbers outcomes — particularly on single-number bets and smaller combinations. The differences aren't massive (we're talking a few rands per hundred), but they compound over time if you're a regular player.

Hollywoodbets' Lucky Numbers payouts are competitive and fair, but they're not trying to be the best in this specific category. Their margins are spread across a much bigger product range.

Edge: Lottostar Winner — slightly better Lucky Numbers payouts on average.

Frequently Asked Questions

Is Lottostar or Hollywoodbets better for Lucky Numbers?

Lottostar wins on Lucky Numbers specifically. More draws (60+ vs 30+), slightly better payouts, and an interface built entirely around lotto-style betting. Hollywoodbets' Lucky Numbers is solid but it's one product among many.

Which has faster payouts?

Hollywoodbets — most withdrawals process within 12-24 hours, and you can collect cash at a branch. Lottostar takes 24-48 hours, which is standard but slower.

Can I bet on sports with Lottostar?

Lottostar's sports offering is very limited. If sports betting matters to you — PSL, rugby, cricket, horse racing — Hollywoodbets is the clear choice. Lottostar is built for Lucky Numbers and virtual games, not traditional sports.

Are both Lottostar and Hollywoodbets legal in South Africa?

Yes. Lottostar is licensed by the Western Cape Gambling Board and Hollywoodbets holds licenses from the KwaZulu-Natal Gaming Board and several other provincial boards. Both operate fully legally and are regulated in South Africa.
