Lucky Numbers — The Main Event

Let's start where it matters most for this comparison. Lucky Numbers is the reason most people use Lottostar, and the reason many Supabets customers stick around between matches.

Lottostar: The Lucky Numbers KingWINNER

Lottostar offers over 100 lottery draws daily from around the world — UK 49s, Irish Lotto, Greek Powerball, Spanish La Primitiva, French Loto, German Lotto 6aus49, and dozens more. Draws happen every few minutes throughout the day, meaning there's always something to bet on.

What sets Lottostar apart is the depth of their number markets. You can bet on single numbers, pairs, triples, and full combos with various bet types — first ball, last ball, any position, exact order. The payout tables are transparent and generally offer better returns than Supabets on equivalent bets. On a typical single-number bet, Lottostar pays around 5.5:1 compared to Supabets' 5:1.

Lottostar also provides historical draw data, hot/cold number analysis, and draw-by-draw results — tools that help regular Lucky Numbers punters make more informed selections (or at least feel like they are).

Supabets: Solid Lucky Numbers, Better Everything Else

Supabets covers the most popular Lucky Numbers draws — UK 49s, Irish Lotto, Greek Powerball — with around 50+ draws daily. That's plenty for casual Lucky Numbers punters, but noticeably less than Lottostar's 100+ offering.

The interface is functional but not as polished as Lottostar's for numbers betting. Where Supabets compensates is by offering Lucky Numbers as part of a much broader platform — you can switch from Lucky Numbers to PSL betting to Aviator without leaving the app.

Sports Betting — Where Supabets Dominates

Supabets: The Full SportsbookWINNER

This is Supabets' home turf. They cover 25+ sports with deep markets across football, rugby, cricket, tennis, basketball, boxing, MMA, and more. PSL coverage is excellent — every fixture gets 30+ markets including correct score, first goalscorer, corners, cards, and halftime/fulltime combos.

Live betting at Supabets is one of the best among mid-tier SA bookmakers. Markets stay open deep into matches, odds update in real time, and the cash-out feature lets you lock in profits (or cut losses) before the final whistle. For the upcoming PSL title race, Supabets is offering outright markets, top scorer props, and relegation specials.

Lottostar: Sports Is an Afterthought

Lottostar has added sports betting to its platform, but it's clearly not the focus. You'll find the basics — match result, over/under, and handicap markets for major football, rugby, and cricket events. But the market depth is thin compared to Supabets. A typical PSL match at Lottostar might have 10-15 markets; the same match at Supabets has 30+.

Live betting at Lottostar is minimal. Don't expect in-play markets on anything beyond the biggest Premier League or Champions League fixtures. There's no cash-out feature, which is a dealbreaker for many modern punters.

If you primarily bet on sports, Lottostar isn't the right platform. Full stop.

For Lucky Numbers, the picture reverses. Lottostar consistently offers better payouts on number bets. Here's a comparison on UK 49s:

Mobile Apps

Lottostar App

Lottostar's mobile experience is optimised for Lucky Numbers. The app makes it easy to browse upcoming draws, pick numbers, and check results. The design is colourful and slightly busy — lots of draw countdowns and jackpot amounts competing for attention — but it works well for its core purpose.

The sports betting section within the app feels bolted on. Navigation between Lucky Numbers and sports isn't seamless, and the sports interface lacks the polish of dedicated sportsbooks.

Supabets App

Supabets' Android app covers everything — sports, Lucky Numbers, casino, virtual sports — in a single interface. It's not the prettiest app (the home screen is cluttered with promotions), but it's functional and reliable.

The data-free mobile site is Supabets' ace card. On Vodacom, MTN, Cell C, and Telkom, you can bet without using mobile data. This is a massive advantage for SA punters — check our data-free betting guide for details.

Casino & Virtual Sports

Both offer casino games — slots, Aviator, and crash games are available at both. Supabets has a wider selection overall, including live dealer tables and more slot providers. Lottostar's casino is adequate but smaller.

Virtual sports are solid at both. Supabets offers virtual football, horse racing, greyhounds, and motor racing. Lottostar has virtual football and horse racing with decent graphics and frequent events. If 24/7 action matters, both deliver.
