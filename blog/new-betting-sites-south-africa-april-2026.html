FICA Requirements — What You Need to Know

Every licensed SA bookmaker must comply with the Financial Intelligence Centre Act (FICA). This means identity verification before you can withdraw winnings. Here's what you'll need:

Document What's Accepted Tips
ID Verification SA ID card, SA ID book, passport Clear photo, all four corners visible
Proof of Address Utility bill, bank statement, lease agreement (within 3 months) Must match the name on your ID
Selfie Photo holding your ID next to your face Some sites require this, others don't

Most new bookmakers verify FICA within 24-48 hours. Saffaluck was the fastest in our testing at under 2 hours. Pantherbet was the slowest at 3 days.

⚠️ Don't skip FICA: Some punters bet and win, then can't withdraw because they haven't done FICA. Do it first — before you deposit. Save yourself the headache.

What to Check Before Signing Up to a New Bookmaker

  1. Licence: Check the footer for a provincial gambling board licence number. No licence = don't touch it.
  2. Withdrawal speed: Check reviews and social media. How fast do they actually pay out?
  3. Welcome bonus terms: A R10,000 bonus means nothing if the wagering is 50x. Read the T&Cs.
  4. Market depth: Do they cover the sports you actually bet on? PSL props? IPL? Horse racing?
  5. App quality: Download the app and place a few small bets before committing. Does it lag? Does live betting update in real time?
  6. Customer support: Send them a message before you deposit. How long do they take to respond?

Should You Stick With Established Sites Instead?

The smart move? Sign up to one or two new sites to grab the welcome bonuses, but keep your main action on an established platform. That way you get the best of both worlds.

Frequently Asked Questions

Are new betting sites in South Africa safe?

If they hold a valid provincial gambling board licence, yes. Check for a licence number on their website footer. All SA-licensed bookmakers must comply with FICA and responsible gambling rules. The sites listed in this guide are all licensed.

Which new SA betting site has the best welcome bonus?

For the biggest amount: Betshezi (R10,000 across 4 deposits). For the easiest to convert: Saffaluck (R20 free, 3x wagering). For free spins: Pantherbet (50 free spins, no deposit).

Do I need FICA to bet in South Africa?

Yes. All SA bookmakers require FICA verification before you can withdraw. You'll need your SA ID or passport, and proof of address. Some let you bet small amounts before FICA, but you can't cash out until verified.

Can I have accounts on multiple SA betting sites?

Yes. There's no law against having accounts at multiple licensed bookmakers. In fact, it's a smart strategy — compare odds across sites for every bet and always take the best price. See our odds comparison guide.
