Playabets vs Supabets South Africa 2026: Which Budget Bookmaker Is Better?

Published March 11, 2026 • 9 min read • Brandon K

Not everyone wants to throw R1,000 at a bookmaker to get a welcome bonus. Some of us just want to place a R20 bet on Sundowns to beat Chiefs on the weekend without breaking the bank. If that's you, Playabets and Supabets are probably on your radar — two bookmakers that cater to budget-conscious South African punters without making you feel like a second-class citizen.

But which one should you actually use? We've tested both extensively — deposits, withdrawals, odds, apps, the lot — and here's how they stack up. Before you pick sides, run any bet through our betting calculator to see what you'd actually pocket.

Welcome Bonuses: R350 vs R500

Let's start with what gets you in the door.

Playabets offers up to R350 as a free bet when you sign up and make your first deposit. The wagering requirements are standard — nothing unusual, nothing sneaky. For a budget bookmaker, R350 in free bets is a solid starting point. It won't change your life, but it gives you a few extra bets to explore the platform without risking your own cash.

Supabets goes a bit bigger with up to R500 on your first deposit. The requirements are comparable to Playabets, and the minimum deposit to qualify is reasonable. If you're looking purely at welcome bonus value, Supabets wins this round — you get more to play with upfront.

Here's the real talk though: neither of these bonuses is going to blow your mind. This isn't Betway's R1,000 or Betfred's R21,000. These are budget bookmakers, and the bonuses reflect that. What matters more is the ongoing experience — odds, markets, and how quickly they pay you.

Winner: Supabets — slightly bigger welcome bonus.

Odds Quality: Who Pays Better on Match Day?

PSL (DStv Premiership)

This is the bread and butter for most SA punters, and it's where Supabets makes its strongest case. On a typical PSL weekend — your Orlando Pirates vs Mamelodi Sundowns, Kaizer Chiefs vs AmaZulu — Supabets consistently offers competitive odds. Their margins on PSL matches are tighter than you'd expect from a bookmaker in this price bracket. On some fixtures, their prices match or beat larger bookmakers like Sportingbet.

Playabets covers all PSL matches, but the odds tend to be slightly wider. You're paying a bit more margin on each bet. It's not terrible — you won't feel robbed — but if you're betting R100+ on PSL regularly, those few percentage points add up over a season.

Winner: Supabets — noticeably better PSL odds.

International Football (Premier League, Champions League)

On international football, both bookmakers are competitive but neither is best-in-class. Supabets maintains its edge with slightly tighter margins on major European football. Playabets is serviceable but you're leaving a bit of value on the table compared to what you'd get at the bigger bookmakers.

If you bet heavily on international football, neither Playabets nor Supabets should be your primary bookmaker. Check our odds comparison tool for real-time prices across all SA bookmakers.

Winner: Supabets — marginally better international odds too.

Lucky Numbers

Now here's where the tables turn. Playabets is one of the best Lucky Numbers bookmakers in South Africa. They cover a massive range of draws — local and international — with decent payouts. UK 49s, SA Daily Lotto, Irish Lotto, Ghana Lotto, and dozens more. If Lucky Numbers is your game, Playabets has you sorted.

Supabets offers Lucky Numbers too, but the selection isn't as deep and the odds aren't always as good as Playabets' offering. It's perfectly fine for the occasional punt, but Playabets is the specialist here.

Winner: Playabets — significantly better Lucky Numbers selection.

Deposits: The R1 Minimum That Changes Everything

This is Playabets' party trick, and it's genuinely impressive. You can deposit as little as R1 at Playabets. One rand. That's not a typo. For someone who's new to betting, who just wants to try placing a couple of bets without committing real money, this is brilliant. You can literally start with your lunch money.

Supabets requires a R10 minimum deposit. Still very low — you won't find many bookmakers complaining about R10 — but it's ten times what Playabets asks. For most people, this doesn't matter. But for the truly budget-conscious punter, or someone testing the waters for the first time, Playabets' R1 minimum is unbeatable.

Winner: Playabets — R1 minimum deposit is the lowest in SA.

Withdrawals: Both Average, Neither Great

Let's be honest — if you're coming to Playabets or Supabets expecting Betway-level instant withdrawals, you're going to be disappointed. Both bookmakers process withdrawals via EFT, and both typically take 24–48 hours. Sometimes faster during business hours, sometimes slower on weekends.

Playabets has a R10 minimum withdrawal, which is great if you've just won a small amount and want to cash it out. Supabets requires R50 minimum, which is standard but means small wins might need to accumulate before you can withdraw.

Neither charges withdrawal fees, which is a plus. And both require FICA verification before your first payout — have your ID and proof of address ready.

For a detailed guide on getting your money out of any SA bookmaker, check our complete withdrawal guide.

Winner: Playabets — lower minimum withdrawal (R10 vs R50).

Mobile Experience: Apps Built for Budget Phones

Both bookmakers know their audience. Most of their users are on mid-range Android phones, not iPhone 15 Pros. And both have built their mobile experiences accordingly.

Playabets has an Android app that's lightweight and doesn't chew through your data. The mobile website also works well on any phone. Navigation is simple — you can find markets, place bets, and manage your account without any drama. It won't win design awards, but it works reliably, which is what matters when you're trying to get a bet on before kickoff.

Supabets' Android app is in a similar category — functional, data-light, and covers all the essentials. Their mobile site is slightly better organised than Playabets', with cleaner navigation and quicker access to live betting markets. The search function works well for finding specific matches.

Neither bookmaker has an iOS app — you'll use the mobile website on iPhone. Both mobile sites work fine in Safari, though they're clearly optimised for Android first.

Winner: Draw — both are functional and data-friendly. Supabets has slightly better mobile site navigation.

Live Betting: Supabets Takes This One

Live betting at budget bookmakers is always going to be more limited than what you'd get at Betway or Hollywoodbets. But there's a clear difference between these two.

Supabets offers decent live betting on football, including PSL matches. You'll get the standard in-play markets — match result, next goal, over/under, both teams to score — and the odds update reasonably well. It's not the slickest experience, but for a quick in-play bet during a Chiefs game, it does the job.

Playabets' live betting is more basic. Fewer markets per match, slower updates, and not all events are covered in-play. If you're the type who bets during the match rather than before it, Supabets is the better choice here.

Winner: Supabets — better live betting markets and coverage.

Horse Racing: Neither Is Hollywoodbets

Let's set expectations: if horse racing is your primary bet, neither Playabets nor Supabets should be your first choice. Hollywoodbets owns this market in South Africa, and for good reason.

That said, both bookmakers cover SA horse racing from the major tracks — Turffontein, Greyville, Kenilworth. Supabets has slightly deeper coverage with more bet types available (Win, Place, Swinger, etc.). Playabets covers the basics but it's clearly not their focus.

For the occasional punt on a Saturday race meeting, either will do. For serious horse racing punters, look elsewhere.

Winner: Supabets — marginally better horse racing coverage.

Customer Support: Supabets Has More Options

Supabets offers live chat, email, and phone support. Having a phone number to call is increasingly rare among SA bookmakers, and for punters who aren't comfortable with live chat, it's a genuine advantage. Response times on live chat are reasonable — typically under 10 minutes during business hours.

Playabets has live chat and email. The live chat works, response times are acceptable, but the lack of phone support is a drawback for some users. If you run into a tricky issue, being able to call someone and explain it verbally can make the difference.

Winner: Supabets — phone support is a nice extra.

The Verdict: Playabets vs Supabets South Africa

If we had to pick one, Supabets wins on points. Better PSL odds, stronger live betting, slightly bigger welcome bonus, and better customer support options. For a punter who bets on football regularly, Supabets delivers more value where it matters most.

But Playabets has a legitimate claim if you're on the tightest of budgets or if Lucky Numbers is your main game. That R1 minimum deposit and R10 minimum withdrawal is genuinely unique in South Africa, and their Lucky Numbers selection is hard to beat.

The honest answer? If you're choosing between these two, you're probably budget-conscious — so why not use both? Create accounts at Playabets and Supabets, compare odds on each match, and bet where the price is better. FAQ

Is Playabets or Supabets better for PSL betting?

Supabets edges Playabets on PSL coverage. Their odds on DStv Premiership matches are consistently competitive, and they offer a wider range of markets per PSL match including correct score, half-time/full-time, and player props. Playabets covers the PSL, but with fewer market options.

What is the minimum deposit at Playabets vs Supabets?

Playabets has one of the lowest minimum deposits in South Africa at just R1. Supabets requires a minimum deposit of R10. Both are very accessible for budget-conscious bettors, but Playabets wins on pure entry-level affordability.

Which pays out faster — Playabets or Supabets?

Both bookmakers process withdrawals within 24–48 hours via EFT. Neither offers instant withdrawals like Betway. In our experience, Supabets tends to be slightly faster on average, but the difference is marginal. Both require FICA verification before your first withdrawal.

Does Playabets or Supabets have better Lucky Numbers?

Playabets has a stronger Lucky Numbers offering. They cover more international and local lotto draws with competitive odds. Supabets offers Lucky Numbers too, but Playabets is known specifically for this market. If Lucky Numbers is your main game, Playabets is the better choice.

Are Playabets and Supabets both licensed in South Africa?

Yes. Both Playabets and Supabets hold valid licences from South African provincial gambling boards. Both are legal, regulated, and required to comply with FICA verification before processing withdrawals. Your funds are protected under SA gambling legislation.
