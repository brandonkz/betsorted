Whether it is a Test series at the Wanderers, a T20I at Newlands, or an ODI in Centurion, there are betting markets worth understanding — and bookmakers worth comparing. Cricket Betting Markets Explained

Cricket offers more market variety than most sports. Here is what you will find on SA bookmakers for a typical Proteas match:

Match Winner

The simplest market. Pick which team wins. In Tests, there is also the draw option, which adds complexity and value. For T20Is and ODIs, it is straightforward two-way betting (with super over rules applying in knockouts).

Example: Proteas vs India T20I

Proteas to win: 1.85
India to win: 1.95

A R500 bet on the Proteas at 1.85 returns R925 (R425 profit). Use the betting calculator to check your return at any stake.

Top Batsman

Pick which batter scores the most runs in an innings or match. This market is popular because it lets you bet on individual players. If you know the Proteas lineup and pitch conditions, you can find real edges here.

Look at batting order and recent form. An opener who faces 80+ deliveries in a Test innings is statistically more likely to top-score than a lower-order batter. In T20Is, the aggressive middle-order hitters can deliver value at longer odds.

Top Bowler

Same concept, but for wickets. Pick the bowler who takes the most wickets. Pitch conditions matter enormously — a green top at the Wanderers favours seamers, while a dry Centurion pitch might bring spinners into play.

Total Runs Over/Under

The bookmaker sets a line — say 320.5 runs for a T20I — and you bet on whether the actual total goes over or under. This market rewards you for understanding pitch conditions, weather, and team scoring patterns.

Example: T20I Total Runs

Line: Over/Under 320.5 runs
Over 320.5: 1.90 | Under 320.5: 1.90

At Newlands in February, with two strong batting lineups and a flat pitch, you might lean over. At a spicy Wanderers deck, under could be the play. A R200 bet at 1.90 returns R380.

Session/Innings Runs

Available mainly for Tests. Bookmakers set a line for how many runs will be scored in a specific session (morning, afternoon, evening). This is a specialist market that rewards punters who understand how conditions change throughout a day's play — the morning session in Jo'burg plays differently to the afternoon when the sun bakes the pitch.

Method of Dismissal

Bet on how the next wicket falls: caught, bowled, LBW, run out, stumped. This is a fun market with decent odds on the less common dismissals. It is hard to predict, but that is partly the point — the odds reflect the uncertainty.

Man of the Match

A popular market for T20Is and ODIs. You are essentially betting on the standout performer, which combines both batting and bowling possibilities. Look for all-rounders who can contribute in both innings.

Live Cricket Betting — Ball by Ball

Live betting transforms cricket because the game unfolds over hours (or days for Tests). Unlike football where the action is continuous, cricket has natural pauses between deliveries, overs, and innings that give you time to think and react.

Markets update ball by ball. After a wicket falls, the odds for the batting team to reach a certain total shift dramatically. After a big over, the over/under line adjusts. This creates opportunities that pre-match betting simply cannot offer.

When to Bet Live on Cricket

After a batting collapse: If the Proteas lose 3 wickets in the first 5 overs of a T20I, the odds on the opposition winning will drop sharply. But if you know the middle order can recover — think of a David Miller rescue job — there may be value in backing the Proteas at inflated live odds.

At the innings break: In T20Is and ODIs, the break between innings is a natural pricing reset. If the first innings total was below par, the chasing team's odds shorten. But conditions might change (dew factor, floodlights), which the bookmaker's algorithm might not fully account for.

During a partnership: When two batters are set and scoring freely, the over/under line starts climbing. If you think a wicket is coming (new bowler, tired batter, spin introduced), the under might offer value before the market catches up.

Betting by Format: Test, ODI, T20I

Test Cricket

Tests reward patience and knowledge. The draw market adds a third outcome, which means more pricing inefficiency for smart bettors. Weather, pitch deterioration, and day/night scheduling all create edges. Live betting on session runs is where the real value lives in Tests.

ODIs

The 50-over format is the middle ground. Total runs markets are popular, and the toss can matter significantly (batting first vs chasing under lights). The Proteas have historically been strong chasers at home, which is worth factoring into your pre-match thinking.

T20Is

Fast, volatile, and full of variance. Individual brilliance can swing a T20I in three overs. Accumulators are risky here because the variance is high — use the accumulator calculator to check the maths before combining T20I legs. Single match bets or in-play wagers tend to be better value.

SA20 — South Africa's T20 League

The SA20 league runs during the South African summer and features international stars alongside local talent. All major SA bookmakers cover it extensively, with match winner, top batsman, top bowler, and total runs markets available on every game.

SA20 is useful for cricket bettors because you can build familiarity with venues (Newlands, Wanderers, Centurion, St George's) and conditions that apply to Proteas international matches at the same grounds. The form you see in SA20 often carries over into international fixtures.

Cricket Betting Mistakes to Avoid

Ignoring the toss: In subcontinental conditions and on specific SA pitches, the toss can shift odds by 10-15%. Wait for the toss result before placing pre-match bets when conditions are expected to favour one side of the coin.

Over-staking on accumulators: Cricket is volatile. Three "safe" legs can all lose to individual brilliance. Keep acca stakes small and treat them as fun bets rather than core strategy.

Not shopping odds: The difference between 1.85 and 1.95 on a match winner is significant over a full international summer. Having accounts with at least Betway and Hollywoodbets gives you the ability to compare and grab the better price.

Betting without watching: Cricket rewards attention. Conditions change through the day, bowlers tire, new batters struggle early. If you are not watching, you are guessing — and the bookmaker's algorithms are smarter than blind guessing.

FAQ

Which bookmaker has the best cricket odds in South Africa?

Betway and Sportingbet generally offer the most competitive cricket odds for SA bettors. Hollywoodbets has good market depth on Proteas matches but odds can be slightly wider on international fixtures. Always compare prices before placing your bet.

Can I bet on cricket live in South Africa?

Yes. Most licensed SA bookmakers offer live cricket betting with markets that update ball-by-ball. Betway has the fastest in-play engine for cricket, while Hollywoodbets offers more prop markets during live play.

What is the most popular cricket bet in South Africa?

Match winner is the most popular market, followed by top batsman and total runs over/under. For T20 matches, most sixes and man of the match are also very popular with SA punters.

Is cricket betting different from football betting?

The mechanics are the same — you pick a market, choose your stake, and collect if you win. But cricket has unique markets like top bowler, method of dismissal, and session runs that do not exist in football. The longer format also means more live betting opportunities.

How do I bet on the SA20 league?
