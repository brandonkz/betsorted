PSL Betting Tips October 2026: Chiefs vs Stellenbosch, Pirates vs TS Galaxy and the Full Week's Fixtures Betting Guide

Published: 12 October 2026 | By BetSorted Team | 13 min read

October is shaping up as one of the busiest fortnights of the 2026/27 Betway Premiership season. Before the full weekend slate on 17–18 October, the PSL drops a heavy midweek programme on Tuesday 13 October – four fixtures in one night, including Kaizer Chiefs at home against Stellenbosch FC, Orlando Pirates vs TS Galaxy, and the always interesting Richards Bay vs Mamelodi Sundowns. That alone is enough to make any PSL punter cancel Tuesday evening plans.

This guide breaks down every fixture from 13 to 18 October, tells you where the value sits, and gives you a clean accumulator structure to work with. The October window is exactly when form lines start to settle and early bookmaker prices can still carry a soft edge. Use it.

Tuesday 13 October: The Main Event Night

Four PSL fixtures on one Tuesday evening is a proper treat. The Tuesday 13 October programme carries the four biggest clubs in the league's history across two of the four slots, which means bookmaker attention – and SA punter attention – will be squarely on FNB Stadium and wherever Pirates are hosting. Let's break down each match.

Orlando Pirates vs TS Galaxy

Start here because this is the safest-looking price on the card. Orlando Pirates at home against TS Galaxy at approximately 1.65 is not a price that screams value on its own, but it is a price you can build around. Pirates have been one of the more consistent home sides in the Betway Premiership over the past two seasons. Their front four in attack have enough penetration to break down a TS Galaxy midfield that has been leaking goals this campaign.

TS Galaxy away from home is a concern for the purists. They have been difficult to beat at the Mbombela but have not produced their best when travelling to the bigger venues. Pirates at 1.65 is a legitimate anchor leg for a Tuesday accumulator – not because it is a certainty, but because the margin of form supports the price with reasonable conviction.

If you want a bit more return from this fixture, look at Pirates to win and over 1.5 goals at approximately 1.80–1.85. Pirates tend to score early at home and that can open the game up for a multi-goal evening.

Kaizer Chiefs vs Stellenbosch FC

This is the fixture that divides opinions every time. Kaizer Chiefs at home at FNB Stadium sounds straightforward on paper, and the 1.90 home-win price reflects a marginal-to-moderate favourite status. But Stellenbosch FC are not a team you beat by turning up. They finished the 2025/26 season as genuine top-four challengers and have maintained the squad discipline and defensive structure that made them awkward to play against under Steve Barker.

Chiefs at 1.90 is a playable price for a home-win acca leg – but it is not a banker. The draw at 3.30 is where the value creeps in if you are building a combination bet or double-chance approach. Chiefs have drawn more than their share of high-profile home games over the past calendar year. If you want Chiefs, the Chiefs to win by 1 goal (correct-score 1-0 or 2-1) market – where it is available – is often priced around 4.50–5.00, which is worth checking at Hollywoodbets specifically.

Stellenbosch away win at 4.00 has value only if you are backing the Stellenbosch defensive system to grind out a counter-attacking result. That is not impossible – they have done it at bigger grounds – but 4.00 for a genuine away win at FNB Stadium asks you to believe Chiefs are significantly worse than the market suggests. The data does not quite support that yet.

Richards Bay vs Mamelodi Sundowns

The Mamelodi Sundowns away machine is one of the most dependable PSL betting angles year on year, and the 1.80 away-win price reflects that accurately. Sundowns have the depth, the structure and the individual quality to win at Richards Bay even without their best available eleven. The concern is the Richards Bay setup at home, which has been particularly organised this campaign and has kept a clean sheet in two of its last three home outings.

Sundowns winning at 1.80 is the market's consensus for good reason. The more interesting prices sit in the handicap or goals markets. Sundowns -1.0 handicap at approximately 3.20 (Sundowns to win by 2+ goals) is worth checking if you believe Sundowns bring their A team. Alternatively, under 2.5 goals at approximately 1.85 suits the Richards Bay home defensive approach and gives you a scenario where a narrow Sundowns 1-0 is enough to cash.

If you are building a three-leg PSL acca for Tuesday night, Sundowns away is your safest leg. The price is modest but it is justified.

Durban City vs AmaZulu

The Durban derby carries its own emotional weight regardless of league position. Durban City are the home side at 3.20 – they are not exactly favourites – and AmaZulu at 2.40 is the market's preferred outcome. AmaZulu have the stronger squad on paper this season and their set-piece quality gives them an edge in tight matches.

The draw at 3.10 is almost the same price as the home win, which reflects the genuine uncertainty here. Durban derbies are unpredictable by nature. The draw at 3.10 is arguably the value bet if you are comfortable with a lower-probability, higher-return approach. Both teams will respect what is at stake and the early-season caution can produce a tight, tactical affair that ends all square.

If backing for a win, AmaZulu at 2.40 is the more grounded choice. But this fixture is one where keeping it off your accumulator and treating it as a standalone low-stake play is smart bankroll management.

Accumulator Strategy: Tuesday 13 October

Here are three structured acca options based on the Tuesday fixtures. None of these are guarantees – use them as a framework, not a prescription.

The Conservative Double (Lower Risk)

  • Orlando Pirates to win – approx. 1.65
  • Mamelodi Sundowns to win away – approx. 1.80
  • Combined return: approx. 2.97

This is your anchor combination. Two clear-favourite legs with form support. The downside is the modest combined return – nearly 3x is not going to change anyone's fortunes but it is a sensible daily bet structure if you are staking anything meaningful.

The Three-Legger (Moderate Risk)

  • Orlando Pirates to win – approx. 1.65
  • Mamelodi Sundowns to win – approx. 1.80
  • Kaizer Chiefs to win – approx. 1.90
  • Combined return: approx. 5.64

Adding Chiefs brings the combined return into proper acca territory. The Chiefs leg carries variance – Stellenbosch can absolutely nick a draw or win at FNB Stadium – but if you are confident in the home advantage and the early-season momentum at FNB, this is a defensible three-legger at approximately 5.64.

The Value Play (Higher Risk)

  • Orlando Pirates to win – approx. 1.65
  • Mamelodi Sundowns to win – approx. 1.80
  • The draw in Durban City vs AmaZulu – approx. 3.10
  • Combined return: approx. 9.20

The draw leg in the Durban derby is genuinely playable based on the even pricing, but it is a higher-risk call. This combination returns over 9x if all three legs land. Stake accordingly – this is a small-unit play, not your main bet.

Weekend Fixtures: October 17–18

The proper weekend slate on 17–18 October features four PSL fixtures, though none carry the marquee appeal of Tuesday's programme. This weekend is where the mid-table battle gets genuinely interesting.

Chippa United vs Kruger United (Saturday 17 Oct)

Chippa at home at approximately 2.20 is a reasonable favourite in a fixture that typically carries lower scoring. Kruger United are a promoted side finding their feet at the top level, and while they have shown resilience away from home, Chippa's experience at Nelson Mandela Bay should give the home side the edge. The draw at 3.20 is attractive if you want to fade both sides. This is a tough match to price confidently – if Chippa are going through a difficult run, treat it as a match to watch rather than one to back.

Golden Arrows vs Milford FC (Saturday 17 Oct)

Golden Arrows at home at 2.00 versus Milford FC – one of the newer faces in the Premiership – is the safer-looking price of the weekend batch. Milford are building a following and have not embarrassed themselves in the top flight, but they have also not been consistent enough to back on the road. Golden Arrows at 2.00 is a reasonable weekend anchor if you want PSL exposure on Saturday.

Sekhukhune United vs Marumo Gallants (Saturday 17 Oct)

This fixture has produced entertainment in recent meetings. Sekhukhune at 2.10 against Marumo Gallants, with Gallants at 3.50 and the draw at 3.20, is a fairly balanced three-way price line. Sekhukhune's Limpopo home support gives them a meaningful edge, but Marumo have been surprisingly competitive away this season. If you want an each-way-style play, the draw at 3.20 is not dismissible. Sekhukhune to win is the majority call at 2.10.

Siwelele vs Polokwane City (Sunday 18 Oct)

Sunday's sole PSL fixture is Siwelele against Polokwane City. At approximately 2.30 home and 3.10 away, this is almost too close to call on form alone. Polokwane at 3.10 away could appeal as a small value play if you believe the Limpopo outfit have been underrated early in the campaign. But without strong recent form data to lean on definitively, this is another match where waiting for pre-match team news is worth more than any preview odds.

Odds Shopping: Which Book to Use for PSL Betting

For PSL fixtures specifically, the marginal differences between bookmakers can be meaningful on short-priced favourites. Here is a quick rundown of where to look:

  • Hollywoodbets – Often competitive on PSL match odds and typically fastest to publish prices for midweek fixtures. Their Betbuilder is useful for combining match result with goal markets.
  • Betway – Solid PSL coverage with a clean app experience. Tends to price PSL draws slightly differently to Hollywood, which creates odds-shopping opportunity on even-priced matches like the Durban derby.
  • World Sports Betting (WSB) – Strong on PSL first-goalscorer and total-goals markets. Worth checking their PSL prices if you are betting goals rather than outright results.

Bankroll Management for a PSL-Heavy Week

With eight PSL fixtures spread across Tuesday to Sunday, it is tempting to be everywhere at once. The disciplined approach is to identify two or three high-confidence plays per session rather than trying to cover the full board.

  • Tuesday: Focus on Pirates and Sundowns as your core legs. Add Chiefs only if the team news supports it.
  • Weekend: Golden Arrows at 2.00 is the cleanest-priced weekend anchor. The Sekhukhune vs Gallants game is interesting for a small speculative play.
  • Acca size: Two or three legs maximum for meaningful confidence. A four-leg PSL acca on a busy week is a recipe for the draw gremlins to eat your slip.
  • Stake sizing: Never stake more than 2–5% of your bankroll on any single accumulator, regardless of how confident you feel.
