PSL Golden Boot 2026: Who Will Be Top Scorer? Betting Guide

Published: 21 March 2026 · Reading time: 11 min · By BetSorted

With nine or ten rounds of the DStv Premiership remaining in the 2025/26 season, the PSL Golden Boot race is entering its most intense phase. The top scorer award remains one of the most interesting outright betting markets in South African football — the race is genuinely competitive most seasons, and the bookmakers don't always get the pricing right.

Whether you're a Sundowns supporter backing the clinical striker who's been tearing up defences, a Pirates fan convinced their man has the goals in him to pull ahead, or just a punter looking for value in a market you understand well, this guide breaks down every major contender, the available odds, and where to place your bets.

The 2025/26 PSL Top Scorer Race — Where Things Stand

As of late March 2026, the Golden Boot race is tight at the top with several strikers within two or three goals of each other. This is exactly the kind of scenario where outright market value exists — when there's genuine uncertainty, bookmakers have to price multiple players generously.

The Main Contenders

Peter Shalulile — Mamelodi Sundowns

~2.50 favourite

Shalulile is the benchmark. The Namibian striker holds the PSL single-season scoring record (23 goals, 2021/22) and has been a consistent Golden Boot contender every year since his arrival at Sundowns. When he's on form and Sundowns are playing well, he's essentially unplayable in the PSL.

Form: Back to his best this season after a period of inconsistency. Running in behind defences, getting into the box, and converting at a high rate.

Remaining fixtures: Sundowns' run-in looks manageable — no back-to-back away trips to provincial rivals, and several home fixtures at Loftus where they tend to dominate. The Africa Champions League doesn't appear to be fatiguing the squad the way it did two seasons ago.

Verdict: The favourite for good reason. At 2.50 he's not exceptional value, but if you want the safest bet in this market, this is it.

Tshegofatso Mabasa — Orlando Pirates

~3.50

Mabasa is the most clinical finisher outside of Sundowns this season. The Pirates striker has the added advantage of playing in a team that's genuinely title-contending, which means they're creating chances in the right types of games. He also scores in big matches — the Soweto Derby, the Nedbank Cup — which matters for his confidence and form.

Form: Excellent. Has shown a knack for bagging braces in matches where Pirates are dominant, which accelerates his tally faster than single-goal-per-game strikers.

Remaining fixtures: Pirates' remaining schedule includes home games that they should be winning comfortably. A potential title race run-in means Pirates will be pushing hard in every game, creating more chances for Mabasa.

Verdict: The best value pick in the field. At 3.50, the odds are generous for a striker of his quality who's playing for a title-chasing side. Worth a meaningful stake.

Evidence Makgopa — Bafana Bafana / Orlando Pirates

~5.00

Makgopa is the wild card. He's shown in glimpses — particularly in Bafana Bafana colours — that he has the physical tools and finishing ability to win a Golden Boot. His challenge is consistency. He can go three or four games without a goal, then bag a hat-trick against a team who can't handle his movement.

Form: In and out of the starting eleven depending on form and tactical decisions. When he starts, he's a genuine threat; when he's used as an impact substitute, his tally grows more slowly.

Remaining fixtures: If Pirates need goals in the title run-in, Makgopa could find himself with more starts. A genuine outside chance — but at 5.00, the price reflects the uncertainty around his consistency.

Verdict: Each-way interest at 5.00. Not a conviction bet, but worth a small stake if you believe in him.

Iqraam Rayners — Stellenbosch FC

~6.00

Rayners is the most interesting non-Big-Four striker in this race. Stellenbosch have been one of the PSL's better stories this season — they play attractive, attack-minded football, and Rayners is the beneficiary. He's a clever striker who makes excellent runs and has a decent goals-per-game ratio.

Form: Consistent throughout the season. No explosive purple patches, but regular contributions — the kind of striker who ends on 14 goals and sneaks the Golden Boot in a quiet year.

Remaining fixtures: Stellenbosch's run-in depends on whether they're still chasing a top-four spot. If they're in a comfortable mid-table position, expect some rotation and potentially fewer chances for Rayners.

Verdict: Interesting outsider at 6.00. If Shalulile picks up a niggle or goes quiet over two or three games, Rayners could capitalise. A speculative bet worth small stakes.

Gaston Sirino — Mamelodi Sundowns

~9.00

Primarily a creator rather than a goal scorer, but Sirino scores more than his label suggests. He's capable of explosive runs of goals when his confidence is high. The risk is that Sundowns have Shalulile as the main striker, which limits Sirino's volume of goalscoring opportunities compared to a striker in a team that runs the ball through him.

Verdict: Fun bet but not a serious Golden Boot contender. Too many competing options at Sundowns.

Alternative Betting Strategies

Anytime Goalscorer Bets

If you're not comfortable committing to a season-long outright, anytime goalscorer bets on individual PSL matches are a strong alternative. You're backing a striker to score at any point in a specific match — no season-long exposure.

The best targets for anytime goalscorer on the PSL: Shalulile in Sundowns home games (especially against bottom-half sides), Mabasa in Pirates' home fixtures, and Rayners whenever Stellenbosch are playing away from home against mid-table opposition.

Typical odds for these players as anytime goalscorer in favourable matchups range from 1.65–2.20 — manageable risk, decent return. Betting on Shalulile to score at home against a team in the bottom four at 1.70 is a more repeatable, lower-variance strategy than a season outright at 2.50.

💡 Anytime goalscorer tip: Combine two or three anytime goalscorer picks from the same match round into a small accumulator. Three selections at 1.75, 1.80, and 2.00 respectively gives combined odds of 6.30 — solid return if all three score. Use our accumulator calculator to build your multi.

First Goalscorer Betting

Higher odds than anytime, but more variance. First goalscorer markets on PSL fixtures are available at Betway and Hollywoodbets. Shalulile and Mabasa as first scorer in home fixtures of their respective clubs can range from 4.00–7.00, offering meaningful returns on modest stakes. The risk is obvious — your striker scores, but not first.

Accumulator Strategies Using the Top Scorer Race

One approach worth considering: build a weekly PSL accumulator using the Golden Boot contenders as anytime goalscorers. If you're following the race closely and you know Shalulile is due a run of fixtures against weaker sides, backing him as anytime scorer across three or four matches over two weekends — using our accumulator calculator to check the combined odds — can be a disciplined, well-researched way to bet on the Golden Boot race without putting all your eggs in one outright basket.

  • Pirates playing cautiously in must-win cup fixtures — changed tactics can reduce striker involvement
  • A domestic cup final run — cup games don't count toward PSL Golden Boot

Frequently Asked Questions

Which bookmakers offer PSL Golden Boot betting in South Africa?

Betway is the most reliable SA bookmaker for PSL outright markets including the Golden Boot. Hollywoodbets and Sportingbet also offer top scorer betting but the markets can be inconsistent. Betway typically lists the PSL top scorer market with 8–12 named players and updates odds throughout the season. Check their football outrights section under South Africa / PSL.

How many goals does the PSL Golden Boot winner typically score?

The PSL Golden Boot winner typically scores between 14 and 20 goals in a 30-game season. Peter Shalulile set the PSL record with 23 goals in the 2021/22 season. In recent seasons the winner has scored 15–18 goals. Strikers on teams that create many chances — Sundowns, Orlando Pirates — tend to dominate the top scorer race.

Can I bet on anytime goalscorer in PSL matches?

Yes. Betway and Hollywoodbets both offer anytime goalscorer markets on PSL matches. These are typically available for the biggest fixtures — Soweto Derby, Sundowns vs Pirates, Nedbank Cup matches. Anytime goalscorer is a popular alternative to outright top scorer betting because you're betting on a single match rather than the entire season.

Is PSL top scorer betting worth it late in the season?

Outright top scorer odds compress significantly as the season progresses and the race becomes clearer. Late-season top scorer betting can still offer value if there's a genuine multi-way battle. The real value is typically in the first half of the season when several players are in contention and bookmakers are pricing the field more generously. That said, backing the current leader with a 2–3 goal cushion late in the season can be a profitable low-risk play.
