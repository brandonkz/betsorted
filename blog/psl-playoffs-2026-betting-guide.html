PSL Playoffs Format: How It Works

The PSL Playoffs take the top 8 teams from the regular season and run them through a knockout-style bracket:

  • Quarter-Finals (QF): Home and away legs. Higher seed plays the first leg away, second leg at home. Aggregate score decides who advances.
  • Semi-Finals: Single-leg matches at neutral or home venues.
  • Final: Single-match decider, venue TBC (typically FNB Stadium for big finals).

The seedings matter a lot. The top seed (almost certainly Sundowns) gets the most favourable QF draw, facing 8th place. The home leg advantage in the semis and final typically goes to the higher remaining seed. This is why the top-4 regular season finish is so valuable — it makes life measurably easier in the knockouts.

Potential Quarter-Final Matchups

Based on current seedings, here's how the QF bracket would look if it were drawn today:

1 Sundowns vs 8 Richards Bay

On paper, the most lopsided of the four matchups. Sundowns should advance comfortably barring a serious implosion. Richards Bay finishing 8th is a genuine achievement but this is a mountain too steep.

2 Cape Town City vs 7 AmaZulu

More interesting than it looks. AmaZulu can be genuinely dangerous on their day, especially over two legs. City should advance but don't rule out AmaZulu nicking an aggregate win if they get a first-leg result.

3 Orlando Pirates vs 6 Stellenbosch FC

This is the QF of the round for neutrals. Stellenbosch have been the season's best story — compact, hard to beat, tactically excellent. Pirates love a knockout — they have the squad and atmosphere to grind through. Expect goals and drama.

4 Kaizer Chiefs vs 5 SuperSport United

This one needs no introduction. Chiefs vs SuperSport in a playoff tie. SuperSport are typically very disciplined and make it difficult, but Chiefs have been better this season. Close, probably settled by a moment of individual quality.

Who Can Actually Beat Sundowns?

Let's be honest about this: Sundowns at 1.40 are short for a reason. They've been the best team in the PSL by a significant margin and their squad depth is unmatched. Even if they rest key players in the QFs, they have enough to get through.

But playoffs are weird. Two legs, high-pressure, and one bad night can end everything. The teams most likely to cause the upset:

  • Orlando Pirates (5.50–5.75): The most realistic threat. They have the history, the crowd, the quality. If they get a draw in the first leg away from home, they can win the tie at Orlando.
  • Kaizer Chiefs (8.50–9.50): Chiefs have upset Downs in knockout stages before. They'll be the sentimental pick for a lot of punters. The 9.00 at Hollywoodbets represents reasonable each-way value.
  • Cape Town City (6.75–7.00): Consistent but do they have the X-factor for a deep playoff run? They've been solid but not explosive. Tough to see them lifting it.
The dream final? Pirates vs Sundowns — or Chiefs vs Sundowns. Either would produce the kind of atmosphere that reminds you why football is South Africa's favourite sport. The bookmakers agree that one of those finals is most likely.
