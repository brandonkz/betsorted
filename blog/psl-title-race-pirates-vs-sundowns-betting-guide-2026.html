The Story So Far

This season has been a proper cat-and-mouse chase between Gauteng's two heavyweights. Pirates kicked the season off in blistering form, while Sundowns — chasing a ninth consecutive title — took a while to hit their stride.

The momentum shifted after the Soweto Derby on 1 March, where Pirates dismantled Kaizer Chiefs 3-0. That result sent a message to the entire league: Jose Riveiro's Pirates aren't just title contenders — they're favourites. Evidence Makgopa's headed goal sealed it, and the Buccaneers went top.

But Sundowns responded immediately. A hard-fought 2-1 win over Golden Arrows — achieved with 10 men — showed the champions' mentality. They're not going quietly.

Both teams then won their midweek fixtures (Pirates 2-1 at Polokwane City, Sundowns maintaining pace), leaving them deadlocked heading into the crucial final stretch.

Most SA bookmakers still have Sundowns as slight favourites — that's the "eight consecutive titles" premium. And honestly, it's hard to argue. Sundowns have been here before, they know how to grind out a title run-in, and they have the deepest squad in the league.

But there's genuine value on Pirates at 2.10+. Here's why:

  • Momentum: That 3-0 Soweto Derby win was a statement. Pirates are playing with incredible confidence.
  • Goal difference: Being top on GD means Pirates effectively have a half-point advantage. In a season this tight, that matters.
  • Jose Riveiro: The Spanish coach has transformed Pirates. His tactical flexibility — switching between formations mid-match — has been elite.
  • Home fortress: Pirates' home record has been outstanding. With key fixtures still at Orlando Stadium, they control their own destiny.
💡 Betting angle: If you fancy Pirates, the best time to back them outright is now, while bookmakers still give Sundowns the experience premium. If Pirates win their next two home games (Richards Bay, Siwelele), their odds will shorten significantly.

Can Anyone Else Win It?

Short answer: no. Kaizer Chiefs' 3-0 derby loss effectively ended any lingering hopes. Stellenbosch and other mid-table sides have been impressive but lack the consistency for a title challenge. This is a two-horse race, pure and simple.

The only scenario where a third team matters is as a "kingmaker" — if one of the top two drops points against a mid-table side in the final weeks. Smart punters should watch for fixtures where Pirates or Sundowns face tricky away trips and look for value on the draw or slight underdog.

How to Bet on the PSL Title Race

If you're new to outright betting, here's how it works:

  1. Outright winner: You bet on which team lifts the trophy at the end of the season. Your bet settles when the league is mathematically decided — you don't need to wait for the final matchday if a team clinches early.
  2. Top 4 finish: Some bookmakers offer markets on which teams finish in the top 4 for CAF Champions League qualification. Lower odds but higher probability.
  3. Relegation: For the other end of the table, you can bet on which teams go down. Orbit College and Siwelele (promoted sides) are typically favourites in this market.

Where to bet: Betway and Hollywoodbets typically have the best PSL outright markets. Check both before placing — odds can differ by 10-20% on outright winner bets.

This is genuinely 50/50. Sundowns have the experience and squad depth. Pirates have the momentum and a manager who's tactically elite. If we had to pick one, we'd lean slightly towards Pirates at 2.10+ — the value is on their side given both teams are locked on points.

But honestly? The smartest bet might be to back both at these odds — you're guaranteed a profit if you balance your stakes correctly. A R100 bet on Pirates at 2.10 and R115 on Sundowns at 1.85 guarantees a small return regardless of who wins.
