🎯 Our Tip: Stellenbosch Win or Draw (Double Chance)

Stellenbosch have quietly become one of the most difficult sides to beat at Danie Craven this season. Steve Barker's side plays compact, disciplined football that neutralises technically superior opponents — and Chiefs in 2026 are not the dominant force they once were.

The Glamour Boys travel to the Cape with question marks in midfield and a patchy record away from Johannesburg. With nothing but pride to play for at this point in the season, motivation on the Chiefs side is inconsistent. Stellenbosch, still fighting for a top-8 finish and potential CAF Confederation Cup qualification, have every reason to push hard here.

Our recommended bet: Stellenbosch Win or Draw (Double Chance) at ~1.45. Low risk, good foundation for an accumulator leg. Alternatively, Under 2.5 Goals at ~1.70 if you think this plays out as the tactical, low-scoring match the form suggests.

Confidence: ⭐⭐⭐⭐ (4/5) · Stakes recommended: 1–2 units

Richards Bay vs Mamelodi Sundowns
📅 Saturday 11 Apr · 17:30 · King Zwelithini Stadium, Richards Bay
Richards Bay Win8.00
Draw4.50
Sundowns Win1.40
🎯 Our Tip: Sundowns Win + Over 2.5 Goals

Richards Bay are deep in a relegation scrap and have been leaking goals at an alarming rate in recent weeks. Against a Sundowns side motivated by a title race they can't afford to drop points in, this has "heavy defeat" written all over it.

Masandawana travel with Rulani Mokwena's side having conceded just three league goals in their last eight away fixtures. Richards Bay's defensive record at home is among the worst in the division. Sundowns should win this comfortably, and the over 2.5 goals line looks generous given Sundowns' attacking firepower.

Our recommended bet: Sundowns Win & Over 2.5 Goals combined at ~2.30. Sundowns are the closest thing to a banker bet in SA football right now — but always use the accumulator calculator to check if the combined odds justify the bet.

Value play: Sundowns to Win by 2+ Goals at ~2.10 if available on your bookmaker.

Confidence: ⭐⭐⭐⭐⭐ (5/5) · Stakes recommended: 2 units

Chippa Win3.20
Draw3.00
City Win2.30
🎯 Our Tip: Cape Town City Win

Chippa United are in genuine trouble. Last in form over the past six weeks, shipping goals and struggling to convert at the other end. The Chilli Boys desperately need points to escape the relegation zone, but Cape Town City arrive as the form team of the two.

Eric Tinkler's City side have been solid in away fixtures and have the technical quality to expose a fragile Chippa defence. The 2.30 about City winning in Gqeberha looks like legitimate value — Chippa's home record has been poor this campaign.

Contrarian angle: Chippa to score first at ~3.00 could be a value pick if you believe in the home side's desperation to get a result. Relegation-threatened sides often start fast before fading.

Confidence: ⭐⭐⭐ (3/5) · Stakes recommended: 1 unit only

Orlando Pirates vs SuperSport United

Pirates Win1.80
Draw3.50
SuperSport Win4.20
🎯 Our Tip: Pirates Win & Both Teams to Score

This is the standout match of the weekend. Pirates at Orlando Stadium, still alive in the title race and needing maximum points, against a SuperSport United side that has upset bigger teams on the road this season. Matsatsantsa a Pitori are not here to make up the numbers.

Pirates have been electric at home — Bucs fans turn Orlando Stadium into a fortress, and the Buccaneers' pressing style is best expressed on that pitch. But SuperSport have scored in every away match since January, making the "both teams to score" market worth serious consideration alongside the Pirates win.

Our recommended bet: Pirates Win & BTTS at approximately 3.20. Pirates should win this, but clean sheet is not guaranteed against SuperSport's attack.

Alternative: Pirates -1 Asian Handicap at ~2.50 if you think Pirates run away with it. This nullifies the risk of a narrow 1-0 win if the handicap is -1.

🏆 Title Race Context: What Sundowns and Pirates Need

Going into this weekend, Mamelodi Sundowns hold a 4-point lead at the top of the DStv Premiership table with five games remaining. A win over Richards Bay on Saturday would extend that to 7 points — effectively ending the title race if Pirates slip up on Sunday.

Orlando Pirates cannot afford to drop points. A Pirates win on Sunday against SuperSport, combined with a Sundowns win, leaves the gap at 4 points with 4 games to play. Tight. Very tight. That's why Sunday's match at Orlando Stadium carries title race weight even with Sundowns playing a day earlier.

"This is the most competitive PSL title race since the early Pitso Mosimane years. Sundowns are still favourites, but Pirates have the character to make this go to the wire." — common view among SA football analysts this season

⬇️ Relegation Battle: Who's in Danger?

With five games left, the bottom three places are not yet decided. Chippa United are the most vulnerable — currently in the automatic relegation zone, they face a Cape Town City side that will be ruthless. Richards Bay are a point above Chippa and face Sundowns — a loss would drag them deeper into trouble.

For bettors, this creates value in handicap markets on the relegation-threatened sides. When a team like Chippa is desperate for a win but playing against a top-half side, the pre-match odds often underestimate how badly they'll defend under pressure.
