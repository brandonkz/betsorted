Orlando Pirates travel to the north coast on Easter weekend as overwhelming favourites — and rightly so. The Sea Robbers have been exceptional away from Orlando Stadium this season, posting the longest unbeaten run in the league (14 matches). Junior Dion leads the golden boot race with 12 goals and continues to terrorise defences.

Chiefs are motivated to keep pressure on Sundowns in the title race, and the 94,736-capacity FNB is the loudest venue in South African football.

TS Galaxy were on the wrong end of the season's biggest thrashing (0-6 against Orlando Pirates at home) and have looked like a relegation side for months. Kaizer Chiefs should win this comfortably, and a -1 Asian Handicap might actually offer better value than the outright win at 1.65.
