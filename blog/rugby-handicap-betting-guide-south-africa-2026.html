What Is a Rugby Handicap Bet?

A handicap bet (also called a line bet or spread) adds or subtracts points from a team’s final score. If the favorite is -7.5, they must win by 8+ for you to cash. If the underdog is +7.5, they can lose by 7 or fewer (or win outright) and you still win.

Handicaps are popular in rugby because blowouts are common, but pricing can be soft when local teams are underrated. Use handicap markets to avoid short moneyline prices and get more value on strong teams.

How to Read Line Movement

Line movement shows where the market is leaning. If the Springboks open at -6.5 and move to -8.5, it means bettors (or sharp money) are backing the Boks to win by more. That often happens when team news drops or the market expects heavy forward dominance.

  • Move toward favorite: More money on the favorite, or odds makers anticipating dominance.
  • Move toward underdog: Injury news, weather, or public money fading the favorite.
