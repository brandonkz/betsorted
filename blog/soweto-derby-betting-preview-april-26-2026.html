Why This Derby Matters in April 2026

This isn't just bragging rights. With the Betway Premiership season in its final few rounds, both clubs have something concrete at stake beyond local pride.

Orlando Pirates — Aiming High

Pirates have been one of the more consistent performers in the 2025-26 season. With Mamelodi Sundowns in their usual dominant position at the top of the table, the race for second place — and the prestige that comes with it — is still very much alive. Pirates have assembled one of the more complete squads in recent memory, with Brazilian striker Gilberto leading the attack and midfielder Tshegofatso Mabasa pulling the strings in midfield.

A derby win would put Pirates in excellent shape to close out their best league season in several years. Three points at home also sends a statement to the rest of the division: this is a Pirates side that delivers when it counts.

Kaizer Chiefs — Back From the Brink

Kaizer Chiefs have had a turbulent few seasons, but coach Nasreddine Nabi has managed to stabilise the club and inject belief into the Amakhosi. The January 2026 transfer window additions have made a real difference — particularly in the holding midfield role where the squad was visibly short for much of the first half of the campaign.

Chiefs are playing to secure a top-eight finish and, crucially, avoid an embarrassing defeat to their fiercest rivals. In derby football, the gap between the two sides matters less than the occasion. Chiefs have a track record of rising to the Soweto Derby even in tough seasons.

Key Players to Watch

Orlando Pirates

  • Evidence Makgopa (Striker): The Bafana Bafana man has been lethal in front of goal this season. Has scored in three consecutive home matches. Best price for first goalscorer is around 4.50.
  • Tshegofatso Mabasa (Midfielder): The engine of Pirates' midfield. Creates opportunities and can arrive late in the box — a threat from set pieces in particular.
  • Kabelo Dlamini (Right Wing): The pace merchant who terrorises full-backs. Look for him to be a key outlet if Pirates want to play on the break after scoring.

Kaizer Chiefs

  • Ranga Chivaviro (Striker): Chiefs' most reliable goal threat. Tall, physical, and a constant aerial danger. If Chiefs are going to score, chances are he'll be involved.
  • Edson Castillo (Defensive Midfielder): The Venezuelan international is the man who makes Chiefs tick. If Castillo wins the midfield battle, Chiefs are dangerous. If he's overwhelmed, Chiefs struggle to build.
  • Rushwin Dortley (Centre Back): Has been excellent under Nabi. Composed, reads the game well, and will be crucial in handling Pirates' front three. A quiet game from Dortley is often a prerequisite for a Chiefs clean sheet.
