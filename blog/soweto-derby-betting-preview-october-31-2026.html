The Week Before: CAF Commitments

Both clubs have continental football on Saturday 24 October, a week before the derby. Orlando Pirates host African Stars at Orlando Stadium in the return leg of their CAF Champions League tie. Kaizer Chiefs host Red Arrows at Moses Mabhida Stadium in the second leg of their CAF Confederation Cup tie. Chiefs played the first leg in Ndola on 17 October.

Those games affect the derby in three ways.

  • Travel and minutes. Check which starters played the full 90 minutes a week earlier.
  • Squad rotation. A coach who protects players on the 24th may do it for league games too.
  • Mood. A club that goes out of Africa lands in the derby with a different mindset to one that goes through.

Wait for the 24 October results before you commit to a derby stake. Prices placed early do not account for injuries from those games.

Where the Derby Markets Sit

Match result (1X2)

This is the headline market. Pirates are the nominal home side, but FNB Stadium is a neutral-feeling venue for this fixture. Do not assume a normal home edge. Bookmakers price derbies tighter than league games between the same clubs. Compare all three outcomes, not only the win.

Double chance and draw no bet

Derbies are cagey and a single goal often decides them. Double chance and draw no bet cut your risk when you lean one way but fear a stalemate. The price is shorter. You pay for the safety. Our draw no bet guide explains when that trade is worth making.

Total goals

Check the over/under 2.5 line and the under 1.5 line. Derbies often play out tight, but that is a tendency, not a rule. Check recent results for both clubs, then compare the line at three bookmakers. A half-goal difference in the line changes the bet entirely.

Both teams to score

This market tracks how open each coach wants the game to be. If team news shows both sides picking attacking line-ups, BTTS gets more interesting. If one coach picks a defensive midfield, it gets less interesting.

Bet builders

A bet builder lets you combine markets from one match, such as a result plus total goals plus cards. Derbies are card-heavy, so cards and corners markets are popular. Remember that every extra leg lowers the chance the bet lands. Keep builders to two or three legs.

First goalscorer

It pays well and it hits rarely. Treat it as a small-stake bet. Wait for confirmed line-ups, because a player who starts on the bench loses his price value at once.

How Derby Prices Behave

Derby prices carry more public money than a normal league game. Casual punters back their club, and bookmakers shade prices to protect themselves. That can leave the draw or the opposition slightly longer than a pure form line would suggest. It can also mean the favourite is shorter than it should be.

You cannot see that from one bookmaker. You see it by comparing. If one operator is clearly longer than the others on an outcome, ask why before you take it. Sometimes it is a stale price. Sometimes it is a bookmaker that has seen team news you have not.

Prices also move on the morning of the match. Many punters bet early and miss the late move once line-ups drop. If you can wait, wait. If you must bet early, keep the stake small and leave room to add after team news.

Pre-Match Checklist

  • Check suspensions and injuries from the 24 October CAF games.
