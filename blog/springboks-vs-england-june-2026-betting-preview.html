Form Guide: Springboks vs England Head-to-Head

South Africa and England have faced each other 46 times since 1906. The Springboks lead the series 27–16, with 3 draws. Recent meetings have been tight — the 2019 World Cup final (Boks won 32–12), the 2021 Autumn series (England won 27–26 at Twickenham), and friendlies that have gone either way.

At Ellis Park, the Springboks are a different beast. The altitude affects England's fitness, and the Boks' scrum and maul thrive at the Highveld. In their last five home Tests against England, South Africa have won four and lost one. When the Boks play at altitude, betting against them is rarely profitable.

England are rebuilding after a mixed Six Nations campaign. Their forward pack is strong, but the backline lacks the firepower to trouble the Boks consistently. Unless England's set piece dominates (unlikely at altitude), this is a Springboks win on paper.
