Odds Comparison: Who Pays Better?

This is where the money is, so let's start here.

Betway consistently offers tighter margins across most sports. On international football — Premier League, Champions League, La Liga — Betway's prices are typically 2-4% better than Sunbet. That might not sound like much on a single bet, but over 50 or 100 bets across a season, it's the difference between being profitable and slowly bleeding money.

For PSL matches, the gap narrows. Sunbet has improved their Betway Premiership odds significantly over the past year, and on big derbies (Soweto Derby, Tshwane Derby) you'll sometimes find Sunbet matching or even slightly edging Betway's prices. This suggests Sunbet is trying to compete specifically on local football, which is good for punters.

Rugby tells a similar story. Betway tends to offer better prices on Springbok tests and international rugby, while Sunbet's URC odds are competitive. For horse racing, Sunbet's offering is decent but doesn't compare to specialists like Hollywoodbets or World Sports Betting.

Winner: Betway overall, but Sunbet is competitive on PSL and URC.

Withdrawal Speed: Not Even Close

Betway's instant EFT withdrawals are borderline ridiculous. Complete FICA, request a withdrawal, and the money is in your bank account within minutes. Not "up to 24 hours." Not "1-3 business days." Minutes. Multiple punters have confirmed seeing funds land within 2-5 minutes of requesting a payout.

Sunbet processes withdrawals in 24-48 hours via EFT. That's industry-standard for SA — plenty of bookmakers operate on the same timeline. It's not bad. But standing next to Betway's instant payouts, it feels slow.

One thing Sunbet does offer is the ability to link your Sun International casino account. If you're already a Sun MVG member, your rewards points accumulate across both sports betting and casino activity. That won't speed up your withdrawal, but it adds value if you're a Sun International regular.

Winner: Betway, and it's the clearest advantage in this entire comparison.

Welcome Bonuses: R2,000 vs R1,000

On paper, Sunbet's R2,000 matched deposit bonus dwarfs Betway's R1,000 free bet. But as always, the devil is in the wagering requirements.

Sunbet's R2,000 bonus requires a matched deposit (deposit R2,000, get R2,000 in bonus funds). The wagering requirements are on the higher side — you'll need to turn over the bonus amount multiple times at minimum odds before withdrawing. Read the full terms carefully. Higher bonus + higher rollover doesn't always equal better value than a smaller bonus with easier conditions.

Betway's R1,000 free bet is simpler. Deposit R1,000, get R1,000 in free bets. The wagering requirements are more straightforward, and many punters find them easier to clear. Betway also runs ongoing Betway Boost promotions — enhanced odds on selected markets every week — which add long-term value beyond the initial bonus.

For experienced punters, the ongoing promotions matter more than the welcome offer. You claim the welcome bonus once. Promotions keep coming.

Winner: Sunbet on raw bonus size, but Betway on overall value and ease of clearing.

Casino & Cross-Platform Betting

This is where Sunbet genuinely differentiates itself.

Sunbet is backed by Sun International, South Africa's biggest casino group. This means your Sunbet account gives you access to slots, table games, live dealer games, and Lucky Numbers alongside sports betting — all on one platform. If you're someone who enjoys both sports betting and casino games, having everything in one wallet is genuinely convenient.

The Sun MVG (Most Valued Guest) rewards programme links your online Sunbet activity with Sun International's physical casinos. Points earned from sports betting contribute to your MVG tier, which unlocks benefits at GrandWest, Sun City, Carnival City, and other Sun International properties. If you already gamble at Sun casinos, adding Sunbet to the mix makes your rewards accumulate faster.

Betway offers casino games too, but they're a separate section of the platform rather than an integrated experience. There's no equivalent loyalty programme linking online and physical gambling.

Winner: Sunbet, clearly. If you value casino-sports integration and loyalty rewards, this is Sunbet's strongest selling point.

Mobile Experience

Betway's mobile app is one of the best in South African betting. Available on iOS and Android, it's fast, clean, and mirrors the full desktop experience. Live betting is smooth on mobile, multi-bets are easy to build, and the app supports biometric login. It feels like an app built by a team that actually uses it.

Sunbet has an Android app and a mobile-optimised website. The app is functional — you can place bets, manage your account, and access most markets. But the interface isn't as polished as Betway's. Navigation can feel slightly clunky, especially in the live betting section where speed matters. The mobile site is adequate but loads slower than Betway on lower-end devices.

Neither bookmaker offers USSD betting (that's a Hollywoodbets thing), so both require data or WiFi to use.

Winner: Betway on app quality and responsiveness.

Sport Coverage & Markets

Betway covers more sports and offers deeper markets per event. This is especially noticeable in:

  • Live betting: Betway offers hundreds of live markets per match (next goal, corners, cards, minute-by-minute markets). Sunbet's in-play offering is more limited.
  • International football: Betway covers lower European leagues, South American football, and Asian leagues that Sunbet doesn't always include.
  • Tennis & MMA: Betway's depth on individual sports is stronger across the board.

Sunbet covers all the major sports SA punters care about — PSL, EPL, Champions League, URC, Springboks, cricket, and horse racing. You won't struggle to find the markets you want on mainstream events. But if you're into more niche betting (Japanese football, minor cricket leagues, eSports), Betway has a clear edge.

Both bookmakers offer Same Game Multis on major football leagues, which is increasingly important for SA punters who enjoy building custom bets.

Winner: Betway on depth and breadth, but Sunbet covers all the essentials.

PSL & Rugby Betting

Both bookmakers take PSL seriously. You'll find match result, over/under goals, BTTS, correct score, first goalscorer, and Asian handicap markets on all Betway Premiership fixtures.

For URC rugby, both offer comprehensive coverage of Bulls, Sharks, Stormers, and Lions matches. Betway has a slight edge on live rugby markets — you'll get more in-play options during a URC match on Betway than Sunbet.

For Springbok tests, both bookmakers compete aggressively on pricing. The November internationals and Rugby Championship tend to see the sharpest odds as both try to attract the massive SA rugby betting audience.

Cricket coverage is similar — both cover Proteas internationals and CSA domestic competitions. Betway's live cricket betting is slightly more developed, but the pre-match markets are comparable.

FAQ

Is Sunbet or Betway better for PSL betting?

Both offer solid PSL coverage. Betway tends to have slightly more in-play markets for PSL matches, while Sunbet offers competitive pre-match odds. For overall PSL betting depth, Betway edges it. But check the odds comparison for specific matches — Sunbet occasionally beats Betway on PSL prices.

Which bookmaker pays out faster — Sunbet or Betway?

Betway, by a wide margin. Betway processes EFT withdrawals in minutes (often 2-5 minutes). Sunbet withdrawals take 24-48 hours. Both require completed FICA verification before processing any withdrawal.

Does Sunbet have a mobile app?

Sunbet has an Android app and a mobile-optimised website. Betway offers both iOS and Android apps with a more polished interface and better live betting experience. Neither offers USSD betting — for that, you'd need Hollywoodbets.

Can I use Sun Rewards points from Sunbet at Sun International casinos?

Yes. Sunbet activity contributes to your Sun MVG rewards tier. Points earned from sports betting and casino games count towards your tier status, which unlocks benefits at Sun International properties including GrandWest, Sun City, Carnival City, and others.

Are both Sunbet and Betway legal in South Africa?

Yes. Both bookmakers hold provincial gambling board licences and are fully regulated under the National Gambling Act. Your funds are protected, and you have legal recourse if any issues arise.
