Odds Comparison: Where Your Money Goes Further

Neither Sunbet nor Easybet will consistently beat Betway or Hollywoodbets on every single market. But they both offer competitive odds in certain areas — and knowing where each one shines can save you real money over a season of betting.

PSL (Betway Premiership)

Sunbet is the stronger option for PSL betting. Their odds on local football matches are consistently competitive, and they offer a wider range of markets per game than Easybet. On a typical Kaizer Chiefs vs Orlando Pirates match, Sunbet might offer 60+ pre-match markets — from match result and both teams to score, through to player-specific props and correct score combos. Easybet will give you the essentials (1X2, over/under, BTTS) but rarely goes much deeper.

Where Sunbet really differentiates is on accumulators. Their multi-bet odds tend to be slightly more generous than Easybet's, which makes a difference when you're building a PSL weekend accumulator. Run your potential acca through our accumulator calculator to see the exact returns before locking it in.

Rugby (URC, Springboks)

Sunbet takes this one comfortably. The Sun International backing means they've invested in decent rugby coverage — URC matches, Springbok Tests, and the Rugby Championship are all well-covered with competitive odds and a reasonable range of markets. You'll find handicap lines, try scorer markets, and half-time results.

Easybet's rugby coverage is functional but limited. You'll get the basics on major Springbok matches and URC derbies (Sharks vs Stormers, Bulls vs Lions), but the depth drops off quickly. If rugby is your main sport, Sunbet is the better choice here — though neither can match the top rugby betting sites in SA.

Cricket

Both bookmakers cover international cricket — SA20, Proteas Tests, ODIs, and T20Is. Sunbet edges it with slightly more markets and competitive odds on cricket. Easybet covers the headline matches but doesn't go deep on session betting or player props.

Withdrawal Speed: Easybet's Surprise Advantage

Here's where Easybet lands a genuine punch. Their withdrawal speeds are impressive for a smaller bookmaker.

Once you've completed FICA verification, Easybet processes EFT withdrawals quickly — most go through the same day, and many within an hour during business hours. It's not quite Betway's "instant" level, but it's faster than most of the competition. Weekend withdrawals are slower (banks don't process EFTs on weekends), but you'll typically see it hit your account first thing Monday morning.

Sunbet's withdrawal process is more standard. You're looking at 24 to 48 hours for EFT payouts. That's not terrible — it's roughly the industry average for SA bookmakers — but it feels slow when you know Easybet is processing the same thing in under an hour. Sunbet also supports bank transfers and some e-wallet options, but the speeds don't vary dramatically across methods.

Winner: Easybet — surprisingly fast payouts for a mid-tier bookmaker.

Welcome Bonuses: Sunbet's Bigger Offer

Sunbet offers up to R1,000 in free bet credits when you sign up. It's a matched deposit bonus — deposit R1,000, get R1,000 in free bets. The wagering requirements are reasonable but worth reading carefully. You'll need to roll over the bonus amount at minimum odds before you can withdraw any winnings from free bet stakes.

Easybet's welcome bonus is up to R500 in free bets. Smaller, but the qualifying conditions are straightforward. The minimum deposit to trigger the bonus is lower than Sunbet's, making it more accessible if you're starting with a modest bankroll. Easybet also runs regular promotions on football matches — usually enhanced odds specials on selected PSL fixtures.

Neither bonus is going to change your life. The smart approach is to treat bonuses as a small cherry on top, not a reason to pick one bookmaker over another. What matters more is long-term odds quality, market coverage, and how quickly you get paid. For a deeper dive into what's available, check our best betting bonuses guide.

Winner: Sunbet on size. Easybet is more accessible for smaller budgets.

Mobile App: Clean vs Feature-Rich

Sunbet's mobile app (Android) and mobile-optimised website give you the full platform experience. The interface is clean, navigation is logical, and you can access live betting, deposits, and account management without friction. The app loads quickly and handles live betting updates well enough — no major lag or crashes during busy PSL match days.

Easybet went for a different approach: keep it light. Their app is stripped back and fast. It doesn't try to cram every feature onto one screen. You can find a match, place a bet, and check your slip in about four taps. For punters who just want to get their bets down quickly — maybe during half-time at a braai — Easybet's simplicity is actually a strength.

The trade-off is depth. Sunbet's app gives you more markets, more stats, and more tools within the app itself. Easybet keeps things minimal. Neither app will blow you away if you're used to Betway's polish, but both are perfectly functional for day-to-day betting.

Winner: Draw — depends on whether you value features (Sunbet) or speed (Easybet).

Live Betting: Sunbet Takes the Lead

Sunbet's live betting is genuinely solid. During a PSL match, you'll find a decent range of in-play markets — match result, next goal, corners, cards, over/under, and some player-specific options. The odds update reasonably quickly, and bet placement during live action is smooth. They also offer live betting on international football, rugby, cricket, and tennis.

Easybet's live betting covers the main markets — match result, over/under, BTTS — and the odds are fair. But the range of available markets during live play is narrower than Sunbet's. If you're the type to bet on the number of corners in the second half or the next player to score, you'll find more options with Sunbet. If you stick to the main lines, Easybet handles live betting adequately.

Neither bookmaker offers live streaming, which is a limitation. For that, you'll need to look at Betway or Hollywoodbets. Read our live betting guide for tips on how to get the most from in-play markets.

Winner: Sunbet — more markets, better depth in-play.

Deposit Methods: Both Play Nice with SA Banks

Both Sunbet and Easybet accept the deposit methods that South African punters actually use:

  • EFT — instant bank transfers via Nedbank, FNB, Standard Bank, Absa, Capitec
  • Instant EFT — via Ozow, Pay@, or SiD for immediate processing
  • Debit/Credit Card — Visa and Mastercard
  • Vouchers — OTT vouchers, 1ForYou

Sunbet also accepts deposits via Capitec Pay, which is a plus if you're banking with Capitec (as roughly a third of SA betting punters are). Easybet's voucher deposit options are broad, making it easy to fund your account from a retailer if you prefer not to use bank transfers.

Horse Racing: Sunbet Has More Depth

If you bet on the horses, Sunbet is the better choice between these two. They cover all major SA racecourses — Turffontein, Greyville, Kenilworth, Scottsville — and offer win, place, and some exotic bet types. It's not the most comprehensive racing platform in SA (that's Hollywoodbets), but it's serviceable for the occasional flutter on the Durban July or a Saturday afternoon at Kenilworth.

Easybet's horse racing is basic. They cover the big meetings but don't offer much depth in terms of bet types or minor meetings. If horse racing is a significant part of your betting diet, neither of these is your primary bookmaker — but Sunbet is the better secondary option.

FAQ

Is Sunbet or Easybet better for PSL betting?

Sunbet generally offers better PSL odds and more betting markets per match. Their connection to the Sun International group means they invest heavily in football coverage. Easybet is competitive on main match markets but has fewer options for player props and in-play betting during PSL games.

Which pays out faster — Sunbet or Easybet?

Easybet has the edge on speed. Most EFT withdrawals process within a few hours, sometimes under an hour. Sunbet typically takes 24–48 hours for EFT payouts. Both require FICA verification before your first withdrawal.

Do Sunbet and Easybet both have mobile apps?

Both offer Android apps and mobile-optimised websites. Sunbet's app is more feature-rich with a polished interface. Easybet's app is lighter and simpler, which some punters actually prefer for quick bets on the go.

Can I bet on horse racing with Sunbet and Easybet?

Sunbet offers decent horse racing coverage across SA tracks including Turffontein, Greyville, and Kenilworth. Easybet also covers horse racing but with fewer exotic bet types. Neither matches Hollywoodbets for racing depth, but Sunbet is the stronger of the two.

Are Sunbet and Easybet both licensed in South Africa?

Yes. Sunbet is licensed through Sun International's existing gambling licences and operates under the relevant provincial gambling boards. Easybet holds licences from the Gauteng Gambling Board. Both are fully legal and regulated in South Africa.
