South African Bookmaker Showdown

Sunbet ⚡ GBets

Casino-backed betting platform vs Goldrush's community favourite

Sunbet and GBets are two of SA's most interesting mid-tier bookmakers — and one of the least-compared pairs in the local market. Both have carved out real followings, both are fully licensed and regulated, and both offer something the big players don't quite nail.

Sunbet is operated by Sun International — the casino and resort giant behind SunCity, The Table Bay, and dozens of other properties across southern Africa. GBets is run by the Goldrush Group out of Midrand, with a strong community following and arguably the best Lucky Numbers product in the country.

We've deposited real money and tested both platforms. Here's the honest breakdown.

Welcome Bonuses: Different Philosophies

Sunbet runs a deposit match: 100% up to R1,000, with a 3x rollover requirement on odds of 1.50 or higher. Deposit R500, get R500 bonus, wager R1,500 to unlock it. The 3x rollover is one of the lowest in the SA market — genuinely easy to clear over a weekend of PSL and rugby betting. This is a proper bonus, not a bait-and-switch with impossible wagering conditions.

GBets takes a different angle: a R50 free bet with no deposit required. You get R50 to bet with before putting in a cent. The free bet needs to be wagered 5x (R250) before you can withdraw winnings. For someone who wants to test the platform before committing real money, this is the better entry point. GBets also runs reload bonuses for existing customers and a Goldrush Rewards points system that gives back over time.

Our take: If you're planning to deposit R500 or more, Sunbet's 3x rollover match is the better raw deal. If you want a zero-risk trial of a new bookmaker, GBets' no-deposit free bet is unique in this tier of SA bookmakers.

PSL Odds: GBets Edges on Value, Sunbet More Consistent

We tracked 20 PSL fixtures across the 2025–26 season to compare both platforms against the best available market price.

Sunbet was consistently competitive on the high-profile fixtures — Sundowns, Pirates, Chiefs, and the Cape Town City games that draw larger betting volumes. Their live odds refresh quickly, and the in-play betting interface is clean. One standout feature: Sunbet often offers early prices on PSL weekends (sometimes by Thursday), giving punters more time to shop around.

GBets was occasionally a fraction below Sunbet on the marquee fixtures, but routinely outperformed on smaller PSL teams — Stellenbosch FC, Richards Bay, Royal AM. If you specialise in value-hunting on lesser-watched matches, GBets' PSL coverage is worth bookmarking. Their multi-bet interface is also exceptionally fast to use — building a 4-leg PSL accumulator takes under a minute.

Lucky Numbers: GBets Is the Clear Winner

If Lucky Numbers is central to your betting, this decision is straightforward: use GBets.

GBets operates multiple Lucky Numbers draws throughout the day, including their own proprietary Goldrush draws alongside the standard Lotto, Daily Lotto, and Powerball. Bet types include straight, combination, and overall plays starting from as little as R1. The USSD option (*120*3652#) means you can play Lucky Numbers without data — a genuine advantage for millions of SA bettors.

Sunbet offers Lucky Numbers, but it's clearly not their core focus. The selection covers main national draws with limited bet types. No USSD support. If Lucky Numbers represents a meaningful chunk of your betting activity, Sunbet simply isn't the right platform.

Horse Racing: Sunbet's Hidden Strength

This one goes to Sunbet, and it's not particularly close. Sun International's deep connections to the South African horse racing industry — through their casino properties and partnerships — translate into a noticeably stronger racing product.

Sunbet covers all SA venues (Turffontein, Kenilworth, Greyville, Scottsville, Vaal) with full race fields, early prices, each-way betting, and exotic bets (exacta, trifecta). International racing from the UK, Australia, Hong Kong, and Dubai is also available with reasonable market depth.

GBets covers local SA racing adequately but international racing is thin and the bet types are more limited. For serious horse racing bettors, Sunbet is the better platform.

Loyalty Programmes: Two Different Systems

Sunbet connects to the Sun MVG (Most Valued Guest) loyalty programme — the same points system used at SunCity, The Table Bay, and other Sun International properties. If you also visit Sun International casinos or resorts, your Sunbet betting activity earns points redeemable across the whole ecosystem. For the Sun International regular, this is a genuine value-add. For someone who doesn't visit Sun properties, it's less compelling.

GBets uses the Goldrush Rewards programme. You earn points on sports bets, Lucky Numbers, and terminal betting at Goldrush outlets nationwide. Points can be redeemed for free bets or converted to cash. For bettors who use Goldrush terminals in shops, the cross-platform points accumulation is useful.

Both loyalty systems reward long-term use. Which one adds more value depends on your lifestyle — Sun International patron or Goldrush terminal user.

Withdrawal Speed: GBets Is Faster

GBets processed EFT withdrawals in 2–4 hours in our testing, frequently on the faster end during business hours. Their customer support team can expedite withdrawals on request during business hours if there's a delay.

Sunbet averaged 4–8 hours on EFT withdrawals, with requests after 3 PM sometimes processed the next business day. Their payment processing is consistent but slower than GBets. Capitec Pay withdrawals on both platforms were faster than EFT.

Both require FICA verification (ID, proof of address, bank confirmation) before your first withdrawal. Complete this when you register.

Mobile Apps

Sunbet has a clean, modern Android and iOS app. The interface reflects the Sun International brand — polished, corporate, and easy to navigate. In-play betting is smooth, and the horse racing section is the best part of the app. Minor criticism: the live betting section can be slightly slow to load during peak hours.

GBets app is well-optimised and fast. The Lucky Numbers section is front-and-centre (as it should be for their audience), and the PSL accumulator builder is genuinely one of the quickest in the SA market. The USSD betting option is a unique addition you won't find on Sunbet's app.

🏆 Our Verdict

Sunbet wins for sports bettors — better horse racing, faster early prices, lower bonus rollover, and a loyalty system that adds value if you're part of the Sun International ecosystem.

GBets wins for Lucky Numbers and accessibility — the strongest Lucky Numbers product in this tier, faster EFT withdrawals, USSD support, and a no-deposit welcome bet for new punters.

If you're a sports-first bettor: go Sunbet. If Lucky Numbers are your thing or you want a free trial: go GBets. Running accounts on both is a smart play for SA bettors who want options.
