Sunbet is the online arm of Sun International — the casino and hotel empire behind GrandWest, Sun City, and Time Square. Odds Quality: Who Pays Better?

Let's be honest: neither Sunbet nor Hollywoodbets is going to win any awards for having the sharpest odds in South Africa. Both cater to recreational punters rather than sharp bettors, and their margins reflect that. If you're looking for the tightest lines, Betway or Sportingbet will usually beat both.

That said, there are differences between Sunbet and Hollywoodbets.

On international football — Premier League, Champions League, La Liga — Sunbet tends to offer slightly better odds. They're not dramatically better, but over time, 1-2% better margins add up. For a Saturday Premier League accumulator, Sunbet will often give you a marginally better return than Hollywoodbets on the same selections.

For PSL matches, Hollywoodbets edges it. As the PSL's title sponsor (it's literally called the Betway Premiership, but Hollywoodbets is everywhere in the stadiums and broadcasts), they invest heavily in local football markets. Their PSL odds are competitive, and they often run boosted odds on marquee matches — Chiefs vs Pirates, Sundowns vs any top-four rival.

For rugby, it's close to a dead heat. Both offer standard markets (match result, handicap, totals) on URC and international matches. Neither has exceptional depth in rugby props.

Bottom line: Hollywoodbets for PSL, Sunbet for international football. Neither is best overall — that honour goes to Betway or Sportingbet.

Withdrawal Speed

Sunbet processes withdrawals within 24–48 hours for EFT. It's not instant like Betway, but it's consistent and predictable. Funds land in your bank account reliably within two business days. Sunbet supports all major SA banks — FNB, Standard Bank, Absa, Nedbank, Capitec.

Hollywoodbets' online withdrawals take 24–72 hours, which is a wider window and can feel frustrating when you're waiting for a payout after a good weekend. However, Hollywoodbets has one massive advantage: cash withdrawals at branches. If you live near one of their hundreds of outlets (they're everywhere in KZN and Gauteng), you can walk in and collect your winnings in person. That's effectively an instant withdrawal if you don't mind the trip.

For online-only punters, Sunbet is marginally faster. For punters near a Hollywoodbets branch, Hollywood's cash option is hard to beat.

Winner: Tie. Depends on whether you value online speed or cash accessibility.

Bonuses and Promotions

Sunbet's welcome bonus goes up to R1,000 as a matched deposit bonus. Deposit R1,000, get R1,000 in bonus funds. Standard wagering requirements apply — you'll need to turn the bonus over multiple times before withdrawing. It's a decent offer, especially if you're already planning to deposit that amount.

Hollywoodbets gives you R25 just for signing up. No deposit required. It's not much, but it's risk-free money. You can place a few bets, test the platform, and walk away with winnings if you get lucky. It's the lowest-barrier entry in SA betting.

Where things get interesting is ongoing promotions. Sunbet leverages its casino side — you'll often see cross-promotions between sports betting and their online casino games. If you enjoy both sports betting and casino games, Sunbet gives you more value from a single account.

Hollywoodbets runs regular promotions on PSL matches, horse racing (Lucky 7, Bet & Spin), and their Spina Zonke games. Their promotions feel more frequent and more varied, which keeps the platform engaging even during quiet midweek periods.

Winner: Sunbet for welcome bonus value, Hollywoodbets for ongoing promotions and no-risk entry.

Horse Racing: Hollywoodbets Dominates

This section could be one sentence: for horse racing, use Hollywoodbets.

But let's explain why. Hollywoodbets was literally born at the racetrack. Their horse racing product is the best in South Africa by a significant margin. They offer fixed odds, tote betting, and exotic bets (Swinger, Place Accumulator, Jackpot, Pick 6) across every SA racecourse — Turffontein, Greyville, Kenilworth, Fairview, Scottsville. They also cover international racing from the UK, Ireland, Australia, and Hong Kong.

The form guides are detailed, the interface is purpose-built for racing punters, and the market depth is unmatched. For the Durban July, the Met, the Summer Cup, or just a random Wednesday card at Turffontein, Hollywoodbets is where serious racing punters go.

Sunbet offers horse racing, and it's fine for casual bets. But the market range is smaller, the form information is more limited, and the exotic bet options are fewer. If horse racing is your main thing, Sunbet simply isn't in the same league.

Winner: Hollywoodbets, and it's not close. Plan your each-way bets with our each-way calculator.

PSL and Local Football

Hollywoodbets has invested heavily in South African football. They sponsor PSL clubs, advertise at stadiums, and their PSL markets are comprehensive. You'll find match result, correct score, both teams to score, goalscorer markets, and more on every Premiership fixture. During a Chiefs vs Pirates derby, Hollywoodbets often runs enhanced odds or special promotions.

Sunbet covers the PSL but with less fanfare. The basic markets are there — match result, over/under, handicap — but you won't find the same depth of props or the promotional push that Hollywoodbets offers. Sunbet's football focus is more international: Premier League, Champions League, La Liga.

If you're a PSL punter, Hollywoodbets is the better choice. If you primarily bet on international football, Sunbet offers better odds on those markets.

Winner: Hollywoodbets for PSL, Sunbet for international football.

Casino and Gaming Crossover

This is where Sunbet has a genuine edge that no other SA bookmaker can match.

Sunbet is part of Sun International, which operates some of South Africa's most iconic casino resorts — GrandWest in Cape Town, Carnival City in Gauteng, Sun City in the North West, Time Square in Pretoria. This means Sunbet's online casino offering is premium: hundreds of slots, table games (blackjack, roulette, baccarat), and a live dealer section that's among the best in SA.

If you enjoy both sports betting and casino games, having everything in one account with one balance is genuinely convenient. Sunbet also runs cross-promotions — bet on sport, get free spins on slots, that kind of thing.

Hollywoodbets has Spina Zonke (their slot games) and Lucky Numbers (lottery betting), which are hugely popular — especially in KZN and the Eastern Cape. But the casino game selection isn't as deep as Sunbet's, and there's no live dealer option. For pure casino gaming, Sunbet is the better platform.

Winner: Sunbet, significantly. If casino games matter to you, it's not even a comparison.

Mobile Experience

Sunbet doesn't have a native mobile app — they rely on a mobile-optimised website. It works fine in a browser, and you can add it to your home screen, but it doesn't feel as slick as a dedicated app. The mobile site loads reasonably fast and covers all the functionality of the desktop site.

Hollywoodbets has a dedicated Android app (no iOS app, but the mobile site works on iPhone) plus USSD betting. The USSD option is a game-changer for punters in areas with limited data — you can place bets with just a phone call or SMS, no internet required. In a country where data costs are still a real concern for many people, this matters.

Hollywoodbets' app is functional and reliable, though it's not the prettiest in the market. It handles bet placement, deposits, and withdrawals without any drama. The USSD option is something Sunbet simply doesn't offer.

Winner: Hollywoodbets for accessibility (app + USSD), though Sunbet's mobile site is perfectly usable.

The Verdict

Sunbet and Hollywoodbets serve different types of punters, and neither is objectively "better" — it depends on what you bet on and how you bet.

Sunbet is the choice for bettors who want a casino-sports hybrid experience with a credible, JSE-listed company behind it. If you enjoy live dealer blackjack between PSL fixtures and want slightly better odds on international football, Sunbet delivers.

Hollywoodbets is the choice for punters who value horse racing, local sport, and accessibility. The branch network, USSD betting, and no-deposit bonus make it the most accessible bookmaker in South Africa. For PSL and racing, it's the market leader.

FAQ

Is Sunbet safe and legitimate?

Yes. Sunbet is operated by Sun International, a JSE-listed company that has been in the South African gaming industry for decades. They hold all required gambling licences and are regulated by provincial gambling boards. Your money is as safe as it gets with an SA bookmaker.

Can I use Sunbet's casino games from my sports betting balance?

Yes. One of Sunbet's biggest advantages is a single wallet across sports betting and casino games. Deposit once, use your balance for both. This makes it easy to switch between a PSL bet and a few spins on the slots without transferring funds between accounts.

Does Hollywoodbets have an iPhone app?

Hollywoodbets doesn't have a native iOS app on the App Store. However, their mobile website works well on Safari and can be added to your home screen for an app-like experience. They do have a dedicated Android app available for download from their website.

Which bookmaker has faster withdrawals?

For online EFT withdrawals, Sunbet is marginally faster (24–48 hours vs 24–72 hours). However, Hollywoodbets offers cash withdrawals at their physical branches, which is effectively instant. For the fastest online payouts in SA, neither beats Betway's instant withdrawals.

Do I need to complete FICA for both?

Yes. All licensed South African bookmakers require FICA verification before processing withdrawals. You'll need your SA ID, proof of address, and sometimes proof of your payment method. Complete this as soon as you register to avoid delays when you want to withdraw.
