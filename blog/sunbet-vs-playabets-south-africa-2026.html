Background — Who Are These Bookmakers?

Sunbet

Sunbet is the online betting arm of Sun International, one of South Africa's largest hospitality and gaming companies. If you've been to Sun City, GrandWest, or Carnival City, you've been to a Sun International property. That brand weight matters — it means Sunbet has serious financial backing, a well-established compliance infrastructure, and a reputation to protect.

Sunbet launched their online sportsbook in 2018 and has steadily improved since. They're not trying to be the cheapest or flashiest bookmaker — they're going for a slightly more premium, reliable experience. Think of them as the Pick n Pay to Hollywoodbets' Shoprite — not the cheapest, but dependable and trustworthy.

Playabets

Playabets is a smaller, independent operation that's been growing steadily in the SA market. They're licensed through the Mpumalanga Economic Regulator and have built a reputation as a solid no-nonsense bookmaker, particularly popular with football punters and accumulator enthusiasts.

Where Sunbet leans on corporate backing, Playabets competes on value — slightly better odds, bigger bonuses, and a wider range of markets. They're the hungry underdog, and that competitive energy often benefits the punter.

Sports Markets — What Can You Bet On?

Both bookmakers cover the major sports that SA punters care about — soccer (PSL, EPL, Champions League), rugby (URC, Springboks), cricket (Proteas, IPL, SA20), horse racing, and tennis. But the depth varies:

Sunbet Markets

  • Good coverage of major sports
  • Strong on horse racing — covers all SA racecourses plus international meetings
  • Decent prop markets for big events
  • Limited coverage of niche sports (MMA, esports)
  • Live betting is a standout — good selection of in-play markets

Playabets Markets

  • Excellent soccer coverage — more markets per match than Sunbet
  • Strong accumulator features with boosted odds promos
  • Good coverage of American sports (NBA, NFL, MLB)
  • Decent horse racing but fewer international meetings
  • Better selection of exotic/prop bets

Winner: Playabets — they simply offer more markets, especially for football. If you're the kind of punter who likes betting on corners, cards, both teams to score, or first goalscorer, Playabets gives you more options. Sunbet wins on horse racing depth though, so it depends on your sport.

Live Betting

Live betting (also called in-play) is where you place bets while a match is happening. It's fast, exciting, and requires a responsive platform. Here's where Sunbet genuinely pulls ahead.

Sunbet's live betting is noticeably smoother. The odds update quickly, the interface doesn't lag (much), and there's a solid selection of in-play markets for major sports. You get live stats, a decent visual representation of play, and reasonably fast bet acceptance.

Playabets' live betting works but feels a step behind. The odds updates can be sluggish, particularly during busy fixtures, and the range of in-play markets is narrower. It's functional, not exceptional.

Winner: Sunbet — if you're a live bettor, this is one of Sunbet's genuine strengths. The Sun International tech infrastructure seems to give them an edge here.

Mobile Experience

Let's be real — most SA punters bet on their phones. So mobile matters.

Sunbet has a proper native app for both Android and iOS. It's clean, loads reasonably fast, and mirrors the desktop experience well. The live betting section works better on the app than the mobile browser version. It's not going to win any design awards, but it does the job without frustration.

Playabets has an Android app and a responsive mobile website for iOS users. The mobile site is actually pretty good — it loads quickly and bet placement is straightforward. The design is more basic than Sunbet's, but some punters prefer that no-frills approach.

Winner: Draw — both work fine. Sunbet has a slight edge with the iOS app, but Playabets' mobile site is good enough that it's not a dealbreaker.

Lucky Numbers

Lucky Numbers — where you bet on the outcomes of international lottery draws — is massive in South Africa. Both Sunbet and Playabets offer it, but there are differences.

Sunbet offers the standard SA draws (UK 49s, SA Daily Lotto, various international lotteries) with competitive payouts. The interface is clean and easy to navigate.

Playabets offers a wider selection of draws, including some less common international lotteries. They also tend to run Lucky Numbers-specific promotions occasionally.

Winner: Playabets — more draws, similar payouts. If Lucky Numbers is your thing, Playabets gives you more choice.

Horse Racing

Horse racing is where Sunbet quietly outperforms most mid-tier bookmakers. The Sun International connection — they own or partner with several casino-racecourse complexes — gives them natural strength here.

Sunbet covers all South African race meetings (Turffontein, Kenilworth, Greyville, Hollywoodpark) plus major international racing (UK, Ireland, Australia, Hong Kong, Dubai). Their odds are competitive, and they offer exotics (Place Accumulator, Pick 6) alongside standard win/place/each-way markets.

Playabets covers SA racing and some international meetings but with less depth. The exotic markets are more limited, and odds are generally a fraction shorter than Sunbet on horse racing.

Winner: Sunbet — comfortably. If horse racing is important to you, Sunbet is the better choice between these two.

Customer Support

Neither bookmaker is going to wow you with customer support — that's pretty standard in the SA market. Both offer:

  • Email support (response within 24 hours typically)
  • Live chat during business hours
  • Phone support
  • Social media (Facebook, Twitter/X)

Sunbet's support feels slightly more professional — probably the corporate backing again. Playabets' support is decent but can be slow during peak hours. Neither is terrible, neither is exceptional.

Winner: Sunbet — marginally, based on consistency and professionalism.

Security & Licensing

Both are fully licensed and legal in South Africa:

  • Sunbet: Licensed under the Gauteng Gambling Board. Backed by Sun International, a JSE-listed company. About as financially secure as an SA bookmaker gets.
  • Playabets: Licensed through the Mpumalanga Economic Regulator. Smaller operation but fully compliant with South African gambling regulations.

Your money is safe with both. If corporate backing and brand size matter to you, Sunbet has the edge. But Playabets has never had any notable regulatory issues.

🏆 The Final Verdict

Choose Sunbet if: You value live betting, horse racing, brand trust, and easier bonus terms. Sunbet is the safer, more polished choice — ideal for punters who want reliability over raw value.

Choose Playabets if: You're a soccer punter who wants the best odds, widest markets, and biggest bonuses. Playabets offers more bang for your buck if you're focused on football and accumulators.

Our recommendation: If we had to pick one, Playabets edges it for the average SA punter — most people bet primarily on soccer, and that's where Playabets is stronger. But honestly? Open accounts with both. They're free, and you can always compare odds before placing your bet.

Frequently Asked Questions

Is Sunbet or Playabets better for soccer betting in South Africa?

Playabets edges ahead for soccer betting. They offer more markets per match, slightly better odds on PSL and international football, and their accumulator feature is stronger. Sunbet is still solid but has fewer exotic soccer markets.

Which has better odds — Sunbet or Playabets?

It's close, but Playabets generally offers slightly better odds on football and popular sports. Sunbet tends to be more competitive on horse racing and live betting markets. We recommend comparing odds on specific events using the BetSorted best odds finder.

Which bookmaker pays out faster — Sunbet or Playabets?

Both process withdrawals within 24–48 hours via EFT. Sunbet has a slight reputation for faster processing, likely due to Sun International's established financial infrastructure. Neither is known for problematic payouts.

Do Sunbet and Playabets both offer Lucky Numbers?

Yes, both Sunbet and Playabets offer Lucky Numbers betting, which lets you bet on numbers drawn in various international lotteries. Both offer similar draws and payout structures, though Playabets has a slightly wider selection of available draws.

Are Sunbet and Playabets licensed in South Africa?

Yes. Both hold valid licences from South African provincial gambling boards. Sunbet operates under Sun International's Gauteng Gambling Board licence, while Playabets is licensed through the Mpumalanga Economic Regulator. Both are fully legal and regulated.
