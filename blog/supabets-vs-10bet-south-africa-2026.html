Sports Coverage: Where Each Bookie Shines

PSL Betting: SupaBets Takes It

For PSL punters, SupaBets is hard to beat. They offer 80+ markets per match including goals, corners, cards, first goalscorer, and same-game multis. The odds are competitive, and the mobile app updates instantly during live matches.

10bet covers the PSL well too, but with fewer markets (around 40 per match) and slightly weaker odds on niche bets like "total corners" or "both teams to score in both halves." If Kaizer Chiefs vs Orlando Pirates is your bread and butter, SupaBets gets the nod.

Rugby: 10bet Wins for Springboks and Beyond

When it comes to rugby, 10bet pulls ahead. They offer deeper markets on URC, Six Nations, Rugby Championship, and international fixtures. For example, a Springboks vs New Zealand match will have 100+ markets on 10bet compared to 60+ on SupaBets.

10bet also offers better odds on rugby accumulators and exotic bets like "first try scorer" or "total tries over/under." SupaBets is solid for local rugby (Bulls, Stormers, Sharks), but 10bet covers the global game better.

International Football: 10bet Edges Ahead

Both bookies cover EPL, La Liga, Serie A, and Champions League, but 10bet typically offers 5-10% better odds on accumulators and sharper lines on big matches. For a Liverpool vs Man City accumulator leg, that difference adds up.

SupaBets is competitive on match winners and over/under goals, but 10bet gives you more markets per match (corners, cards, player props) and better value on multi-leg bets.

Cricket: Both Solid, SupaBets Slightly Better for SA Matches

For Proteas cricket and IPL betting, both bookies are neck-and-neck. SupaBets has a slight edge on local matches (like Proteas vs Australia T20s) with more granular markets (runs in powerplay, fall of next wicket), while 10bet shines on international tournaments like the Cricket World Cup.

Mobile Experience: SupaBets is Faster, 10bet is Richer

SupaBets has one of the fastest betting apps in SA. It's lightweight, loads in under 2 seconds, and works perfectly on older phones or slower data connections. The interface is clean, placing bets takes 3 taps, and live betting updates are instant.

10bet's app is feature-rich but slightly heavier. It includes live streaming, bet builder tools, and detailed stats, which is great if you're on WiFi or have a strong LTE connection. The trade-off is it can be slower on 3G or budget devices.

Verdict: If you bet on-the-go with limited data, SupaBets wins. If you want bells and whistles and don't mind using a bit more data, 10bet delivers.

Bonuses and Promotions

SupaBets now pushes a broader welcome package: R50 free bet + 100 free spins + up to R5,000. They also run regular promotions like "Multi Boost" (extra winnings on 4+ leg accumulators) and "Bet and Get" offers during big PSL weekends.

10bet: 100% First Deposit Match up to R5,000

10bet's current headline is bigger than older pages suggested: 100% first deposit match up to R5,000. The catch is that the 6x rollover applies to deposit plus bonus at 2.00+ odds, which creates a much bigger real turnover target than the headline alone suggests.

Their ongoing promos include "Acca Insurance" (get your stake back if one leg fails on 5+ accumulators) and "Price Boosts" on selected rugby and football matches.

Verdict: SupaBets is better if you want more moving parts and smaller-entry promo value. 10bet is better if you are depositing serious money and are comfortable with a much heavier rollover target.

Deposits and Withdrawals: Speed Matters

SupaBets: Instant Deposits, Fast Withdrawals

SupaBets supports Instant EFT, Ozow, and card payments. Deposits are instant, and withdrawals via Instant EFT hit your bank in 1-6 hours. Ozow withdrawals can take up to 24 hours.

The minimum deposit is just R5, making it accessible for casual punters or those testing small bets.

10bet: Reliable, But Slower Withdrawals

10bet accepts EFT, Visa, Mastercard, and e-wallets. Deposits are instant, but withdrawals take 12-48 hours depending on the method. EFT is fastest (12-24 hours), while cards can take up to 48 hours.

The minimum deposit is R50, which is higher than SupaBets but still reasonable.

Verdict: SupaBets wins on withdrawal speed and accessibility. If you need your winnings quickly or want to bet with small stakes, SupaBets is the better choice.
