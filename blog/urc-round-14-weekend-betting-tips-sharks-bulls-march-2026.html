Why the Sharks Should Dominate

This should be a comfortable night for the Sharks. Cardiff have been one of the weaker sides in this season's URC, and their away record against South African opposition is poor. The Durban heat and humidity in late March will sap the Welsh side's energy, especially in the second half.

  • Sharks home record: Strong this season with physical, aggressive pack play
  • Cardiff away form: Struggled against SA teams — the travel and conditions take a toll
  • Key players: Expect the Sharks' Springbok contingent to be rested from international duty for this club fixture
  • Weather factor: Durban humidity favours the home side who are accustomed to it

Match 2: Bulls vs Munster (Saturday 13:00)

🐂 Bulls vs Munster 🔴

Venue: Loftus Versfeld, Pretoria

Kick-off: Saturday, 13:00 SAST

💡 BetSorted Tip: Bulls to Win & Over 35.5 Total Points @ 2.20 — Loftus is a fortress and altitude opens games up

The Battle at Loftus

This is the marquee fixture of the weekend. Munster are a far sterner test than Cardiff — they're one of Ireland's traditional powerhouses with a proud European and URC pedigree. But they're coming to Loftus Versfeld at altitude, and the Bulls at home are a different beast.

  • Altitude factor: Pretoria sits at 1,350m — European teams consistently struggle with the thin air, especially in the second half
  • Bulls home form: Dominant at Loftus this season with a pack that bullies opposition at set pieces
  • Munster quality: Don't write them off — they have international-class players and big-game experience
  • Historical context: SA teams have a strong record against Irish provinces at home, though Munster have pulled off upsets before
