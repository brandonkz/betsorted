What's at Stake: URC Playoff Picture

With only Round 20 to come after this weekend, let's spell out what each SA team needs:

  • Stormers (2nd, ~54pts): Win at Scotstoun = realistic shot at finishing first and getting a home quarter-final. Loss = likely locked into 2nd or 3rd.
  • Lions (5th, ~46pts): Win vs Munster = almost certainly secure a top-four home QF. Loss = sweating on other results and possibly dropping to 5th-8th for an away trip.
  • Bulls (8th, ~43pts): Win at Loftus = likely secured in playoffs. Loss = things get hairy depending on Edinburgh, Cardiff, and Ospreys results.
  • Sharks (10th, ~36pts): Win = keeps mathematical hope alive. Still needs a massive collapse from teams above them. Realistically this is season done, but they'll fight anyway.

The URC playoff format takes the top eight teams into quarter-finals, with 1v8, 2v7, 3v6, and 4v5 matchups. Home advantage in the quarters goes to the higher seed — which is exactly why the Lions vs Munster match is so pivotal. Home QFs in SA are near-impossible for visiting teams given the altitude.
