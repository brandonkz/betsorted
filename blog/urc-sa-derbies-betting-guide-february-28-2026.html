URC SA Derbies Betting Guide: Lions vs Stormers & Bulls vs Sharks (February 28, 2026)

Published February 27, 2026 • 9 min read • Brandon Katz

Two SA derbies on the same day. Lions hosting Stormers at Ellis Park, Bulls welcoming the Sharks to Loftus. This is the kind of Saturday that makes URC rugby in South Africa special — and if you're betting on it, you need to get your homework done before kickoff.

Here's our full breakdown of both matches: form, key matchups, markets worth considering, and a couple of accumulator ideas to keep things interesting. Check our odds comparison page for the latest prices before you lock anything in.

Match 1: Golden Lions vs DHL Stormers — Ellis Park, Johannesburg

The Setup

The Lions at Ellis Park have been a different beast this season. The altitude advantage is real — visiting teams from the coast consistently struggle with the pace of the game at 1,750 metres above sea level. The Stormers travel well generally, but Johannesburg has been a bogey venue for Cape Town sides in the URC.

The Lions have shown flashes of brilliance at home this campaign, with their loose forwards creating havoc at the breakdown and their backline looking sharper than it has in years. Their set piece has been more consistent too, which gives them a genuine platform to attack from.

The Stormers, meanwhile, are grinding through a transitional phase. They've still got quality across the park — their defence is typically Stormers-tough — but their attack hasn't consistently fired this season. The loss of key players to Springbok duties earlier in the campaign disrupted their combinations, and they're still finding their rhythm.

Key Players to Watch

  • Lions: Their flyhalf's goal-kicking has been crucial at home. Watch the loose trio — they've been dominant in the contact area and could exploit the Stormers' breakdown.
  • Stormers: The pack is where they'll try to win this. If their maul functions at Ellis Park and they can slow the Lions' tempo, they've got a chance. Their centres have been solid defensively all season.

Match 2: Vodacom Bulls vs Cell C Sharks — Loftus Versfeld, Pretoria

The Setup

The big one. Bulls vs Sharks at Loftus is always a proper derby — physical, intense, and usually decided by fine margins. Both teams have playoff ambitions, which means neither can afford to drop points here.

The Bulls at Loftus are formidable. Their forward pack has been one of the best in the URC this season, and Loftus — like Ellis Park — sits at altitude. The Bulls' driving maul has been a consistent weapon, and their defence has been stingy at home. They don't give you easy points at Loftus.

The Sharks have improved markedly this season. Their recruitment has added depth, and they've found a playing identity that suits their personnel. They're dangerous on the counter-attack and their kicking game is smart. The question is whether they can sustain that for 80 minutes at altitude.

Key Players to Watch

  • Bulls: The front row sets the tone. If the Bulls scrum dominates, the rest follows. Their 10-12 combination has been creating space through inside channels all season.
  • Sharks: Watch their back three. If the Sharks can turn the Bulls around with contestable kicks and win the aerial battle, they'll create scoring opportunities. Their hooker has been exceptional at lineout time.

Quick Form Check

Lions Recent Form (Home)

The Lions have been strong at Ellis Park this URC season, winning more than they've lost on home turf. Their attacking game suits the altitude — they play at tempo and look to tire visiting packs in the final quarter. When they get front-foot ball, their backline can be devastating. The concern? Discipline. They've given away too many penalties in key moments this season.

Stormers Recent Form (Away)

Away from Newlands — sorry, Cape Town Stadium — the Stormers have been inconsistent. They've picked up some good results on the road but have also been caught out when their set piece doesn't fire. Travelling to the Highveld is always their toughest away trip in South Africa.

Bulls Recent Form (Home)

Loftus has been a fortress. The Bulls' home record this season is one of the best in the URC, built on a dominant pack and suffocating defence. They're clinical at converting pressure into points, particularly through their driving maul and goal-kicking.

Sharks Recent Form (Away)

The Sharks have been competitive on the road, but Pretoria is a different challenge to playing in Ireland or Scotland. The altitude factor is real, and the Sharks have historically struggled at Loftus more than any other SA venue. That said, this Sharks side is better equipped to deal with it than previous squads.

FAQ

What time do the URC SA derbies kick off on February 28?

Both matches are scheduled for Saturday, February 28, 2026. Check the URC website or your bookmaker's app for exact kickoff times, as these can shift for TV scheduling. Lions vs Stormers is typically the earlier game, with Bulls vs Sharks in the evening slot.

Which bookmaker has the best odds for URC rugby?

It varies by match and market. Betway generally offers the widest range of URC markets and best live betting. Sportingbet sometimes has sharper pre-match odds on SA derbies. Use our odds comparison tool to check before placing your bet.

Does altitude really affect URC rugby results?

Absolutely. Both Ellis Park (Johannesburg) and Loftus Versfeld (Pretoria) sit at roughly 1,400-1,750 metres above sea level. Visiting teams, especially coastal sides like the Stormers and Sharks, often struggle in the second half as the altitude takes its toll. This is a genuine factor that smart bettors account for.

Can I bet on try scorers in URC matches?

Yes. Betway offers first try scorer, last try scorer, and anytime try scorer markets for most URC matches. Hollywoodbets and Sportingbet offer anytime try scorer markets on the bigger fixtures. These are fun markets but inherently volatile — rugby tries can come from anywhere.
