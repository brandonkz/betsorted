Saturday PSL Action — 18 April 2026

Saturday's Betway Premiership card is stacked. Four matches spread across the afternoon and evening mean you can build a proper acca or dip into individual games for value.

Orlando Pirates vs AmaZulu

Kick-off: 15:00 SAST | Venue: Orlando Stadium

Pirates have been one of the form teams of the second half of the season and host an AmaZulu side still fighting for mid-table respectability. The Sea Robbers are coming off a strong performance in midweek and have key striker Evidence Makgopa leading the line with real confidence. AmaZulu have been leaky at the back away from home — conceding in eight of their last ten away fixtures.

Our tip: Orlando Pirates Win — @1.75 (Betway)

Value play: Pirates to score over 1.5 goals — @2.10. Their front three have been lethal at Orlando Stadium this season.

Polokwane City vs Kaizer Chiefs

Kick-off: 17:30 SAST | Venue: Peter Mokaba Stadium

Kaizer Chiefs desperately need points to keep their top-eight ambitions alive heading into the final stretch. Polokwane City are no pushovers at home but have shown vulnerability to set pieces and direct play. Chiefs coach Nasreddine Nabi has drilled his squad well in attack — they've won four of their last six away matches.

Our tip: Kaizer Chiefs to Win — @2.00 (Hollywoodbets)

A confident selection for your acca slip.

Marumo Gallants vs Siwelele

Kick-off: 15:00 SAST

Marumo Gallants have been solid at home this season, and Siwelele (the club formerly known as Chippa United) have had an inconsistent campaign. Both sides are battling in the lower half of the table. Expect a tight, physical encounter.

Our tip: Both Teams to Score — @1.90. When these sides meet, goals tend to come from both ends.

Orbit College vs Golden Arrows

Kick-off: 15:00 SAST

Orbit College are one of the division's promoted sides working hard to secure their top-flight status. Golden Arrows have far more quality across the park and should be too much to handle away from home.

Our tip: Golden Arrows Win — @1.80

Sunday PSL — 19 April 2026

Two matches round out the Betway Premiership weekend card on Sunday.

TS Galaxy vs Richards Bay

Kick-off: 15:00 SAST | Venue: Mbombela Stadium

TS Galaxy have been unpredictable this season but have real quality on the flanks. Richards Bay are fighting relegation and rarely take points away from home. Galaxy have won three of their last five at Mbombela and should be too strong here.

Our tip: TS Galaxy Win & Over 1.5 Goals — @2.00

Magesi vs Durban City

Kick-off: 17:30 SAST

Magesi are one of this season's surprises — they've punched above their weight all campaign. Durban City (formerly Maritzburg United) have shown flashes of quality but can be breached on the counter. A close one to call, but home advantage counts here.

Our tip: Magesi Win or Draw (Double Chance) — @1.65

Stormers vs Connacht — Saturday 12:45 SAST

The Stormers are second in the overall standings and still have a genuine shot at the URC title this season. Chris Smith has been outstanding at flyhalf, currently sitting as the URC's top point scorer with 106 points for the season. Connacht come to Cape Town on the back of a mixed run and will struggle to contain the Stormers' expansive attack at DHL Newlands.

Our tip: Stormers Win by 13+ points — @1.85 (Betway)

The Stormers have been dominant at home, and Connacht lack the firepower to keep pace if the Cape side play to their best.

Bulls vs Dragons — Saturday 19:45 SAST

The Vodacom Bulls host the Dragons at Loftus Versfeld. The Bulls (8th on 40 points) need a big win to keep their playoff place secure, and the Dragons are one of the weakest sides in the competition this season — they've conceded over 100 points to SA teams alone. Loftus tends to play big in night games and the Bulls faithful will push their side hard.

Our tip: Bulls Win & Over 3.5 tries — @2.20

The Bulls have scored 4+ tries in six of their last eight home matches. Value here.

Lions Watch

The Lions (5th, 43 points) don't play this weekend but will be watching results closely. A Stormers win and a Bulls win could affect the SA Shield standings heading into the final rounds. The Lions need to close the gap on Leinster and Ulster to lock in a top-four playoff position.

Weekend Betting Tips for SA Punters

1. Shop Around for the Best Odds

Don't just default to one bookmaker. The spread between Betway, Hollywoodbets, Sportingbet, and Supabets on PSL matches can be significant. On a R500 bet, even a small odds difference adds up. Compare South Africa's best betting sites →

2. Use Acca Insurance

Both Betway and Hollywoodbets offer acca insurance on accumulators of 5 or more legs. If one leg lets you down, you get your stake back as a free bet. Always check the terms — some offers require minimum odds per leg of 1.40.

3. Look for In-Play Value

If you can't decide pre-game, wait for kick-off. PSL matches often see significant in-play swings in the first 20 minutes. Big sides that concede early (like Pirates or Chiefs) often rally — live odds can offer real value in those moments.

4. Bet on What You Know

PSL bettors tend to outperform when they stick to the league they watch regularly. If you follow the Betway Premiership weekly, trust your knowledge over following tips from Twitter "experts."

5. Set a Weekend Budget

Decide your total weekend betting budget before Saturday morning. Spread it across your selections — don't blow it all on the first match. Our Bankroll Tracker can help you stay disciplined.

6. Check the Weather for Rugby

Cape Town in mid-April can bring afternoon showers. A wet Newlands surface tends to favour the team with the stronger pack and direct game — which helps Stormers' case against Connacht. Loftus at night in autumn is also notoriously cold — high-scoring games are less likely in the cold, so consider under markets for the Bulls match if you're looking for an alternative angle.
