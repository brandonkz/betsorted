Why the 2026 Format Is a Bettor's Dream

The expanded 48-team format isn't just good for smaller nations – it's genuinely good for bettors. Here's why:

  • More open groups: With only 4 teams per group and 3 qualifying, every game matters but nothing is ever truly over after one loss.
  • Third-place qualification: The 8 best third-place teams advance, creating fascinating final-day scenarios where teams might strategically manage results.
  • 72 group games total: That's 72 betting markets with pre-match, live, and special bets – compared to 48 at previous tournaments.
  • More upsets: Lesser nations have a genuine chance. Scotland beating Brazil? Just happened. That creates value lines across the board.
💡 Bettor's Tip: In this format, look for teams who lost their first game but face another first-game loser in round 2. These games are intense, often under-priced on the draw, and historically produce goalscoring action both ways. Think: Bafana vs Czechia.

Best World Cup Betting Markets for SA Bettors

Match Result (1X2)

The straightforward win/draw/lose. Good for backing neutralised favourites or upsets. In a World Cup group stage where every point matters, draws are more common than in the knockout rounds.

Both Teams to Score (BTTS)

Especially relevant in "must-win" matchday 2 and 3 games where one or both teams is already eliminated or desperate. BTTS Yes has been a strong performer in this tournament's early rounds.

Asian Handicap

Great way to back a likely winner without the full outright price risk. If you like Germany to beat Ecuador but don't want to lay 1.30, Germany -1.5 Asian handicap offers much better value at 1.85+.

Over/Under Goals

Group stage World Cup games often see conservative first halves from cautious coaches. Under 2.5 goals is a decent value in certain matchups – particularly where both teams are competing for qualification and neither wants to expose themselves.

Half-Time / Full-Time

Strong teams in must-win positions often start slow and win in the second half. The Germany game was an example – but so was Mexico vs Bafana. The H/T 0–0 / FT Away Win was a result combination that paid well.

First Goalscorer / Anytime Scorer

World Cup-level strikers are in form, motivated, and facing sometimes weaker defences. Anytime scorer markets for top forwards like Mbappé, Lewandowski, Schick offer consistent value.

World Cup Accumulator Strategy for SA Bettors

World Cups are accumulator gold. Every day has 3–4 games, meaning you can build daily accas or specific session accas (afternoon games, evening games). Here's how SA bettors approach this smartly:

  1. Don't back heavy favourites at odds-on in your acca. Brazil at 1.25, France at 1.30 – one draw kills your whole slip. Back them at Asian handicap prices instead (usually 1.75–1.90).
  2. Mix result markets with goals markets. A "Germany win + over 3.5 goals" combo in their opener would've paid well. Look for dominant teams facing weak opposition.
  3. Use the BetSorted accumulator calculator here to see exactly what your acca returns before committing.
  4. Limit accas to 4–6 selections. The more legs, the more can go wrong. Disciplined 4-leg accas outperform 10-leg dream tickets over time.
  5. Track your bets. Use BetSorted's bankroll tracker – World Cups can be expensive if you bet every game emotionally.
