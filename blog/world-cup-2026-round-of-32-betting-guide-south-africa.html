World Cup 2026 Round of 32: Complete Betting Guide for SA Bettors

18 June 2026 · Updated as group stage progresses · 12 min read

The FIFA World Cup 2026 introduced something new — a Round of 32 that's changed how the tournament flows. With 48 teams instead of 32, there's now an extra knockout stage between the group stage and the Round of 16. For South African bettors, this creates more markets, more matches to bet on, and more chances to build profitable accumulators.

This guide breaks down the format, which teams to watch, the best betting strategies for knockout football, and where to get the sharpest odds in South Africa.

The New Format Explained

Here's how the 48-team 2026 World Cup works in plain English:

  • 12 groups of 4 teams — each team plays 3 games
  • Top 2 from each group qualify automatically (24 teams)
  • Best 8 third-place teams also qualify (8 more teams)
  • 32 teams total enter the Round of 32 (June 28 – July 3)
  • From there: Round of 16 → Quarterfinals → Semis → Final (July 19)
What this means for bettors: There are now 32 knockout matches (up from 16 in old format) before the Round of 16. More matches = more markets = more opportunities. The Round of 32 stage often produces giant-killing results because third-place qualifiers are hungrier and sometimes face overconfident group winners.

Betting Strategies for Knockout Football

Knockout football is different from group stage betting. Here's what actually works in the Round of 32 and beyond:

1. Bet Totals (Over/Under Goals), Not Just Match Results

In knockout matches, teams play more conservatively — especially Round of 32 clashes where a draw leads to extra time. Under 2.5 goals tends to cash more often in early knockout rounds than in the group stage. Groups favourites against third-place qualifiers are the exception — expect those matches to be more one-sided.

2. Asian Handicaps Open Up Value

When a clear favourite (e.g. France vs a third-place qualifier) is priced at 1.20–1.30, the value disappears. Instead, take the favourite on an Asian Handicap (-1.5 goals) to increase your return. Hollywoodbets and Betway both offer Asian Handicap markets on all World Cup knockouts.

3. Both Teams to Score (BTTS) in the 80th Minute Market

This is an underrated live betting play. When the score is 1-0 with 80 minutes gone, the trailing team commits to attack. The in-play BTTS "Yes" market often offers 2.50+ — solid value when both teams have already shown attacking quality.

4. Accumulators: Pick 3-4 Heavy Favourites

Four 1.30 favourites combined gives you roughly 2.86 — meaning you nearly triple your money on four "safe" picks. The trick is choosing Round of 32 matchups where a clear favourite is playing a third-place qualifier from a weaker group.
