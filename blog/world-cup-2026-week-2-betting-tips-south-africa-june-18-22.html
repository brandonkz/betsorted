Thursday June 18 — Other Key Matches

Switzerland vs Bosnia and Herzegovina (19:00 SAST)

Group B · SoFi Stadium, Los Angeles

Switzerland drew 1-1 with Qatar in Game 1. Bosnia also drew in their opener. Switzerland are the stronger side on paper — Granit Xhaka marshalling a well-drilled midfield, Zeki Amdouni as the attacking threat. Bosnia play without fear when they have nothing to lose.

Pick: Switzerland Asian Handicap -0.5 (effectively Win) at ~1.80. Switzerland are the more consistent side and shouldn't be dropping points here if they're serious about advancing.

Mexico vs South Korea (03:00 SAST Friday)

Group A · MetLife Stadium, New Jersey

Mexico already beat South Africa 2-0, South Korea beat Czechia 2-1. A draw suits both teams if Czechia beat SA. Expect a cagey affair. If Bafana win earlier in the day, both these teams may play more conservatively knowing they're already through.

Pick: Under 2.5 Goals at ~1.85. Late-night SAST kickoff, both teams have already shown they can score — but motivation in a group where they're likely both advancing either way may dampen the attacking intent.

Friday June 19 — Big Names Continue

Germany & England Fixtures (Groups D & E)

Week 2 brings Germany, England, Spain and Portugal into action for their second group games. These sides are largely expected to progress — but second matches are where upsets happen.

Germany Watch: Germany looked solid in Week 1 and should dominate their second opponent. Back them on the Asian Handicap (-1) to increase returns over the short match winner price.

England Watch: Jude Bellingham has been involved in virtually every England attacking move. If he starts, England are dangerous. Back England to win and Bellingham to have a shot on target as a special/combo market — good value at Betway.

Weekend June 21-22 — Final Group 2 Matchday Begins

By the weekend, some groups are already decided (or down to the wire). Third-round group games are often the most chaotic to bet — teams need specific results and sometimes the "dead rubber" effect kicks in for already-qualified sides. Be careful here: bet on groups where qualification is undecided, not already-sealed matchdays.

Bafana vs South Korea (Date TBC — June 22)

If Bafana win or draw vs Czechia today, they go into the South Korea match with real motivation. South Korea beat Czechia 2-1 and are dangerous on the counter. This will be a pressure cooker for SA fans.

Watch for: Player bookings and disciplinary status after the Mexico red cards. SA cannot afford another man sent off.

Early odds: South Korea favoured at ~2.20. SA at ~3.20. Draw ~3.00. Value to look at: Bafana Win (if they beat Czechia today, momentum and confidence swing dramatically — the price may undervalue SA's form).

Week 2 Tips: What to Avoid

  • Don't chase Week 1 losses: If your Week 1 accas missed, don't increase stakes to recover. Bigger bets, same World Cup, same variance.
  • Avoid betting on dead rubber group matches where one team is already guaranteed to advance. They'll rest key players.
  • Be wary of "sure things": Week 1 of this World Cup already showed Brazil, Spain and Germany can all be vulnerable. The short prices don't always represent certainty.
  • Wait for team news: Several sides had injury scares in Week 1. Starting XIs announced 1 hour before kickoff can completely change the value proposition.
