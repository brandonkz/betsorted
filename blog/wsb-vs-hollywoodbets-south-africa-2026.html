The Brand Story: Two SA Originals

Before we dive into odds and payouts, it's worth understanding what makes these two bookmakers different at their core.

World Sports Betting is a Cape Town institution. Founded in 2002, they've built their reputation on being a straightforward, no-gimmicks bookmaker. They're particularly well-known in the Western Cape, and their sponsorship of Newlands Cricket Ground (now officially WSB Newlands) gives them serious brand visibility. WSB has always been a "punter's bookmaker" — less marketing glitz, more focus on competitive odds and reliable payouts.

Hollywoodbets started in KwaZulu-Natal as a horse racing bookmaker, and that heritage still defines them. They've since expanded into sports betting, casino games, Lucky Numbers, and even international football sponsorship (Brentford FC in the English Premier League). Their network of physical branches across SA gives them a presence that no other online bookmaker matches — you can walk into a Hollywoodbets branch, deposit cash, and place your bet face-to-face. That matters in a country where not everyone has easy access to online banking.

Odds Comparison: Who Pays Better?

PSL (Betway Premiership)

On PSL matches, Hollywoodbets consistently offers competitive odds. They run regular promos on big PSL fixtures — enhanced odds on Soweto derbies, bonus payouts on accumulators, and first goalscorer specials. On a standard PSL match like Mamelodi Sundowns vs Stellenbosch, Hollywoodbets' main market odds are typically tight and competitive.

WSB is no slouch though. Their PSL odds are solid, and they sometimes offer better prices on less popular matches — the Chippa United vs Sekhukhune games that don't get as much attention from the bigger bookmakers. If you're betting on the full PSL fixture list (not just the big games), WSB is worth checking every round.

Rugby (URC, Springboks)

Hollywoodbets covers rugby well — URC matches, Springbok Tests, the Rugby Championship, and major international tournaments. Their rugby odds are competitive, and they offer a reasonable range of markets including handicaps, try scorers, and half-time results.

WSB's rugby coverage is decent but slightly less deep. You'll get the main markets on all major rugby matches, and their odds are competitive on Springbok Tests. But for things like individual try scorer markets on a URC match, Hollywoodbets usually offers more options.

Horse Racing: Hollywoodbets' Kingdom

This isn't even close. Hollywoodbets is the best horse racing bookmaker in South Africa. Full stop. They cover every SA racecourse — Turffontein, Greyville, Kenilworth, Scottsville, Fairview, Flamingo Park — with comprehensive fixed-odds betting, win and place markets, Swinger bets, Pick 6, Jackpot, and all the exotic bet types that serious racing punters want. They also offer live streaming of races, which is a massive plus.

WSB covers horse racing across all major SA tracks and their odds are fair. But the depth of bet types, the quality of form information, and the live streaming all tilt heavily in Hollywoodbets' favour. If horse racing is a significant part of your betting, Hollywoodbets is the obvious choice.

Cricket

Both bookmakers cover international cricket — Proteas Tests, ODIs, T20Is, SA20, and major world events. Hollywoodbets edges it with more cricket betting markets and slightly tighter margins. WSB covers the headline matches well but doesn't go as deep on session-by-session betting or player performance markets.

Withdrawal Speed: Both Reliable, Hollywoodbets Slightly Faster

Hollywoodbets typically processes EFT withdrawals within 24 hours, and often same-day if you request during business hours. The process is straightforward — request via the app or website, and the money hits your bank account without drama. They don't charge withdrawal fees, which is standard but appreciated.

WSB processes EFT withdrawals in 24–48 hours. It's reliable — we've never had a failed or delayed withdrawal in our testing — but it's not quite as quick as Hollywoodbets. WSB also supports withdrawals via bank transfer and certain e-wallets.

Neither of these is in Betway's "instant payout" league (read our fastest paying bookmakers guide), but both are dependable. You'll get your money without hassle.

Winner: Hollywoodbets — slightly faster, plus the branch option for cash withdrawals.

Welcome Bonuses: Modest on Both Sides

Neither WSB nor Hollywoodbets goes big on welcome bonuses — and honestly, that's fine.

WSB offers up to R50 as a free bet when you sign up. It's small, but there's virtually no catch. It gets you started with something to bet on while you explore the platform.

Hollywoodbets' famous R50 sign-up bonus requires no deposit at all. Register, verify your account, and the R50 appears in your free bet balance. It's the easiest entry point in SA betting — you can literally start betting without spending a cent. There are wagering requirements on the free bet winnings, but they're transparent and reasonable.

Neither bookmaker tries to lure you in with R5,000 bonuses that come with impossible wagering requirements. Both keep it honest and low-key. For the bigger bonuses available elsewhere, check our best betting bonuses guide.

Winner: Hollywoodbets — no deposit required is hard to beat.

Mobile App: Hollywoodbets Has the Edge

Hollywoodbets' mobile app is one of the best in South Africa. Available on both Android and iOS (a rarity — most SA bookmaker apps are Android-only), it's fast, well-designed, and covers every feature available on desktop. Live betting works smoothly, bet slips are easy to manage, and the app handles race day without crashing or lagging. They also support USSD betting for punters on feature phones — dial *120*269# and you can place bets without data or a smartphone.

WSB's mobile experience is functional but less polished. They offer an Android app and a USSD channel (*120*971#), plus a mobile-optimised website. The app does what it needs to do — bet placement, account management, deposits, withdrawals — but the interface feels a generation behind Hollywoodbets'. Navigation is sometimes unintuitive, and the live betting section doesn't update as smoothly.

WSB's USSD betting is actually quite good for what it is. If you bet mainly on football and horse racing and you're on a basic phone, WSB's USSD system is quick and easy to use.

Winner: Hollywoodbets — better app, iOS availability, and strong USSD as backup.

Live Betting: Hollywoodbets' Streaming Advantage

Hollywoodbets offers live betting with a solid range of in-play markets on football, rugby, cricket, and tennis. What sets them apart is live streaming — you can watch selected events directly in the app while betting. This is a major advantage during PSL matches or horse racing events. See the action, react in real time, place your bet. No need for a separate streaming subscription.

WSB's live betting covers the main markets competently. You'll find in-play odds on match results, over/under, and other popular markets. But the range of micro-markets is smaller than Hollywoodbets', and there's no live streaming. For punters who treat live betting as a significant part of their approach, Hollywoodbets is the better platform.

Lucky Numbers: A Hollywoodbets Phenomenon

Lucky Numbers — betting on international lottery outcomes — is one of Hollywoodbets' most popular products. You bet on which numbers will be drawn in lotteries around the world (UK 49s, SA Lotto, Irish Lotto, and many others). It's essentially a numbers game with massive payouts on long-shot picks. Hugely popular with casual SA bettors.

WSB also offers Lucky Numbers, but Hollywoodbets has made it their own. The user experience around Lucky Numbers on Hollywoodbets is polished, with quick picks, favourite numbers, and straightforward bet placement. For many Hollywoodbets users, Lucky Numbers is the primary reason they have an account.

If Lucky Numbers is your thing, Hollywoodbets is where you want to be. If you're strictly about sports betting, this category doesn't matter much.

Physical Branches: Hollywoodbets Wins by Default

Hollywoodbets has a network of physical branches across South Africa — particularly strong in KwaZulu-Natal but expanding nationally. This matters for punters who prefer to deposit cash, collect winnings in person, or just want a physical space to watch sport and bet. It's a differentiator that purely online bookmakers can't match.

WSB doesn't have a comparable branch network. They're primarily an online/telephone bookmaker. This isn't a disadvantage for digital-first punters, but for those in areas where internet banking isn't the default, Hollywoodbets' physical presence is a genuine advantage.

The Verdict: WSB vs Hollywoodbets South Africa

Hollywoodbets is the more complete bookmaker. Better app, live streaming, the best horse racing platform in SA, Lucky Numbers, physical branches, and a slight edge on withdrawal speed. They've grown from a regional horse racing bookie into one of South Africa's most powerful betting brands — and the product quality justifies the hype.

But WSB isn't trying to be Hollywoodbets. They're a solid, reliable bookmaker with competitive odds, a loyal following in the Western Cape, and a no-nonsense approach that many punters appreciate. Their R1 minimum deposit is the lowest in the industry, and their odds on lower-profile football matches are sometimes the best available.

Smart punters use both. Hollywoodbets as a primary account for its depth of features, horse racing, and live betting. WSB as a secondary account for odds shopping — checking whether they're paying more on a specific football market before placing your bet. Having both costs nothing and could make you money.

FAQ

Is World Sports Betting or Hollywoodbets better for horse racing?

Hollywoodbets is the clear winner for horse racing. They started as a horse racing bookmaker and have the deepest coverage in South Africa — all major and minor tracks, extensive exotic bet types, live streaming of races, and competitive fixed-odds pricing. WSB covers horse racing too, but with less depth.

Which bookmaker has better PSL odds — WSB or Hollywoodbets?

It varies match by match, but Hollywoodbets generally offers slightly more competitive PSL odds. WSB's football coverage is solid and they often match Hollywoodbets on main markets. The best approach is to compare odds before each bet using a tool like our odds comparison page.

Does World Sports Betting pay out faster than Hollywoodbets?

Both are reasonably fast. Hollywoodbets processes EFT withdrawals within 24 hours, sometimes same-day. WSB is similar — typically 24–48 hours for EFT payouts. Neither is as fast as Betway's instant withdrawals, but both are reliable.

Can I use both WSB and Hollywoodbets at the same time?

Yes. There's no restriction on holding accounts with multiple bookmakers. Many experienced punters use both to compare odds and take advantage of different promotions. It's one of the simplest ways to improve your long-term returns.

Are World Sports Betting and Hollywoodbets both licensed in South Africa?

Yes. Both hold valid licences from South African provincial gambling boards. World Sports Betting is licensed by the Western Cape Gambling and Racing Board. Hollywoodbets is licensed by the KwaZulu-Natal Gaming and Betting Board. Both are fully legal, regulated, and require FICA verification.
