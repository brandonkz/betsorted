YesPlay Review South Africa (2026): Sports, Casino & Lucky Numbers

Published: 29 March 2026 · Updated: 29 March 2026 · 9 min read

YesPlay has been quietly building a loyal following in South Africa since launching under the Mpumalanga Economic Regulator licence. It's one of those bookmakers that doesn't get the same airtime as Betway or Hollywoodbets, but when you look at what they actually offer, there's more substance than the marketing budget suggests.

We've spent time with the platform across sports betting, Lucky Numbers, and their casino section. Here's the honest assessment.

Overview: What Is YesPlay?

YesPlay is a licensed South African online bookmaker offering three main verticals:

  1. Sports betting — football, rugby, cricket, tennis, and more
  2. Lucky Numbers — fixed-odds betting on international lotteries
  3. Casino games — slots, table games, and Aviator-style crash games

They accept ZAR exclusively, support all major SA deposit methods, and target the mid-market punter who wants a bit of everything in one place.

Sports Betting

Coverage

YesPlay covers the sports that SA punters actually care about:

  • Football: PSL, EPL, Champions League, La Liga, Serie A, Bundesliga, Ligue 1
  • Rugby: URC, international tests, Rugby Championship
  • Cricket: CSA domestic, international tests/ODIs/T20s, IPL
  • Tennis: Grand Slams, ATP/WTA tour
  • Other: Basketball, boxing, MMA, esports

For a mid-tier bookmaker, the coverage is solid. You won't find obscure Belarusian league 2 football, but everything mainstream is there.

Live Betting

YesPlay's live betting is decent for PSL and EPL matches — you get match result, next goal, corners, and cards in-play. It's not as fast as Betway's live platform, and the market depth drops off for rugby and cricket. But it works.

Cash-out is available on selected pre-match and live bets. Partial cash-out is not supported yet — an area where Betway has the edge. See our cash-out guide for a full comparison.

Lucky Numbers

YesPlay's Lucky Numbers section covers the standard international draws — UK 49s, Ireland, Russia, France, and more. If you're coming from Hollywoodbets, the experience is familiar:

  • Pick 1-6 numbers per draw
  • Fixed-odds payouts based on how many you get right
  • Draws run throughout the day
  • R1 minimum bet

The odds are competitive with Hollywoodbets and slightly behind LottoStar on some markets. For the average Lucky Numbers player, you won't notice much difference.

Casino Games

YesPlay's casino section has grown significantly in 2026. Key offerings:

  • Slots: 200+ titles including popular SA favourites and international games
  • Aviator / Crash games: The multiplier game that's taken SA by storm. YesPlay's version runs smoothly
  • Table games: Blackjack, roulette, baccarat — standard digital versions
  • Spina Zonke: YesPlay has their own Spina Zonke-style section, though Hollywoodbets still dominates this space

If casino games are your primary interest and you want the widest selection, Hollywoodbets or dedicated casino platforms are better. But YesPlay's offering is good enough as a secondary feature alongside sports betting.

Deposits and Withdrawals

Deposit Methods

  • Instant EFT: Ozow, iPay (all major banks)
  • Capitec Pay: Direct from the Capitec app
  • Vouchers: 1ForYou, OTT, Blu Voucher, Flash
  • Card: Visa, Mastercard
  • Bank transfer: Manual EFT (slower but available)

Minimum deposit is R10. The voucher coverage is comprehensive — important for the large chunk of SA punters who prefer prepaid deposits. See our payment methods comparison for more detail.

Withdrawals

This is where YesPlay could improve. While verified accounts typically see withdrawals processed within 24 hours, actual bank clearing takes 1-3 business days depending on your bank. Capitec users tend to receive funds fastest.

The withdrawal minimum is R50, which is standard. Maximum daily withdrawal varies by account level — new accounts are typically capped lower than established ones.

Mobile Experience

YesPlay offers both a mobile-optimised website and a downloadable APK (Android). There's no iOS app on the App Store (common for SA bookmakers).

The mobile site is clean and loads quickly. Navigation is better than some competitors — the bottom tab bar makes switching between sports, Lucky Numbers, and casino straightforward. Live betting on mobile works without lag issues.

One notable gap: no data-free option. If data costs matter to you, Hollywoodbets and Betway offer data-free access that YesPlay doesn't match.

Bonuses & Promotions

YesPlay runs a rotating calendar of promotions:

  • Welcome bonus: Typically 100% match on first deposit up to R500
  • Free bet rewards: Earn free bets based on weekly betting activity
  • Accumulator boosts: 5-50% boost on accumulators with 4+ legs
  • Lucky Numbers specials: Enhanced payouts on featured draws

The wagering requirements (usually 4-6x) are among the fairest in SA. YesPlay doesn't bury the terms in legal jargon like some competitors. That said, the bonus amounts are modest compared to Betway's promotional spend.

BetSorted Verdict

YesPlay is a solid mid-tier option that punches above its weight in several areas. It's not going to dethrone Betway for serious sports bettors or Hollywoodbets for Lucky Numbers die-hards, but it's a genuinely competent all-rounder.

Where YesPlay shines is for the casual-to-intermediate punter who wants sports, Lucky Numbers, and casino all in one clean interface without needing the absolute best odds on every market.
