YesPlay vs Hollywoodbets: Which SA Bookmaker is Better in 2026?

YesPlay has quietly carved out its own lane in South African betting. It's not the biggest name, not the flashiest brand, but it does the basics well and covers sports, Lucky Numbers, and casino all in one clean package. Hollywoodbets, on the other hand, is the KwaZulu-Natal powerhouse that's expanded into a national institution — branches everywhere, horse racing royalty, and a brand that's become synonymous with betting in South Africa.

Odds Quality: Who Pays More on the Same Bet?

Let's cut straight to what matters most — how much you actually get paid when you win.

For PSL football, both bookmakers offer competitive odds. On a standard match like Kaizer Chiefs vs AmaZulu, you'll typically find the prices within a few cents of each other. Neither consistently beats the other on domestic football, which makes sense — PSL is bread and butter for every SA bookmaker.

Where they differ is in market depth. Hollywoodbets offers more bet types on PSL matches — player props, correct score combinations, and same game multis that YesPlay doesn't always match. If you like building exotic bets on local football, Hollywoodbets gives you more to work with.

For international football — EPL, Champions League, La Liga — Hollywoodbets has a slight edge on odds consistency. YesPlay's international prices are fair but not as sharp. The difference isn't massive, maybe 1–3% on average, but it compounds if you're betting regularly.

Rugby odds are similar across both platforms. Neither stands out as significantly better for Springbok or URC betting.

Bottom line: Hollywoodbets has slightly better odds on international football and more market depth overall. YesPlay is competitive on PSL and local sport but doesn't go as deep on market variety.

Lucky Numbers: YesPlay's Strong Suit

If you're a Lucky Numbers player, this is where YesPlay makes its case.

YesPlay covers a wide range of international lottery draws for Lucky Numbers betting — UK 49s, Irish Daily Millions, French Lotto, Spanish draws, and more. The draw selection is extensive, and they run draws throughout the day, giving you plenty of opportunities from morning to evening.

Hollywoodbets also offers Lucky Numbers, and they've got a solid selection, but YesPlay's coverage of smaller international draws is broader. If you've got a system or favourite numbers you like to play across multiple draws, YesPlay gives you more options in a single session.

Both offer standard Lucky Numbers bet types — single number, pair, triple — and the payouts are roughly comparable. YesPlay occasionally edges it on payout rates for specific draws, but the differences are marginal.

Winner: YesPlay, particularly if you play Lucky Numbers across multiple draws daily.

Horse Racing: No Contest

If you bet on horses, stop reading here and go to Hollywoodbets. We're not being dramatic — it's genuinely that clear.

Hollywoodbets was built on horse racing. They offer fixed odds, tote betting, exotic bets (Swinger, Place Accumulator, Jackpot, Pick 6), and coverage of every South African racecourse — Turffontein, Greyville, Kenilworth, Scottsville, Fairview, you name it. They also stream live racing in-app, with form guides, jockey stats, and trainer info baked into the interface.

For the Durban July, the Met, the Gold Cup, or any Saturday card — Hollywoodbets is where serious horse racing punters go. The market depth is unmatched by any SA bookmaker, let alone YesPlay.

YesPlay's horse racing offering is limited. They cover the basics, but the market depth and racing-specific features just aren't there. If racing is even a secondary interest, Hollywoodbets wins this category by a country mile.

Winner: Hollywoodbets, and it's not even close. Check out our each-way calculator for racing returns.

Deposits and Withdrawals

Both bookmakers accept the standard SA payment methods — EFT, Ozow, 1Voucher, bank transfers, and airtime deposits. Minimum deposit is R10 on both platforms, which is standard across the industry.

Withdrawal speed is where the comparison gets interesting. YesPlay typically processes withdrawals within 24–48 hours, while Hollywoodbets takes 24–72 hours. In practice, both are within the same ballpark, though YesPlay edges it slightly on average processing time.

Hollywoodbets has one advantage YesPlay can't match: physical branches. If you live near a Hollywoodbets branch (especially in KZN, Gauteng, or the Eastern Cape), you can deposit or withdraw cash in person. No waiting for bank transfers, no payment gateway issues. Walk in, hand over your slip, walk out with cash.

For digital-only punters, both are fine. For anyone who values the option of cash transactions, Hollywoodbets wins by default.

Winner: Hollywoodbets for flexibility (branches), slight edge to YesPlay for online speed.

Mobile Experience

Hollywoodbets has a dedicated Android app plus USSD betting. The app is functional, covers all features, and works on most Android devices. USSD means you can place bets even without data or a smartphone — dial a code, navigate the menu, confirm your bet. In a country where data costs are a real concern, this is a genuine advantage.

YesPlay doesn't have a dedicated app, but their mobile website is clean, fast, and responsive. It works well in any browser, loads quickly on mobile data, and the betting slip is easy to use. For most punters, the mobile site is perfectly adequate.

That said, a dedicated app with push notifications and biometric login offers a slightly smoother experience than a mobile site. And USSD is a game-changer for accessibility in rural areas or when data runs out mid-month.

Winner: Hollywoodbets — the app plus USSD combination gives them the edge.

Welcome Bonuses and Promotions

YesPlay offers up to R50 in free bets for new sign-ups, with reasonable wagering requirements. It's modest compared to Betway's R1,000 offer, but the terms are transparent and the turnover conditions are achievable.

Hollywoodbets gives R25 free — no deposit required. Register, get R25, start betting. It's smaller in value but completely risk-free, making it ideal for first-time bettors who want to test the platform.

Both run ongoing promotions — enhanced odds on big matches, free bet offers on specific events, and seasonal specials. Neither has a dramatically better promotions calendar than the other.

Winner: YesPlay on raw bonus value (R50 vs R25), but Hollywoodbets' no-deposit bonus is lower friction for new users.

FAQ

Is YesPlay licensed in South Africa?

Yes. YesPlay is licensed by the Western Cape Gambling and Racing Board. It operates legally in South Africa and is required to comply with FICA verification and responsible gambling regulations.

Does Hollywoodbets have better odds than YesPlay?

On international football, Hollywoodbets tends to have slightly better odds. On PSL and local sport, they're very close. For Lucky Numbers, YesPlay is competitive. Always compare prices before placing large bets.

Can I use both YesPlay and Hollywoodbets?

Yes. There's no restriction on having accounts with multiple licensed bookmakers. Many experienced punters do exactly this to shop for the best odds and take advantage of different promotions.

Which bookmaker is better for PSL betting?

Hollywoodbets has more market depth on PSL matches (player props, correct score combos), but YesPlay covers all the standard markets at competitive odds. For basic PSL betting, both are fine. For exotic bets, Hollywoodbets has the edge.
