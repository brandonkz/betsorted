Sunbet's 100% deposit match up to R500 looks bigger on paper. You deposit R500, you get R500 in bonus funds. But here's the catch South African bettors consistently miss: the wagering requirements attached to that bonus are meaningful. You typically need to turn over the bonus amount a set number of times before it converts to withdrawable cash. For casual bettors placing R50–R100 per match, those requirements add up slowly.

YesPlay's R50 free bet offer is simpler. You bet R50, you get R50 back as a free bet regardless of the outcome. The free bet has its own terms — it won't convert directly to cash, but you can use it on any pre-match market. For a first-timer who isn't planning to churn volume, the YesPlay approach is less tricky.
