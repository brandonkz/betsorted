Sport Coverage — What Can You Bet On?

Yesplay: The Quiet Achiever

Yesplay covers the essentials well — PSL, Premier League, Champions League, URC rugby, Springboks, cricket (Proteas, IPL, SA20), and horse racing. Their international football coverage is surprisingly deep for a mid-tier SA bookmaker, with markets on leagues as far-flung as the Japanese J-League and Argentine Primera División.

Where Yesplay falls short is market depth within each sport. You'll get match result, over/under, and both teams to score for most football matches, but don't expect player props, corner markets, or booking points beyond the top matches.

Supabets: The Street Fighter

Supabets offers wider sport coverage overall, with 25+ sports including boxing, MMA, darts, and virtual sports. Their PSL market depth is excellent — you'll find correct score, halftime/fulltime, first goalscorer, and combo markets for every fixture, not just the big derbies.

Supabets also runs a solid virtual sports section. If you're the type who needs something to bet on at 2 AM on a Tuesday (no judgment, bru), their virtual football and horse racing keep the action going around the clock.

🏉 Rugby punters: For the Nations Championship, both bookmakers will offer Springboks markets. Supabets typically provides more try scorer and handicap options, while Yesplay sticks to the basics (match result, handicap, totals).
