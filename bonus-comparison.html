How We Calculate Real Bonus Value

The "real value" is what you can reasonably expect to withdraw after completing wagering requirements. We calculate it using the bookmaker's wagering multiplier, minimum odds, and the mathematical house edge at those odds.

A 100% matched deposit of R1,000 with 5× wagering at 1.80 minimum odds means you need to bet R5,000 on qualifying markets. At those odds, the house edge is roughly 44% per bet. After 5× turnover at those odds, you'd expect to keep approximately 35–45% of the original bonus.
