How to request a withdrawal

  1. Sign in at betxchange.com/sign-in.
  2. Navigate to the withdrawal or cashier section of your account.
  3. Select your withdrawal method.
  4. Enter the amount (minimum R100).

  6. Confirm the withdrawal request.

Withdrawal problems

  • Withdrawal pending: FICA verification can cause delays on first withdrawals. Make sure your document has been submitted and accepted.
  • Withdrawal rejected: Check that your bank account details are correct and that the withdrawal amount meets the R100 minimum.
  • Below minimum: betXchange's minimum withdrawal is R100. If your balance is less than R100, you will need to continue playing before withdrawing.
