What Does Zero-Rated Mean?

Zero-rating means the mobile network doesn't count that app or website's traffic against your data bundle. You can use the app even with R0 airtime or a depleted data bundle. The network absorbs the data cost as part of their partnership with the bookmaker.

Zero-rating agreements are commercial deals between networks and bookmakers — they can change or end. Always verify current status with your network by dialling their customer service number or checking their website.
