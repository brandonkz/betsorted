How Each-Way Betting Works

An each-way bet is actually TWO bets:

1. Win bet: Your horse must win the race

2. Place bet: Your horse must finish in the places (usually top 2, 3, or 4)

Example

Bet: R50 each-way on a horse at 8.00 odds

Total stake: R100 (R50 win + R50 place)

Place terms: 1/4 odds, 3 places

If the horse WINS:

• Win bet: R50 × 8.00 = R400

• Place bet: R50 × 2.75 = R137.50 (8.00 ÷ 4 + 1)

• Total return: R537.50

• Profit: R437.50

If the horse PLACES (2nd or 3rd):

• Win bet: R0 (lost)

• Place bet: R50 × 2.75 = R137.50

• Total return: R137.50

• Profit: R37.50 (R137.50 - R100 stake)

Place Terms Guide

1/4 odds (most common): Divide win odds by 4

Example: 8.00 odds → (8.00 - 1) ÷ 4 + 1 = 2.75 place odds

1/5 odds: Divide win odds by 5 (better for bookmaker)

1/6 odds: Divide win odds by 6 (rare, major races)

Number of Places

2 places: Races with 5-7 runners

3 places: Races with 8-15 runners (most common)

4 places: Races with 16+ runners (big handicaps)

Famous SA Horse Racing Events

Durban July: South Africa's premier horse race (July)

When to Use Each-Way Betting

✓ Big fields (10+ runners) - better place chance

✓ Long-shot outsiders (8.00+ odds) - insurance if horse places

✓ Uncertain about winner but confident in top 3

✗ Heavy favorites (under 3.00) - place returns too small

✗ Small fields (under 8 runners) - better to bet win only
