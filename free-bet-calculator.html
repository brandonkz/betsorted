What is Wagering Requirement?

A wagering requirement (also called rollover) means you must bet a certain multiple of your bonus before you can withdraw it. A 5× wagering requirement on a R1,000 bonus means you need to place R5,000 worth of qualifying bets first.

The real value of a bonus is what you actually expect to keep after completing wagering. At minimum odds of 1.80, the average bettor keeps roughly 20–30% of a matched bonus as real cash.

Free Bet vs Matched Deposit

Free bet: The bookmaker gives you a bet token. If you win, you keep only the profit (stake not returned). A R100 free bet at 2.00 odds wins you R100, not R200.

Matched deposit: The bookmaker adds bonus cash equal to (some % of) your deposit. This cash usually has wagering requirements before withdrawal.
