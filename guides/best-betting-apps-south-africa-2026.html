How to Choose a Betting App in South Africa

  • Compare odds before every bet. The average gap between the best and worst price on a single match across SA bookmakers is 8.5%. Over a year of regular betting, that gap costs the average punter R180/month. Use a free odds comparison tool to find the best price.
  • Check local league coverage. If you bet on PSL, Nedbank Cup, or Currie Cup, make sure your bookmaker offers deep markets (goalscorer, both teams to score, corners) for those competitions. Hollywoodbets and Betway lead here.
  • Consider your payout method. If you need fast withdrawals, Betway offers instant payouts via EFT for verified accounts. Ozow and FNB eWallet are the fastest alternatives. All SA bookmakers require FICA verification before your first withdrawal.
  • Think about data costs. If mobile data is a concern, SupaBets offers USSD betting that uses zero data. Hollywoodbets has a lightweight mobile site. Apps from Betway and 10bet use more data but offer richer features.
  • Set a budget and stick to it. Use the betting calculator to understand potential returns before placing bets. Never bet more than you can afford to lose. The National Responsible Gambling Programme helpline is 0800 006 008.

Is online betting legal in South Africa? ▼
Yes. Online betting is legal in South Africa under the National Gambling Act of 2004. Bookmakers must hold a licence from one of the provincial gambling boards. All bookmakers listed in this guide hold valid South African licences. The legal gambling age is 18.
Which bookmaker is best for PSL betting? ▼
Hollywoodbets and Betway offer the widest PSL markets, including match result, both teams to score, correct score, and goalscorer markets. Hollywoodbets is the official PSL sponsor and typically offers the most PSL-specific promotions.

How do I withdraw from betting sites in South Africa? ▼
All licensed SA bookmakers offer bank EFT withdrawals. Betway processes withdrawals instantly for verified accounts. Hollywoodbets typically takes 24-72 hours. You will need to complete FICA verification (ID document + proof of address) before your first withdrawal.
Which betting app works without data in South Africa? ▼
SupaBets offers fully data-free USSD betting (*120*7297#). You can place bets, check results, and withdraw without using any mobile data. Hollywoodbets also has a low-data mobile site.
Are betting winnings taxed in South Africa? ▼
No. In South Africa, gambling winnings are not subject to income tax for individual bettors. SARS classifies gambling as a recreational activity, not a trade. However, if gambling is your primary source of income, it may be classified as taxable.
