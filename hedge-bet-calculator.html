Hedge Bet Calculator South Africa

Use this hedge bet calculator to work out how much to stake on the opposite side of a bet. It is built for SA punters using decimal odds and rand stakes.

Bankroll note: a hedge can protect a profit, but it can also turn a great bet into an average one. Use it when reducing risk matters more than the maximum payout.

How the hedge formula works

The balanced hedge stake is: original stake times original odds divided by hedge odds. That gives you roughly the same gross return whichever side wins. Profit still depends on the combined amount you staked across both bets.

When South African bettors use hedging

  • Covering the final leg of a big accumulator before kick-off.
  • Reducing risk after early legs have already won.
  • Locking in some value when live odds move heavily in your favour.
  • Protecting a bankroll before a volatile derby, cup final or rugby handicap.
