A Lucky bet costs more than a normal acca because it includes singles, doubles, trebles and bigger accumulators. The upside is that one losing leg does not automatically kill the whole slip.

When to use it

Lucky 15s make most sense when you like four selections but do not fully trust all four to win. For bigger cards, Lucky 31 and Lucky 63 can protect you from one or two bad legs, but the total stake rises quickly. Compare the result with the accumulator calculator before placing the bet.
