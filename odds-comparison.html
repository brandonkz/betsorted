🔮 What is Polymarket?

Polymarket is a crypto prediction market where traders bet real USDC on outcomes. Unlike traditional bookmakers, Polymarket odds reflect pure market consensus - what thousands of traders actually believe will happen.

How to use Polymarket probabilities:

• Polymarket shows probability (e.g., Arsenal 19.5% chance to win Champions League)

• To convert to decimal odds: 1 ÷ probability

• Example: 19.5% probability = 1 ÷ 0.195 = 5.13 decimal odds

• Then compare this to traditional bookmaker odds (Betway, Hollywoodbets, etc.)

💡 Why compare? If Polymarket says 19.5% (5.13 odds) and bookies offer 6.00 odds, the bookies are giving better value than the market consensus.
