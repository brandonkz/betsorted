Sports Betting in Cape Town: A Local Guide for 2026/27

Published 12 November 2026 · Sources checked 12 November 2026

Cape Town is where a large part of South Africa's betting industry actually lives. Of the 70 Western Cape bookmaker licences on the National Gambling Board register, 58 have an address inside the City of Cape Town, from V&A Waterfront office blocks to Century City, Claremont and the CBD. Those licences are issued by the Western Cape Gambling and Racing Board. The city also has its own sporting calendar to bet on: the Stormers and the Sevens at Green Point, the SA20 at Newlands, a first-division title race, and summer racing at Kenilworth.

This page covers what's coming up in the city and how to bet on it sensibly. Fixtures and venues were checked against team, league and racing sources on 12 November 2026. We don't quote odds here, because they move; use the odds comparison on the day.

Who licenses betting in Cape Town

Bookmakers, casinos, limited payout machines and the horse-racing tote in Cape Town are all licensed by the Western Cape Gambling and Racing Board (WCGRB). Its licence holders page listed 68 bookmaker companies on 12 November 2026, alongside one casino in the metro, GrandWest in Goodwood. Our Western Cape Gambling Board guide explains how to check a licence and where to send a complaint.

Sevens: HSBC SVNS Cape Town, 5–6 December 2026

The Cape Town Sevens returns to DHL Stadium on Saturday 5 and Sunday 6 December 2026. The Blitzboks are chasing a third straight Cape Town title, and SA Rugby has cut ticket prices by 17% to 28% this year. Sevens is a different betting proposition from fifteens: games are two seven-minute halves, a two-minute yellow card can swing a match, and tournament outright markets reward a side that peaks on Sunday rather than one that tops its pool. Look at match handicaps for pool games and keep outright stakes small.

Football: Cape Town City and Stellenbosch FC

With Cape Town City in the second tier this season, the city's football story is a promotion race. Cape Town City FC, who list DHL Cape Town Stadium as their ground, top the Motsepe Foundation Championship after 10 matches (21 points, six wins, three draws, one loss). They are level on points with Magesi and ahead on goal difference. Their next games are away to Lerumo Lions on 21 November and at home to Venda FC on 28 November. Coverage of first-division matches varies between bookmakers, so check that yours lists the game before match day.

The nearest top-flight side is Stellenbosch FC, about 50 km away. Our Stellenbosch FC betting guide covers them, and the PSL fixtures page lists every Premiership game.

Horse racing: Kenilworth and Durbanville

Cape racing is run by Race Coast, whose Western Cape company is also the province's only licensed totalisator. That means tote bets on Cape races go into its pools. The two tracks are Kenilworth Racecourse on Rosmead Avenue and the country course at Durbanville.

The summer season peaks in January. In 2026 the Grade 1 L'Ormarins King's Plate (1,600 m) was run at Kenilworth on 10 January, and the Cape Town Met (2,000 m, R5 million, Grade 1) on 31 January. Justin Snaith trained the last three Met winners: Jet Dark (2023), Double Superlative (2024) and Eight on Eighteen (2025). The Cape Derby followed on 28 February. Expect the same pattern in 2027, but confirm dates on Race Coast's fixtures page once they are published.

Practical tips for Cape Town punters

  • Watch the European kick-offs. Stormers away games in the Champions Cup start late in the afternoon or evening SAST. Live-bet only if you will actually be watching.
  • Shop the derby prices. On Stormers v Bulls or Lions weekends, bookmakers price the game differently, especially on handicaps. Compare before you bet; our best betting sites guide is a good starting list.
  • Verify the licence, not the logo. Plenty of sites use "Cape Town" in their marketing. Check the licence number on the NGB register as shown in our WCGRB guide.
  • Mobile data: if you bet at the stadium, a data-free betting app can save airtime.

Frequently asked questions

Is sports betting legal in Cape Town?

Yes, with a licensed bookmaker. Bookmakers in the Western Cape are licensed by the Western Cape Gambling and Racing Board, and you can check any operator on the National Gambling Board's verified operators register.

Who regulates betting in Cape Town?

The Western Cape Gambling and Racing Board (WCGRB). It licenses bookmakers, casinos such as GrandWest, limited payout machines and the Race Coast totalisator, and it takes complaints at Complaints.Compliance@wcgrb.co.za.

Where do the Stormers play their home games?

At DHL Stadium in Green Point, Cape Town. Their next home games are against the Lions on 19 December 2026 and the Bulls on 3 January 2027 in the URC, then Racing 92 on 16 January 2027 in the Champions Cup.

When is the Cape Town Sevens in 2026?

The HSBC SVNS Cape Town tournament is at DHL Stadium on Saturday 5 and Sunday 6 December 2026.

Where is horse racing held in Cape Town?

At Kenilworth Racecourse in Kenilworth and at Durbanville. Race Coast runs Cape racing, and its Western Cape company is the province's licensed totalisator.
