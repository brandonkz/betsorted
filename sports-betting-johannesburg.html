Sports Betting in Johannesburg: A Local Guide for 2026/27

Published 12 November 2026 · Sources checked 12 November 2026

Johannesburg is South Africa's biggest walk-in betting market. Cape Town is mainly where online bookmakers keep their offices. In Joburg, the National Gambling Board register lists more than 50 licensed bookmaker premises with addresses in the city, Soweto, Randburg, Sandton and Alexandra: shops in taxi-rank malls, on Commissioner and Bree Streets, and at the racecourse. Add the Soweto derby, the Lions at Ellis Park, the Wanderers and the Summer Cup at Turffontein, and the city has something to bet on almost every weekend.

Fixtures, venues and licence data below were checked on 12 November 2026. We don't quote odds, because they move; compare prices on the day with our odds comparison.

Who licenses betting in Johannesburg

Gambling in Johannesburg is regulated by the Gauteng Gambling Board (GGB), a statutory body set up under the Gauteng Gambling Act (Act 4 of 1995). The board notes some history: before August 1996, betting on horse racing and other sport was the only legal form of gambling in Gauteng. Its office is at 125 Corlett Drive, Johannesburg, and the switchboard is (011) 581 4800.

Many online brands you use in Joburg are licensed in another province, often the Western Cape. Complaints go to the board that issued the licence, which you can find on the NGB register. Our Western Cape Gambling Board guide walks through the check.

Football: Kaizer Chiefs, Orlando Pirates and the Soweto derby

Johannesburg is home to the country's two biggest clubs. Kaizer Chiefs play big home games at FNB Stadium in Nasrec, and Orlando Pirates play at Orlando Stadium in Soweto. The Soweto derby is the most heavily bet league game of the season. Sentiment pushes prices around, so the draw and handicap markets often deserve a second look. Frequently asked questions

Who regulates betting in Johannesburg?

The Gauteng Gambling Board, a statutory body under the Gauteng Gambling Act of 1995. Online bookmakers may be licensed by another province's board, which you can check on the National Gambling Board's verified operators register.

Where can I place a bet in person in Johannesburg?

The National Gambling Board register lists licensed bookmaker premises across the city, including Hollywoodbets at Newtown Mall, World Sports Betting at Maponya Mall in Soweto, Betting World at Bree Street Mall and Gandhi Square, and 4Racing at Turffontein Racecourse.

When is the Summer Cup in 2026?

The Grade 1 Summer Cup is at Turffontein Racecourse in Johannesburg on Saturday 28 November 2026.

Where do the Lions play and when is their next home game?

The Lions play at Ellis Park. Their next home games are against the Bulls on 5 December 2026 in the URC and Toulouse on 12 December 2026 in the Champions Cup.

Is the Wanderers hosting SA20 playoff games?

Yes. In season five the Wanderers hosts the Eliminator on 17 February 2027 and Qualifier 2 on 19 February 2027; the final is at Newlands on 21 February 2027.
