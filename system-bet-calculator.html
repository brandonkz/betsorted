What is a System Bet?

A system bet combines multiple selections into various combinations of singles, doubles, trebles, and accumulators. Unlike a standard accumulator (where all picks must win), system bets give you coverage when one or more selections lose. You still get a return from the winning combinations.

System bets are popular for racing, football accumulators, and multi-sport bets where you want insurance against one bad pick ruining your whole ticket.

Types of System Bets

Trixie (3 selections, 4 bets)

Bets: 3 doubles + 1 treble = 4 bets

Minimum winners needed: 2

Example: R10 unit stake = R40 total cost. If 2 selections win, you get a return from the double. If all 3 win, you get 3 doubles + 1 treble.

Patent (3 selections, 7 bets)

Bets: 3 singles + 3 doubles + 1 treble = 7 bets

Minimum winners needed: 1

Example: R10 unit stake = R70 total cost. If only 1 selection wins, you get a return from the single. Full coverage.

Yankee (4 selections, 11 bets)

Bets: 6 doubles + 4 trebles + 1 four-fold = 11 bets

Minimum winners needed: 2

Example: R10 unit stake = R110 total cost. No singles, so at least 2 must win. Popular for racing and football.

Lucky 15 (4 selections, 15 bets)

Bets: 4 singles + 6 doubles + 4 trebles + 1 four-fold = 15 bets

Minimum winners needed: 1

Example: R10 unit stake = R150 total cost. If only 1 wins, you get a return from the single. Full coverage + bonus potential from bookmakers if all 4 win.

Lucky 31 (5 selections, 31 bets)

Bets: 5 singles + 10 doubles + 10 trebles + 5 four-folds + 1 five-fold = 31 bets

Minimum winners needed: 1

Example: R10 unit stake = R310 total cost. Maximum coverage for 5 selections.

Lucky 63 (6 selections, 63 bets)

Bets: 6 singles + 15 doubles + 20 trebles + 15 four-folds + 6 five-folds + 1 six-fold = 63 bets

Minimum winners needed: 1

Example: R10 unit stake = R630 total cost. Full coverage for 6 selections. Popular for big race days (Durban July).

System Betting Tips for South Africa

1. Understand the cost

System bets can get expensive. A Lucky 63 with R10 unit stake = R630 total. Make sure you're comfortable with the total cost before placing the bet.

2. Use Lucky bets for racing

Lucky 15s and Lucky 31s are popular for horse racing (Durban July, Summer Cup) because you get a return even if only one horse wins. Some bookmakers also offer bonuses for all winners.

3. Yankees and Trixies for football

Yankees (4 selections) and Trixies (3 selections) are common for PSL and Champions League multi-bets. No singles, so you need at least 2 wins — but lower cost than Lucky bets.

4. Check for system bet bonuses

Betway and Hollywoodbets often offer bonuses on Lucky 15s, Lucky 31s, and Lucky 63s if all selections win (e.g., 10% bonus on total return). Always check promotions before placing.

5. Don't overdo the legs

A Lucky 63 (6 selections, 63 bets) sounds great, but if your unit stake is R20, you're spending R1,260. Stick to 3-4 selections unless you're confident in all picks and have the bankroll.

System Bet Calculator FAQ

What is the difference between a Lucky 15 and an accumulator?

An accumulator is one bet where all selections must win. A Lucky 15 is 15 separate bets (singles, doubles, trebles, four-fold), so you can still win money if only 1, 2, or 3 selections win.

How much does a Lucky 15 cost?

A Lucky 15 is 15 bets. Total cost = unit stake × 15. If your unit stake is R10, the total cost is R150.

What is better: Lucky 15 or Yankee?

Lucky 15: 15 bets (includes 4 singles). You get a return even if only 1 selection wins.
Yankee: 11 bets (no singles). At least 2 selections must win, but it costs less.

Choose a Lucky 15 if you want full coverage. Choose a Yankee if you're confident at least 2 will win and want to save money on singles.

Can I do an each-way Lucky 15?

Yes. An each-way Lucky 15 doubles the cost (30 bets total: 15 win bets + 15 place bets). Most bookmakers offer each-way Lucky 15s for horse racing.

Which bookmakers offer system bets in South Africa?

Betway, Hollywoodbets, and Sportingbet all offer system bets (Lucky 15, Yankee, Trixie, etc.). Check the betslip for the "system" or "multiple bets" option.

Do bookmakers offer bonuses on Lucky bets?

Yes. Betway and Hollywoodbets often give bonuses (e.g., 10% extra) if all selections in a Lucky 15, Lucky 31, or Lucky 63 win. Check the promotions page before placing your bet.
