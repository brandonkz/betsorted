Tips for Faster Withdrawals

Complete FICA first. Unverified accounts can't withdraw. Submit your ID and proof of address before you need to withdraw — don't wait until you've won.

Instant EFT vs Standard EFT. Standard EFT (manual bank transfer) takes 1–3 business days. Instant EFT (via Ozow, Peach Payments) processes in minutes. Where available, always choose Instant EFT for withdrawals.

Withdraw during business hours. Manual review queues at bookmakers operate during business hours. Requests submitted after 5 PM Friday may only be processed on Monday.

Capitec users: Capitec's real-time clearing means funds arrive faster even for standard EFT transfers compared to other SA banks.
